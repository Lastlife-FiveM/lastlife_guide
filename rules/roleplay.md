# Roleplay

You don't have to be an actor. You do have to play a person who wants to live.

## Stay in character

* **You're in character whenever you're in the world.** Use **OOC** chat for anything else, and keep it short.
* **Don't break a scene** to argue about rules. Play it out, then report it.
* **Be able to communicate.** Use a working microphone, or type.

## Value your life

* **Act like you can die.** Outgunned, surrounded or held up? Give in, stall or talk. Don't reach for your gun with three pointed at you.
* **Fear the dead too.** Don't use zombies as a joke, or throw your life away because you'll respawn.

## Metagaming

Don't use anything your character couldn't know.

* Streams, clips, Discord and private calls are not information your character has.
* **Voice programs outside the game** may not be used to pass on what's happening in a scene, or to get around losing your radio.
* A name over someone's head or in a list isn't an introduction.

## Powergaming

* **Don't force actions** on people that they can't react to ("/me knocks you out and takes everything").
* **Don't do the impossible**, like using a gun that was taken from you or talking on a radio while you're down.

## After you go down

* **While you're down** you can talk, but you can't give out enemy positions.
* **Revived?** You remember what happened, but don't jump straight back into the same fight.
* **Died and woke up?** You forget who killed you and how. That fight is over: no revenge.
* **Going back for your body is fine.** Going back to keep fighting isn't. If the people who killed you are still there, stay back until they leave.

## Leaving the game

* **No combat logging.** Don't disconnect, or switch character, to get out of a fight, a robbery, a raid or a horde. If you crash, come back as soon as you can.
* **Don't swap characters** to get an advantage, to pass items to yourself, or to act on what another of your characters knows.
