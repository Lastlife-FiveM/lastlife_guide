# PvP & Robberies

This page is about the **open world**. In safe zones you never fight. In red zones and the subway you always can.

## Before you shoot

* **There has to be a reason** your character would kill for: a robbery, a threat, a debt, a war, a trespasser who won't leave.
* **Talk first.** Make it clear by voice or text who you mean and what you want.
* **Give a real chance to answer.** Shouting one word while you shoot isn't roleplay.
* **Being armed isn't a reason.** Everybody is armed.

## When you can shoot straight away

* Someone is **shooting or attacking** you or your crewmate.
* Someone **draws or aims at you** after you've warned them.
* They **break a clear agreement** at gunpoint: running, reaching for a weapon, calling for help.
* They're **breaking into your base** or stealing your vehicle in front of you.
* You're **already in a fight** with them that hasn't ended.

## Robberies

* **Say what you want.** Then let people hand it over.
* **Don't kill people who cooperate.**
* **Don't rob the same person again** the same day.
* **Leave new players alone.** If someone clearly just started, with nothing but starting gear, let them go.
* **Don't force people** to empty their base, storage or stash, or to log into anything.

## Kidnapping and hostages

* Keep it **short and worth playing.** If a hostage is bored, you're doing it wrong.
* Don't take friends as fake hostages.

## Not allowed

* **Random killing** (RDM) and **running people over on purpose** (VDM).
* **Leading a horde** onto someone to kill them without roleplay.
* **Third-partying** a scene that has nothing to do with you, without roleplaying your way in.
* **Spawn killing** anyone who has just woken up at a bed or safe zone.
* **Revenge** after you've died. See [Roleplay](roleplay.md).

## Wars between crews

* Crews can go to war, with a reason both sides know about.
* War isn't Kill On Sight. Each fight still needs a moment both sides can see coming.
* Either side can end it by giving in. After that, leave each other alone.
