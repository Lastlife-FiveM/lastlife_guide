# PvP & Roleplay

PvP is switched on everywhere outside safe zones. That doesn't mean it's always allowed.

| Where | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 🛡️ **Safe zones** | No fighting and no griefing, with players or zombies. |
| 🌍 **Open world** | Only with roleplay. No random killing, no kill on sight. |
| 🔴 **Red zones** | Kill On Sight. No roleplay needed. |
| 🚇 **Extraction** | Kill On Sight. |

## Open world fights

* **There has to be a story.** Talk, threaten or make demands first, and give the other person a real chance to answer.
* **Self-defence is fine.** If someone attacks you or your crew, you can fight back.
* **Value your life.** When you're outgunned and held up, act like it.
* **Don't run people down** with vehicles, and don't lead a horde onto someone to kill them without roleplay.
* **Don't ambush safe zone exits** or use the few seconds of protection after leaving to start a fight.

## Staying in the story

* **No metagaming.** Don't use streams, Discord or map knowledge your survivor wouldn't have.
* **No powergaming.** Don't force actions on others that they can't react to.
* **No combat logging.** Don't disconnect to escape a fight, a robbery or zombies.
* **No revenge after death.** When you wake up, that fight is over. Getting your own body back is fine.
