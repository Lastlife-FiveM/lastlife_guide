# Bases & Building

## Where you can't build

Don't block, close off or build inside:

* **Roads, bridges, tunnels and rail lines.**
* **Safe zones** and the way in and out of them.
* **Traders, garages, benches and fuel pumps.**
* **Subway entrances and exits.**
* **Red zones**, nests and the power station at Palmer-Taylor.
* **Loot areas.** Don't wall off shops, gun stores, hospitals, police stations or whole streets for yourself.
* **Landing spots and docks** other people need.

## Building over roads

* **You can build over a road, but not on it.**
* Any walkway over a road must be at least **3 base walls tall**, measured from the ground, so vehicles can pass underneath.
* **No supports in the road.** Pillars and stairs go beside it.
* **Keep it lit or easy to see**, and keep stairs and doors off the road.

## Totems and claims

* **Claim what you use.** A totem is for a base, not for keeping others off an area.
* **Don't box people in.** You may not place a totem or pieces to trap, surround or lock out another crew.
* **Don't claim someone's base** by building around it.
* **Give neighbours room** to reach their own base by road.

## Base design

* **No glitched builds.** Don't build inside rocks, buildings, map objects or the ground.
* **Every room must be reachable** by breaking or opening something the game lets people break or open.
* **No endless walls.** Don't stack layers only to make a base unraidable or the server slow.
* **Traps must be part of the base.** Don't hide them on public ground.
* **Clean up.** Staff can remove abandoned pieces, sky bridges and litter in the world.
