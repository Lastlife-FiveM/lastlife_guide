# Zone Rules

Where you're standing decides the rules. Safe zones and red zones are marked on your map.

| Zone | Fighting | Robbing | Looting bodies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🛡️ **Safe zones** | Never | Never | Never |
| 🌍 **Open world** | With roleplay | With roleplay | Yes, see below |
| 🔴 **Red zones** | **Kill On Sight** | Anything goes | Yes |
| 🚇 **The subway** | **Kill On Sight** | Anything goes | Yes |

## 🛡️ Safe zones

* **No griefing.** That includes pushing, blocking doors, boxing people in, or running them over.
* **Don't bring the dead.** Leading a horde to a safe zone, or zombies onto people at its edge, is griefing.
* **No stealing** from people, vehicles or trades here.
* **Don't abuse the protection.** No running inside to escape a fight you started, and no shooting out from the edge.
* **Don't camp the exits.** You have **20 seconds** of protection when you leave. Nobody may wait outside to jump you when it ends.
* **Park out of the way.** Don't block traders, garages, benches or gates.

## 🌍 Open world

* **No Kill On Sight.** Fights need a story. See [PvP & Robberies](pvp.md).
* **Crews get 10 minutes** with their own bodies. After that anyone can search them.
* **Chasing?** If a fight starts in a red zone and moves out, it may continue without a break. Once you lose each other, the open world rules are back.

## 🔴 Red zones

* **Everyone is fair game**, the moment they cross the line. No roleplay needed.
* **Entering means you accept it.** You can't report being killed there.
* **Don't snipe across the line.** Shooting from outside into a red zone, or out of it at someone who isn't inside, counts as open world.
* **Hate, harassment, cheating and exploits** are still not allowed.

## 🚇 The subway

* **Kill On Sight** from the moment you're inside.
* **Don't block entrances or exits** with vehicles, bases or bodies.
* **Don't camp an entrance from the street.** The street is open world.

## Events

**Airdrops and helicopter crashes** don't change the rules of the place they land in. In the open world, warn people off or make demands first. If both sides come armed and refuse to leave, that's your story.
