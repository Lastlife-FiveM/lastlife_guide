# Vehicles

* **Don't run people over on purpose.** In the open world it counts as killing without roleplay.
* **Value your life and your vehicle.** Don't ram or jump off things in ways no survivor would.
* **Stealing is allowed** in the open world. If someone's sitting in it, it's a robbery, so [roleplay it](pvp.md).
* **Don't steal only to destroy.** Sinking or dumping vehicles where nobody can reach them is griefing.
* **Don't block** roads, gates, garages, traders or other people's bases with vehicles.
* **No towing people** inside their vehicle, or off with it, without roleplay.
* **Armed vehicles and helicopters** follow the same rules as any weapon. No shooting people on sight from the air.
* **Don't hover over bases** to look inside for hours or to push people around.
* **Stuck or lost it?** Ask for help in OOC or use **/report**. Staff won't replace vehicles lost in normal play.
