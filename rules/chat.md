# Chat, Voice & Streaming

## Chat and radio

* **Global and Crew** are in character. **OOC** is for everything else.
* **No spam**, all caps or arguing in OOC.
* **No spoilers.** Don't post secret spots, story finds or raid plans in OOC.
* **English** in public channels, so staff can help.

## Voice

* **No soundboards, voice changers or music over voice** that get on people's nerves or cover up a scene.
* **Don't jam a radio channel** to stop others using it.

## Streaming and recording

* Streaming is welcome. **Add a delay** if you don't want people to know where you are.
* **Stream sniping is metagaming**, whether you use it yourself or pass it on.
* Don't show other people's personal information.
* Recording for reports is always fine.
