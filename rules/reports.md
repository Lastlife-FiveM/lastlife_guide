# Reports & Punishments

## Reporting a player

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Finish the scene

Stay in character and play it out, even if you think a rule was broken.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save your proof

A clip is best. Keep the part **before** it went wrong too, with sound.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Report it

Type **/report** in game if it's happening now, or open a ticket in our Discord. Say **who**, **where**, **when** and **which rule**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Let staff handle it

Don't take revenge and don't call people out in public. Staff can't always tell you what the result was.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

* **Report within 48 hours.**
* **Both sides get heard.** Staff can ask for your recording.
* **False or edited reports** are punished.

## What can happen

| How serious | Examples | What it can lead to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minor** | Bad name, OOC arguing, blocking by accident | A talk or a warning |
| **Medium** | Killing without roleplay, metagaming, blocking a road with a base | Warning, pieces removed, kick or a short ban |
| **Serious** | Repeated griefing, combat logging, exploiting | Longer ban, items or bases removed |
| **Zero tolerance** | Cheating, duping, hate, real-life threats, doxxing | Permanent ban, no warning needed |

Staff look at what happened, what you meant to do and your history. Breaking rules again makes it worse. Being honest makes it better.

## Getting things back

* **Lost to a bug or a cheater?** Open a ticket with proof. Staff may give back what can be shown.
* **Lost in normal play**, to a crash, a raid or a horde? It's gone.

## Appeals

* Open a ticket in Discord and explain what happened, calmly.
* **One appeal at a time.** Don't message staff one by one.
* Someone other than the staff member who punished you looks at it whenever possible.
