# Reports & Punishments

## Reporting

* In game, type **/report** and say what happened.
* For bigger things, open a ticket in our Discord.
* **Bring proof.** A clip says more than a paragraph. Include names, the place and the time.
* **Stay calm in the scene.** Finish the roleplay first, then report.

## What can happen

Staff look at what happened, how bad it was and your history. That can mean a note, a warning, a kick, a ban for some time, or a permanent ban. Cheating, hate and real-life threats can go straight to a permanent ban.

Items lost to a bug can sometimes be given back if you have proof. Items lost to normal play can't.

Think a punishment was unfair? Open a ticket in Discord and explain, calmly, what happened.
