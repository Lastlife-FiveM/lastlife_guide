# Respect & Conduct

These apply everywhere: in game, in chat, over voice and in our Discord. Being in character is never an excuse for breaking them.

## Never allowed

* **Hate.** Slurs, or attacks over race, religion, sex, gender, sexuality, nationality or disability.
* **Real-life threats** or telling someone to harm themselves.
* **Sharing personal information** (doxxing), or threatening to.
* **Sexual harassment**, sexual roleplay with people who haven't agreed to it, and anything sexual involving minors.
* **Roleplaying sexual assault**, real tragedies or real hate groups.

## Harassment and toxicity

* **Don't target people.** Following, baiting or killing the same player over and over to ruin their time is harassment.
* **Trash talk between characters is fine.** Attacking the player behind the character isn't.
* **If someone asks you to stop** something out of character, stop.
* **Don't carry fights** from other servers or from Discord into the game.

## Names and looks

* **Use a believable first and last name.** No joke names, famous people, slurs or symbols.
* **Crew names, tags and outfits** follow the same rules. Staff can change or remove anything offensive.

## Around the server

* **No advertising** other servers or communities.
* **No real-money trading** of items, chits, bases, vehicles or accounts.
* **No begging staff** for items, spawns or teleports.
* **Don't impersonate** staff or other players.
* **Follow staff instructions** in the moment. Disagree? Open a ticket afterwards.
