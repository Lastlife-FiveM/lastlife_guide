# Respect & Conduct

* **No hate.** Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia and slurs are never allowed, in or out of character.
* **No harassment.** Don't follow, bait or target someone over and over to ruin their time.
* **No real-life threats** and no sharing anyone's personal information.
* **No sexual content**, and no roleplaying sexual assault or anything involving minors.
* **Keep it in the story.** A fight between survivors is not a fight between players.
* **Use a believable name.** Joke, offensive or famous names get changed.
* **Use a working microphone or text** when you need to roleplay with someone.
* **No advertising** other servers or selling in-game things for real money.
* **Listen to staff.** Don't lie to them, impersonate them or argue a decision in public. Open a ticket instead.
