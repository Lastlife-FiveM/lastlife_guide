# Raiding

LastLife is a roleplay server, not a wipe-everything raid game. People have lives outside the city, and nobody should log in to an empty base. Raids are allowed, but they need a story, they happen at set times, and they leave the other side something to come back from.

## When raids can happen

Raids only happen on **weekend evenings**. The game won't let you damage a base at any other time (US Central time):

| Day | Raid hours |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday | 6 pm – 11 pm |
| Sunday | 6 pm – 11 pm |

Monday to Friday, every base is safe.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Raid hours don't switch off roleplay.** Every rule below still applies during them: you need a reason, you make contact first, and you match their numbers.
{% endhint %}

* **Offline bases are protected.** A base becomes safe **15 minutes** after the last owner logs off.
* **Logging off doesn't stop a raid.** Once one has started, the base stays raidable for **45 minutes**.
* **Check raiding status** on a piece before you start. If the game says it's protected, leave it.

## Before you raid

* **You need a reason.** A war, a grudge, a debt, something they stole: a story both sides could know about. "They have loot" isn't one.
* **Make contact first.** A demand at the gate, a threat over the radio, a note on their mailbox. Give them a moment to answer, pay up or get ready. Then you can breach.
* **Match their numbers.** Bring **no more raiders than the defenders have online**. Against a solo player, you may bring **two**.
* **Use the tools the game gives you**, like explosives. No clipping, boosting vehicles through walls, or reaching through with a camera.
* **Don't get around offline protection.** Keeping a crew raidable on purpose, or waiting on someone's log-off to finish, counts.

## During a raid

* **Inside the base and right around it**, raiders and defenders may shoot each other without talking first.
* **People who aren't part of it** still have open world rules. Don't shoot passers-by.
* **No combat logging**, on either side.
* **Died in the raid? You're out** until it's over. You can come back for your body afterwards.

## What you take

* **Take at most about half.** Take what you came for, up to roughly half of what's in their storage. Never clean someone out.
* **Leave the base standing.** Breach what you need to get in, nothing more. Don't level it.
* **Don't wall people out** of their own base, or take it over while they still use it.
* **Once is enough.** Don't raid the same crew again within **48 hours**, unless they raid you first.

## Insiders

* Stealing from your own crew is allowed **if it's a story**, not a way to grief or to move items between your own characters.
* Joining a crew only to empty its base the same day is griefing.
* Crews choose who gets access. **Staff won't give back** what someone you trusted took.
