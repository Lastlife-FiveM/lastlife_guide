# Bases & Raiding

* **Don't block the world.** Keep roads, safe zones, traders, loot areas, extraction entrances and event areas open.
* **You can build over a road, but not on it.** Any walkway over a road must be at least **3 base walls tall**, measured from the ground, so vehicles can pass underneath.
* **No glitched bases.** Don't build inside map objects or anywhere the game doesn't let others reach.
* **Claims are not weapons.** Don't place totems just to trap, box in or lock out someone else.
* **Raid with the tools the game gives you.** No glitching through walls and no clipping cameras or vehicles inside.
* **Offline protection is there for a reason.** Don't try to get around it.
* **Don't grief.** Take what you came for. Don't come back again and again only to make someone quit.
