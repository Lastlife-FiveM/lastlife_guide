# Cheating & Exploits

## Cheats

* **No** mod menus, injected code, aim help, ESP, no-recoil, speed or fly hacks, or anything like them.
* **No scripts or macros** that fire, craft, gather, fish or train for you. One key press, one action.
* **Having cheats running while connected** counts, even if you "weren't using them".

## Game edits

* **No** packs, graphics edits or settings that remove fog, darkness, plants, walls or smoke.
* **No** edited sounds or tracers that show where people are.
* **Night is meant to be dark.** Don't use brightness or colour filters to cancel it out.
* Graphics packs that only make things look nicer are fine.

## Exploits

* **Found a bug?** Report it and stop using it. Nobody gets punished for an honest report.
* **No duping.** Keeping, using, selling or passing on duped items counts too.
* **No glitching** through walls, into rocks, under the map or onto places you can't normally reach.
* **No abusing** restarts, crashes, log-offs, zone edges or animations to save yourself or get items.
* **No farming AFK** by tricking the game into thinking you're there.

## Accounts

* **One person, one account.** No second accounts for storage, votes or to get around a ban.
* **Your account is your responsibility**, whoever was playing.
