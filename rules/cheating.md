# Cheating & Exploits

* **No cheats.** No mod menus, aim help, ESP, macros that fire for you, or injected code.
* **No unfair game edits.** No packs or settings that remove fog, darkness, walls or plants, or that give you a crosshair the server doesn't.
* **No exploits.** Found a bug that gives items, money or safety? Report it and don't use it.
* **No duping.** Keeping or passing on duped items counts too.
* **One player, one account.** No alternate accounts to dodge a ban or get around limits.
