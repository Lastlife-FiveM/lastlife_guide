# Crafting & Research

Everything worth having gets made at a bench. Start at a public bench in a safe zone, then build your own and unlock better recipes through **Research**.

## The benches

| Bench | Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🛠️ **Safe-zone bench** | Public, in safe zones. Look for the **Crafting bench** icon on your map. | The basics to get you started: bandages, painkillers, splints, water, a knife, a bat, nails, planks, a stone hatchet, a pickaxe, scrap 9mm rounds, a GPS, a motorized bike, and the **Totem** and **Crafting Table** for your first base. |
| 🪚 **Workbench** | The Crafting Table, placed at your base | Almost everything: medical, weapons, ammo, tools, attachments, armour, packs, vehicle parts, materials and every base piece. It also has the **Research** and **Blueprints** tabs. |
| 🔫 **Weapons table** | Placed at your base | Repair kits for your gear and vehicles. |
| 🔥 **Campfire** and **Hobo stove** | Placed at your base | Cooking, and water. Only work while lit. |
| 🏭 **Smelter** | Placed at your base | Turns ore into ingots. Only works while lit. |
| ♻️ **Recycler** | Placed at your base | Breaks junk down into materials. |
| 💧 **Water well** | Placed at your base | Fills empty bottles with water. |

To use a bench, walk up and press **E** while looking at its screen, or third-eye it.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Fires burn fuel.** Campfires and stoves burn wood. The smelter burns coal. Fill them up and switch them on, or they won't open. A job that's already running still finishes if the fire goes out.
{% endhint %}

## How crafting works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Pick a recipe

Browse by category, or search. Each recipe shows what you have and what you need.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hold to craft

Hold the button until it fills. A quick tap does nothing. The materials leave your pockets **right away**, and the job joins the queue along the bottom of the screen.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Walk away if you like

The queue keeps running while you're gone, even if you log out. Each bench only holds so many of your jobs at once.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Claim it

Come back and press **Claim** on the finished tile. Whatever fits goes into your pockets. Anything that doesn't fit waits at the bench until you make room.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Pin what you're working toward.** Pin a few recipes and they show on your HUD with live counts. Missing materials show in red, so you know what to grab on your next run.
{% endhint %}

### More than one recipe

Some items can be made more than one way. When they can, the recipe shows a **Change recipe** bar. Use it to switch between the ways and pick whichever one you have the materials for.

A few examples:

* **Iron Ingot:** from iron ore, or from metal scrap.
* **Gun Stock:** from wood, or from plastic.
* **Water:** at a bench, or at a fire.

### Rarity

Crafted items carry a **rarity**, shown in your inventory. From most common to rarest:

| Scrap | Salvaged | Scarce | Hoarded | Relic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Plain **Scrap** items stack with the same items you loot.

## Smelting

Raw ore is no good until it's been through a smelter. Mine it first (see [Gathering](gathering.md)), then:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Fuel it

Put **coal** in the smelter and switch it on. You'll see the fire inside.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Smelt

Open it like any bench. It makes:

* **Ingots:** iron, copper, aluminium, lead and zinc.
* **Brass:** copper and zinc together.
* **Silver and gold bars:** worth trading.
* **Steel:** needs the **Smelting** blueprint.
* **Metal Alloy:** needs the **Metalwork** blueprint.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Blueprints go in the smelter itself.** The smelter has its own blueprint stash. A blueprint sitting in your workbench won't unlock anything at the smelter, so move it over.
{% endhint %}

## Recycling

The recycler turns junk into something useful. It needs no power. Pick what you want out of it, and use the recipe switcher to choose what goes in:

| Put in | Get out |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | Cloth |
| Car or motorcycle tires | Rubber |
| Small TVs, toasters | Electronics |
| Electronics, cables | Copper |
| Toasters | Scrap |
| Empty bottles | Plastic |
| Pistols and rifles | Weapon parts |

## Chits

**Chits** are the currency of the wasteland. You spend them on research.

Ways to get them:

* **Search zombies.** Now and then one has a few on it. Bosses carry more.
* **Clear out nests.**
* **Sweep the sand** with a metal detector.
* **Sell to traders** at the safe zones.

## Research and blueprints

Your workbench has a **Research** tab. It's a tree of nodes. Each one gives you a **blueprint**, and blueprints unlock new recipes.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Pick a node

Click a node to see what it unlocks. Some nodes need others done first. The lines show the way.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pay and wait

Hold to research. It costs **chits** plus some materials, taken when you start. Then the clock runs. It keeps running while you're away or offline. You can only research one thing at a time.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take the blueprint

Come back and take your blueprint. You need room in your pockets for it. Each node can only be researched once per character.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put it in a bench

Open the **Blueprints** tab and drag the blueprint from your inventory into the bench. Its recipes light up on the Craft tab.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**A blueprint is a real item, and anyone can take it.** It unlocks recipes at that bench for **everyone** who uses it, and anyone at the bench can drag it out and walk off with it. If your bench is destroyed, its blueprints fall on the ground for whoever gets there first. Keep your base locked.
{% endhint %}

### The research tree

There are three branches. Early nodes are cheap and quick. Later ones cost a lot more and take longer.

## Tips

* **Start at the safe-zone bench.** Nails, planks, a pickaxe, a Totem and a Crafting Table are all you need to get going.
* **Queue before a run.** Set a long job going, head out, and claim it when you're back.
* **Save your chits.** The late nodes are expensive, so don't blow them all early.
* **Check the switcher.** Short on ore? There might be another way to make what you need.
