# HUD, Map & GPS

Your HUD tells you if you're about to starve, bleed out or run dry. The map tells you where the safe ground is. Learn to read both at a glance.

## What the HUD shows

### Your bars

| Bar | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **HP** | Your health. |
| **ARM** | Your armour, from the plates in your carrier. See [Inventory & Gear](inventory.md). |
| **FOOD** | Hunger. Eat before it runs out. |
| **H2O** | Thirst. Drink before it runs out. |
| **STA** | Stamina. Drops while you sprint, comes back when you rest. |
| **INF** | Infection. Fills up when you breathe bad air in the subway. See [The Subway](subway.md). |
| **O2** | Air. Only shows under water. |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Red and pulsing means trouble.** A bar that's running low turns red and starts to pulse. Infection works the other way round: it's bad when it's **high**.
{% endhint %}

### Everything else

* **Compass** at the top: which way you're facing, plus the street and area you're in.
* **Status box**: your ID photo, name, ID, class, your voice range (it lights up when you talk), your cash, and your radio channel when you're on one.
* **Weapon**: when you draw a weapon, its picture, name and ammo show. The clip number goes red when you're nearly empty.
* **Vehicle**: in a vehicle you get speed, gear, revs, fuel, engine and body condition, plus your seatbelt, lights and lock. Aircraft show altitude and heading too.
* **Pinned recipes**: crafting recipes you've pinned, with the materials you still need.
* **Subway card**: inside the tunnels, a card shows your filter, your run and the exits that are open.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Buckle up.** Press **B** for your seatbelt. If you drive off without it, the HUD chimes until you buckle up. **Y** turns cruise control on and off.
{% endhint %}

## The minimap needs a GPS

Out here, you don't get a map for free. **The minimap only shows when a GPS sits in the GPS slot** of your inventory. Take it out and the map is gone.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Craft a GPS

You can make one at a safe zone workbench right from the start.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put it in the GPS slot

Open your inventory and drag the GPS into the **GPS** slot in your gear. In your pockets it does nothing.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Read the map

Your minimap appears in the corner.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

What the minimap shows:

* **You**: the white arrow in the middle.
* **Places**: the same icons as the pause menu map. Nearby ones show up as you get close.
* **Zones**: safe zones, red zones and nests, drawn as glowing areas.
* **Your route**: a red line to your waypoint.
* **Your crew**: your crewmates as dots in your crew colour.
* **Noise**: a faint red circle around you shows how far zombies can hear you.

The map zooms out the faster you go, and much further when you fly. Hold **Z** to look further out for a moment.

## The pause menu

Press **Esc** or **P** to open the pause menu. The game keeps running behind it, so don't stop to read the map in the middle of a horde.

| Page | What's on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | The full map of the state. |
| **Keybinds** | Every key you can change. Click a key, press the new one. It saves when you close the menu. |
| **Settings** | Graphics, audio and controls, plus the menu sound volume. |
| **HUD** | The HUD editor and the crosshair creator. |
| **Crew** | Your crew. See [Chat, Radio & Crews](communication.md). |
| **Skills** | Your skills and how far you've trained them. See [Skills & Gym](skills.md). |

Your character's name and class sit on the left. **Leave server** asks you once more before it lets you go.

## The big map

The pause menu map works with or without a GPS.

* **Drag** to move, **scroll** to zoom, and the target button brings you back to yourself.
* **Double-click** a road to set a waypoint. A red route line shows you the way.
* **Right-click** to clear it.
* **Hover** over an icon or a zone to see its name.

### The legend

The legend lists every kind of place on the map. Click a row to hide or show it, on the big map and the minimap both.

| Group | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| 🏰 **Safe places** | Safe zones and traders. |
| ☠️ **Danger** | Red zones, zombie nests and the subway. |
| 🛒 **Shops and supplies** | Stores, workbenches and other shops. |
| 🏥 **Services** | Hospitals and other public buildings. |
| 🚗 **Vehicles** | Fuel, garages and places to find vehicles. |
| 🧱 **Base building** | Areas where you can't build. |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Green is safe. Red is not.** Safe zones glow green. Red zones and nests glow red and amber. Know which one you're walking into.
{% endhint %}

### Map settings

Open **Map settings** at the bottom right of the map to make it yours: how zone edges look, icon size, the route line's width and style, brightness, the size of your arrow and more. Your choices are saved for next time.

## Make the HUD yours

Go to **Esc > HUD > Open HUD editor**.

* **Pick a layout.** There are five: **Field tape** (the default), **Rings**, **Survival watch**, **Ghost** (fades away when nothing's wrong) and **Skewed**.
* **Move and resize** anything by dragging it. Switch single rows on or off.
* **General settings**: speed in mph or km/h, how dark the backing is, how the map turns, the seatbelt chime and more.
* Press **Enter** to save, **Esc** to cancel.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Lost something?** Type `/resethud` in chat and everything goes back to the default.
{% endhint %}

### Crosshair

Under **Esc > HUD > Crosshair** you can build your own crosshair. Pick a preset or change every part of it, set one per weapon type, or draw your own. Save them to your library and share them with friends using a code. Your crosshair only shows while you aim.

## Tips

* **Craft a GPS first thing.** Running without a map is how people end up in a nest.
* **Watch the noise circle.** If it's big, everything nearby knows where you are.
* **Plan routes on the big map.** Set a waypoint before you leave the safe zone, not while you're running.
* **Hide what you don't need.** Switch off legend rows you don't care about to keep the map clean.
