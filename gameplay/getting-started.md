# Getting Started

Everyone starts the same way: a refugee in a queue, a form on a clipboard, and then everything goes wrong. This page walks you through your first few minutes, from joining the server to crafting your first tools.

## Joining the server

While the game loads, you'll see the LastLife loading screen. It's worth a look:

* **Your welcome card** shows your name, how many survivors are online and how long you've played.
* **Next restart** tells you when the city resets, in your own time.
* **Tips** change above the loading bar. Read them, they're short.
* **Survivor Radio** in the corner plays music while you wait. Press play if you want it.

When it's done, the screen fades out and you arrive at the survivor camp.

## The survivor camp

This is your character select. Your survivors sit around a campfire at a farm at night, while the dead rattle the fence behind them.

* Switch between seats with **A** and **D**.
* Pick a survivor and press **Enter** to play.
* An empty seat with a dropped backpack is a free slot. Pick it to **start a new life**.
* To delete a survivor, press **Del** and type their full name. It's gone for good.

{% hint style="info" %}
Every player gets a couple of character slots to start with.
{% endhint %}

## Making a new survivor

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### The broadcast and the queue

You hear a radio broadcast, then you're standing in the refugee line at the Fort Zancudo checkpoint. Rain, floodlights, soldiers. Wait your turn.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill in the intake form

At the desk you get a paper form. Fill in your name, date of birth, height, sex and nationality. Your blood type is assigned for you.

Then pick your **class** (see below), sign the form and hand it in.

Names have to look like real names. Joke names and banned words get sent back.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Your photo and Survivor ID

Look at the camera. You get your **Survivor ID** card with your photo, your details and an ID number. It goes in your inventory. Use it any time to take a look at it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### The medical tent

Here you see your body for the first time. This is where you make your character's look. Take your time.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Run

The checkpoint is overrun. Alarms, gunfire, the dead pouring in. You run.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wake up

You wake up at the **Galileo Observatory**, a safe zone, with your starting kit. From here, you're on your own.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Making a second survivor?** You skip the broadcast and the queue and go straight to the form. You can skip the overrun too.
{% endhint %}

## Classes

Your class is what your survivor did before the fall. You pick it on the intake form, and it decides your **starting kit**.

| Class | What they're about | Starting kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Soldier** | Frontline fighter. | A pistol (empty), a little 9mm ammo, a knife |
| ⛑️ **Medic** | Keeps the crew alive. | Bandages, painkillers, a first aid kit |
| 🔧 **Mechanic** | Keeps the wheels turning. | A wrench, a repair kit, some scrap metal |
| 🔍 **Scavenger** | Finds what others miss. | Lockpicks, a flashlight |
| 🔨 **Engineer** | Builds and holds the base. | A hammer |
| ❤️ **Survivor** | Built to last. | A knife, extra water and food |

**Everyone** also gets water, a sandwich, a **BMX**, a **Stone Hatchet** and their **Survivor ID**.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Class perks are still being built.** Each class has four perk tiers, and you'll see them on the form. During the beta, your class only decides your starting kit. Pick the one that fits how you want to play.
{% endhint %}

## Choosing where you wake up

When you come back with a survivor who has already been out in the world, you get the spawn screen before you drop in.

| Option | Where it puts you |
| --- | --- |
| 📍 **Last location** | Right where you logged out. |
| 🛡️ **Galileo Observatory** | The safe zone. |
| 🛏️ **Your bed** | Any bed you've placed at a base. |

Switch between them with **A** and **D**, then press **Enter** to spawn. Press **Esc** to go back to the camp.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Last location means last location.** If you logged out in the middle of a horde, that's where you'll be.
{% endhint %}

## Your first steps

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Stay in the safe zone for a minute

You wake up at the Observatory. Nothing can hurt you here, and no zombies spawn. Check your inventory and get your bearings. See [Zones & PvP](zones.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Use the bench

The Observatory has a public **crafting bench**. It doesn't make much, but it makes enough to get going: basic medical supplies, a knife, a bat, a pickaxe, scrap ammo and a **GPS**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Get a GPS

You can't see the map without one. Craft a **GPS** and put it in its gear slot, and your minimap comes back.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Go gather

Take your Stone Hatchet to the nearest trees and start chopping. Craft a pickaxe and break some rock. See [Gathering](gathering.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start a base

Back at the bench, craft a **Totem** and a **Crafting Table**. You can only build inside a totem's area, so the totem always goes down first.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Tips

* **Ride your BMX.** Use it from your inventory to put it down, and third-eye it to pick it back up. Later you can turn it into a motorized bike at the bench.
* **Daytime is kinder.** There are fewer zombies during the day, and they hit softer.
* **Don't head into a red zone on day one.** You will lose everything you've got.
* **Find a crew.** Everything is easier with people watching your back.
