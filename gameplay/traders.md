# Traders

Not everyone out here wants to kill you. A few survivors have set up shop in the safe zones. They'll take what you've scavenged and pay you in **chits**, or swap you something useful for it.

## Finding a trader

Traders are people standing in the safe zones. Open your map and look under **Traders** to find them.

Each trader deals in their own line of goods. A scrap dealer wants your junk electronics and cables. A medic wants bandages and cloth. If one trader won't touch something, try another.

## How to trade

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Walk up and third-eye them

Look at the trader and open your third eye. Pick **Trade with** them to open the trade screen.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### See what they want today

Your pockets and backpack show on the right. Anything this trader buys is highlighted.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Sell or barter

Pick a tab:

* **Sell for chits:** hand items over, get chits back.
* **Barter:** swap items for items. Some swaps take chits as part of the deal.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Only your pockets and backpack count.** Traders never touch what's in your hands or your gear slots, so your weapon and armour are safe. Move anything you want to sell into your pockets or bag first.
{% endhint %}

## Prices change every day

Traders don't want the same things forever. Every server restart, each one picks a fresh handful of offers, and their prices move too.

| | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 📈 **Good day** | A trader pays more than usual for something. Sell it now. |
| 📉 **Bad day** | They're paying less than usual. Hold on to it, or try another trader. |
| 🔄 **New offers** | Something they wanted yesterday might not be on their list today. |

The trade screen shows whether each price is above or below the usual one.

## Stock is shared

Every trader only wants so much of each thing, and **that's shared with every survivor on the server**. The screen shows how many more they'll buy today.

Once they've taken enough, they're **full up** until the next restart. If you're sitting on a pile of something, don't wait too long, or someone else might get there first.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Make room first.** If you can't carry what you're getting back, the trade won't go through. You won't lose anything, though: a failed trade hands everything back.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Shop around.** The same item can fetch a very different price from one day to the next.
* **Sell early in the day.** Stock is shared, so the first survivors in get the best of it.
* **Barter for what you can't find.** Sometimes a swap gets you something that's hard to scavenge.
* **Clean your loot first.** Items still marked as exposed from the subway can't be traded. Decontaminate them first.
* **Walking away closes the screen.** Stay close until you're done.
