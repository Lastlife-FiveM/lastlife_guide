# Chat, Radio & Crews

The grid went down with everything else. If you want to talk to anyone past shouting distance, you need a radio. And if you want to stay alive, you want a crew.

## Text chat needs a radio

Text chat runs over the radio. **No radio in your Radio slot, no chat.** You can still type commands, but your messages won't go out, and you won't see anyone else's.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get a radio

Craft one after the **Radio Repair** research, or trade for one with a trader when they have it in stock.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put it in the Radio slot

Open your inventory and drag the radio into the **Radio** slot in your gear. A radio in your pockets doesn't count.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open chat

Press **T**. Your chat now works as a pager.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Chat keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **T** | Open chat. |
| **Tab** | Next channel (or finish a command you're typing). |
| **↑** / **↓** | Go through what you sent before. |
| **Esc** | Close chat. |
| **L** | Switch how chat shows: **When active**, **Show** or **Hide**. Your choice is saved. |

### Channels

| Channel | Who hears it |
| --- | --- |
| **Global** | Everyone with a radio. |
| **Crew** | Only your crew. Your crew tag shows in your crew colour. Needs a crew. |
| **OOC** | Out of character talk. Shows your player ID. |
| **Announcements** | Server news. You can't write here, only read. |

New messages on your channel pop up for a moment. Messages on other channels add a count on that channel's button.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Announcements always get through.** Server news pops up with a pager sound, even when chat is hidden. You still need a radio in the slot to get it.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**No signal underground.** In the subway, text chat cuts out: you can't send or get Global, Crew or OOC. Commands and radio voice still work.
{% endhint %}

## Talking

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **`** (the key under Esc) | Change how far your voice carries. The status box on your HUD shows your range. |
| **Left Alt** | Talk on the radio, once you're on a channel. |

To talk over the radio, **use** the radio from your inventory. It opens the radio, where you pick a channel. Everyone on the same channel hears you when you hold the talk key. Your HUD shows the channel you're on and turns red while you transmit.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Pick your channel with your crew before you split up.** Out here, a quiet channel is a safe channel.
{% endhint %}

## Crews

A crew is your group: you share a base, vehicles, a chat channel and each other's backs. Everything to do with your crew is on the **Crew** page of the pause menu (**Esc > Crew**).

### Start or join one

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Start a crew

On the Crew page, pick **Start a crew**. Give it a name, a short tag and a colour. Your tag and colour show in chat, on the map and over your heads.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Invite people

Stand close to the person you want, open **Members** and pick **Invite nearby player**. They get a notification and find your invite under **Invites** on their own Crew page.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### They join

New members join as **Recruit**. Promote them when they've earned it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Crew ranks

Every crew starts with five ranks. The leader can rename them and change what each one is allowed to do on the **Ranks** page.

| Rank | Starts with |
| --- | --- |
| **Leader** | Everything. Only one, the crew's owner. |
| **Officer** | Nearly everything, including inviting, removing and promoting lower ranks. |
| **Builder** | Building at the crew base, storage and crew vehicles. |
| **Member** | Doors, storage and crew vehicles. |
| **Recruit** | Crew doors only. |

Permissions cover building, doors, storage, stocks, crew vehicles, managing members, seeing the activity log and changing the tag and colour.

### On the page

* **Members**: who's in, who's online, invite, promote, remove.
* **Ranks**: rename ranks and set what each can do.
* **Activity**: a log of joins, leaves, rank changes and more. Only ranks allowed to see it get the tab.
* **Map**: your crewmates show as dots in your crew colour on both maps. Hide single people, or everyone, just for you.
* **Name tags**: turn them on to see `[TAG] Name` over your crewmates' heads when they're close. Off by default.

The leader can also **hand over** the crew to someone else.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Think before you leave.** After you leave a crew, there's a wait before anyone can invite you back in. You can still start your own straight away.
{% endhint %}

## Your rank

Separate from your crew rank, you have your own **survivor rank**. You earn XP for surviving out here, and enough XP takes you up a rank.

| How you earn XP |
| --- |
| Killing zombies. |
| Hunting animals. |
| Getting out of the subway alive. |
| A bonus when your crewmates get out of the subway with you. |

When you earn XP, a bar shows how much you got and how close you are to the next rank. Press **Z** to see your rank at any time.

## Tips

* **Your radio is your lifeline.** No radio, no chat, no news. Keep a spare.
* **Use Crew chat for plans.** Global is read by everyone, including the people you're about to rob.
* **Don't hand out Officer lightly.** Officers can remove people and change ranks.
* **Turn on name tags in a firefight.** It's how you tell your crew from the people shooting at you.
