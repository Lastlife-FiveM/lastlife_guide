# Gathering

Before you can build, craft or trade, you need materials. You'll get them straight from the world: break rock, cut down trees and search the beaches.

## How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get the right tool in your hands

Each job needs its own tool, and it has to be **in your hands**, not just in your bag.

* **Axes and pickaxes:** draw them like you would a weapon.
* **Power tools:** **use** the item from your inventory to take it out. Use it again to put it away.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the right spot

Go up to rock, a tree trunk or sand, depending on what you're after, and look right at it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Third-eye it

Open your third eye. If you're holding the right tool and you're close enough, you'll see what you can do: **Mine rock**, **Chop tree** and so on.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Do the work

Your survivor gets to work. When the bar fills up, the materials go into your inventory. You can cancel at any time, but then you get nothing.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Every spot runs out.** A rock or tree only gives so much before it's worked out. Move on to the next one, or come back later and it'll be ready again.
{% endhint %}

## What you can gather

| Where | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| ⛏️ **Rock**: cliffs, boulders, quarries | Stone, plus ores and minerals now and then. Iron, copper and coal are common. Some ores are rare, and a few are *very* rare. |
| 🪓 **Trees** | Wood logs, plus sticks and tree bark. |
| 🔍 **Sand**: beaches and desert | Only with a Metal Detector. Scrap, lost cash, ammo, and now and then something valuable someone buried and never came back for. |

## Tools

Better tools work faster and get more out of every spot. You start with the basics and unlock the rest through **Research**.

| Tool | For | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stone Hatchet | Trees | You start with one |
| Hatchet | Trees | Craft it |
| Pickaxe | Rock | Craft it |
| Metal Detector | Sand | Craft it |
| Battle Axe | Trees | Research |
| Jackhammer | Rock | Research |
| Chainsaw | Trees | Research |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Power tools are heavy.** The jackhammer and chainsaw are the best at what they do, but they take up a good chunk of your carry weight. Plan your trip.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Bring a bag.** Ore and logs add up fast. If your pockets are full, whatever doesn't fit drops at your feet.
* **Work in groups.** You can't fight while you're busy, so have someone keep watch.
* **Hold on to rare finds.** Silver, gold and rough diamonds are hard to come by.
* **Smelt your ore.** Raw ore isn't much use until it's been through a smelter.
