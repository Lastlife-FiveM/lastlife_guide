# Dying & Your Body

You will die. Probably a lot. When it happens, you don't lose your survivor, but you do lose everything you were carrying. The good news: it's all still on your body, if you can get back to it first.

## Going down

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### You're down

Take too much damage and you drop. You're not dead yet, but you're bleeding out. The screen tells you how long you've got.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### A friend can pick you up

While you're down, anyone with a **First Aid** kit can help you up. It takes them a while, it uses up the kit, and they can't fight while they're doing it. Get someone to watch their back.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### You're dead

If nobody gets to you in time, you die. **Hold E** to respawn.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wake up

You wake up at the **Galileo Observatory** safe zone with **empty pockets**. Everything you had is still back where you fell.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Getting revived means no corpse.** If a friend picks you up, you keep everything you're carrying.
{% endhint %}

## Your body

When you respawn, your body stays where you died, wearing your clothes, with **everything you had on you**:

* Your weapons and ammo
* Your plate carrier, with the plates still in it
* Your backpack, with everything still inside
* Your cash
* Food, meds, materials, all of it

If you died with nothing on you, there's no body.

### Searching a body

Walk right up to it. A small **Corpse** prompt shows above it. Press **E**, or third-eye the body and pick **Search**. It opens like any other container: take what you want.

## Who can loot it

| Where you died | Who can search your body |
| --- | --- |
| 🌍 **Open world** | Only **you and your crew** at first. After a while, **anyone**. |
| 🔴 **Red zone** | **Anyone**, straight away. |
| 🚇 **Subway** | **Anyone**, straight away. |

Anyone who isn't allowed yet gets told they can't search it yet.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Bodies don't last forever.** After a while, your body disappears, and so does whatever is still on it. A server restart clears every body too. If you want your stuff back, go now.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Go back fast, but not alone.** Whatever killed you is probably still there.
* **Carry a First Aid kit** and make sure your crew does too. A revive saves you the walk.
* **Carry bandages.** They stop bleeding. Don't let a small wound turn into a long walk back.
* **Think about what you bring into a red zone.** If you die there, anyone can take it the second you fall.
* **The other side of the coin:** a dead survivor in a red zone is a loot box. Others are thinking the same about you.
