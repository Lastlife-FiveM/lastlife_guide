# Zones & PvP

Los Santos isn't one big free-for-all. Some places are safe, some are lawless, and everywhere in between runs on roleplay. Know which one you're standing in.

## The rules at a glance

| Zone | Fighting | Loot | Zombies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🛡️ **Safe zones** | None. You can't attack anyone. | No looting | None |
| 🌍 **Open world** | Only if it's roleplayed out | Normal | Everywhere |
| 🔴 **Red zones** | **Kill On Sight** | Better (tier 2) | Tougher, plus bosses and nests |
| 🚇 **Extraction** (the subway) | **Kill On Sight** | The best (tier 3) | Toughest |

## 🛡️ Safe zones

Safe zones are where survivors catch their breath. There are four:

* **Galileo Observatory**
* **Sandy Shores**
* **Del Perro Pier**
* **Paleto Bay**

They're marked in green on your map. A message pops up when you walk in or out.

Inside a safe zone:

* **No fighting.** You can't shoot or swing at anyone, and nobody can hurt you.
* **No zombies.** They don't spawn here, and any that wander in are gone.
* **No looting.** Containers inside a safe zone can't be searched.

{% hint style="info" %}
**You stay protected for a short moment after you leave.** A countdown tells you when your protection runs out. Use it to get clear of the edge, not to start a fight.
{% endhint %}

The Observatory is also where new survivors wake up, and it has a public crafting bench. See [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

## 🌍 The open world

Everywhere that isn't a safe zone, a red zone or the subway is open world. This is where you'll spend most of your time: gathering, looting and building.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**PvP in the open world has to be roleplayed.** No random killing. If you want to rob, fight or kill another survivor out here, there has to be a story behind it: talk, threaten, make demands. Shooting someone on sight out here breaks the rules.
{% endhint %}

The dead are everywhere out here. There are more of them at night, and they're tougher in the dark.

## 🔴 Red zones

Red zones are where the good stuff is, and where people die for it. They're marked in red on your map. Right now that's the **Canal Houses** and the **Prison**.

* **Kill On Sight.** Anyone you see can shoot you, and you can shoot them. No roleplay needed.
* **Better loot.** On top of the normal stuff, you can find rifles, strong pistols, shotguns, attachments, weapon parts and heavier ammo.
* **Tougher zombies.** They take more hits and they hit harder.
* **Bosses** turn up here (see below).
* **Bodies are fair game at once.** If you die in a red zone, anyone can search your body straight away. See [Dying & Your Body](death.md).

## 🚇 Extraction: the subway

The subway under the city is the most dangerous place in Los Santos, and the most rewarding.

* **Kill On Sight**, just like a red zone.
* **The best loot in the game.** Explosives, snipers, heavy guns and the top scopes only come from the subway crates.
* **The toughest zombies.** They take a lot more hits down here than they do on the surface.

Getting in takes a **Transit Access Card**, a **Gas Mask** you're wearing, and at least one **Gas Filter**. The air down there will kill you without them. Only a few entrances are open at a time, and they change. Getting out is a whole thing of its own. See [The Subway](subway.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Bring a crew, bring spare filters, and don't get greedy.** What you find down there is only yours once you make it out.
{% endhint %}

## Zombie nests

Nests are where the dead come from. They only grow in **red zones** now, and they show on your map once you're close.

* A nest keeps sending zombies at you while it stands.
* Standing right next to one hurts.
* **Burn it.** Molotovs are how you take a nest down.
* Whoever destroys a nest gets its **loot** straight into their inventory: weapon parts, ammo, attachments, and now and then a rifle. If your bag is full, it drops at your feet.

Nests grow back after a while.

## Bosses

Some infected aren't like the rest. In the red zones you can run into **bosses**: ones that burn, ones that are toxic, and the juggernaut. They're hard to put down, but every boss drops **boss loot** when it falls.

## Armed guards

The best military spots aren't run by the dead. Armed survivors hold **Fort Zancudo**, **NOOSE HQ**, **LSIA**, the **aircraft carrier** and **Cayo Perico**. They shoot on sight.

* These spots are marked as **Enemy Nests** on your map.
* Take out the nest and you get its loot: weapon parts, precision parts, rifle ammo, and rarely an MK2 rifle.
* Military and armoured vehicles only show up at these spots now. If you want one, this is where you fight for it.

## Tips

* **Check your map before you go.** Green is safe. Red is not.
* **Leave your good gear at home** when you head into a red zone for the first time.
* **Fight at a distance near nests.** Throw your molotov and back off.
* **In the open world, talk first.** A good robbery is better roleplay than a bullet.
