# Staying Alive

Zombies aren't the only thing out to get you. Hunger, thirst, blood loss and broken bones will put you down just as fast. Keep an eye on your HUD and always carry the basics.

## Hunger and thirst

Your **Food** and **Water** bars on the HUD drop slowly all the time. Training at the gym uses some too. If either one hits empty, you start losing health. Eat and drink before it gets that far.

## Water

Clean water is easy to find if you know where to look.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find empty bottles

Search bins, dumpsters and vending machines. Empty bottles turn up all the time.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill them

Take them to a lake or river and fill them up, or use a **Water Well** at a base.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Or make it

Turn an empty bottle into Water at the safe-zone bench, or at a lit campfire or stove.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Loot vending machines.** Soda, water, coffee and snack machines and fridges can give you Water, Sprunk and empty bottles.
{% endhint %}

## Food

Hunting is the most reliable way to eat.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Hunt

Kill an animal, then search it with a **cutting weapon** in hand: a knife, dagger, switchblade, machete, hatchet or battle axe. You get raw meat and hide.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Light a fire

Place a **Campfire** or **Hobo stove**, fill it with wood and switch it on. It only works while it's lit.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cook

Open the fire like a bench and cook your meat. Raw meat isn't for eating. Cook it first.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

| Animal | Raw meat | Cooks into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cow | Raw Beef | Cooked Beef, or a **Burger** |
| Deer | Raw Venison | Cooked Venison |
| Pig, boar | Raw Pork | Cooked Pork |
| Chicken, birds | Raw Poultry | Cooked Poultry, or a **Chicken Sandwich** |
| Everything else | Raw Game Meat | Cooked Game Meat |

You'll also find the odd sandwich while looting bins.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Keep the hides.** Animal hide is needed for some research, and traders will buy it.
{% endhint %}

## Medical

| Item | What it does | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🩹 **Bandage** | Stops bleeding and heals you a little | Safe-zone bench, workbench |
| 💊 **Painkillers** | Dulls the pain so your injuries don't slow you down | Safe-zone bench, workbench |
| 🦴 **Splint** | Fixes injured arms and legs | Safe-zone bench, workbench |
| 🧰 **Individual First Aid Kit** | Heals a good chunk of health and works like a painkiller | Workbench |
| ⛑️ **First Aid** | Gets a downed survivor back on their feet. Use it next to them. It takes a while. | Workbench |
| 💉 **Antidote** | Clears infection from the tunnels | Found as loot, or bought from a medical trader |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**First Aid is for your friends.** It won't help you when you're down. Someone else has to use it on you, and it takes time, so make sure the area is clear first.
{% endhint %}

## Infection

The air in the subway tunnels is poison. Without a working gas mask filter, infection builds up. You start coughing, then your vision hazes and you run out of breath faster. Stay too long and it starts eating your health.

Some of it follows you back out. Only an **Antidote** clears it. See [The Subway](subway.md) for more.

## Tips

* **Never leave without water and bandages.** They're cheap, and they'll save your life.
* **Carry spare empty bottles.** Fill them at the next lake you pass.
* **Cook in bulk.** Cooked meat travels well. Do a big batch at the fire before a long trip.
* **Splint, then run.** A bad leg slows you down. Fix it before you try to outrun a horde.
* **One First Aid per group.** Make sure someone in your crew is carrying one.
