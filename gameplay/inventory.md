# Inventory & Gear

What you carry decides how long you last. Your inventory is a grid of pockets with gear slots around it: weapons, a bag, armour and the kit that unlocks your radio, your map and the tunnels.

## Opening it

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tab** | Open your inventory. |
| **K** | Open the glovebox when you sit up front in a vehicle, or the trunk of the vehicle you're looking at. |
| **1** / **2** | Draw or holster your Primary / Secondary weapon. |
| **3** to **7** | Use whatever sits in that hotbar slot. |
| **F1** | Show your hotbar. |
| **R** | Reload. |

When it opens you'll see up to three columns: **Gear** (what you're wearing), **Survivor** (your pockets) and whatever you're looking into: the ground, a trunk, a stash or a crate.

{% hint style="info" %}
All keys can be changed in the pause menu under **Keybinds**.
{% endhint %}

## Pockets and hotbar

Your **pockets** are the big grid in the middle. They show a few rows at a time. Scroll to see the rest.

Under them sit **five hotbar slots**, on keys **3** to **7**. Put food, water, meds or anything else you want fast in there. Press the key and you use it, no need to open the inventory.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**No weapons on the hotbar.** Guns, knives and grenades only come out of the weapon slots. See below.
{% endhint %}

Right-click an item for its options: **Use**, **Equip**, **Take off**, **Fit in carrier**, **Give** to the nearest player, or **Drop**. Hover over it to see its name, rarity, description, weight, amount and condition. Weapons also show their ammo and serial number.

### Rarity

Every item has a rarity colour, from common to rare:

| Rarity | |
| --- | --- |
| **Scrap** | Everyday junk. Easy to find. |
| **Salvaged** | Patched up, but works. |
| **Scarce** | Getting hard to come by. |
| **Hoarded** | Someone kept this for themselves. |
| **Relic** | From before. You won't see many. |

## Weapons

Weapons are only drawn from your two weapon slots. A gun in your pockets stays in your pockets.

| Slot | Key | What fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | **1** | Any weapon: rifles, SMGs, snipers, machine guns, launchers. Also held tools like the metal detector or a jerry can. |
| **Secondary** | **2** | Pistols, shotguns, melee weapons and throwables. |

Press the key to draw, press it again to holster. Drag a drawn weapon back to your pockets and your survivor puts it away.

## Gear slots

The **Gear** column is what you're wearing. Each slot only takes its own kind of item.

| Slot | What goes in | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎒 **Backpack** | A backpack | Opens a third box to carry more. |
| 🦺 **Carrier** | A plate carrier | Holds armour plates. |
| 🛡️ **Plates** | Armour plates | Your armour. As many as your carrier holds. |
| 📻 **Radio** | A radio | Unlocks text chat. See [Chat, Radio & Crews](communication.md). |
| 😷 **Mask** | A gas mask | Lets you breathe in the subway. See [The Subway](subway.md). |
| 🔫 **Ammo pouch** | An ammo pouch | Opens extra ammo cells. |
| 🧭 **GPS** | A GPS | Turns on your minimap. See [HUD, Map & GPS](hud-and-map.md). |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**A radio or GPS in your pockets does nothing.** It has to sit in its own gear slot.
{% endhint %}

## Backpacks

Drag a backpack into the **Backpack** slot and a third box opens next to your pockets. Take it off and the box closes. Everything inside stays in the bag.

| Backpack | Size | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Day pack** | Small | Craft it after the **Packs** research. |
| **Hiking pack** | Large | Craft it after the **Expedition Packs** research. |

* The bag has its **own weight limit**. What's inside it doesn't weigh down your pockets. Only the bag itself does.
* You can't put a backpack inside a backpack.
* When you open a trunk or stash with a bag on, all three boxes show, so you can move loot straight in and out of the bag.
* Drop your bag and the loot stays in it. Whoever picks it up and wears it gets what's inside.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Someone can take your bag.** If you're looted, they can take the whole backpack off you, contents and all.
{% endhint %}

## Ammo pouches

An ammo pouch in the **Ammo pouch** slot opens extra cells that only take ammo. When you reload, ammo in the pouch counts.

| Pouch | Cells | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small Ammo Pouch** | 1 | Craft it after the **Packs** research. |
| **Medium Ammo Pouch** | 2 | Craft it after the **Expedition Packs** research. |

Empty the pouch before you take it off or swap it. Ammo you pick up goes to your pockets, not straight into the pouch.

## Plate carriers and armour

There are no vests out here. Your armour comes from **plates** in a **plate carrier**.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Wear a carrier

Drag a carrier into the **Carrier** slot. On its own it gives no armour.

| Carrier | Plates it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Light carrier** | 2 |
| **Standard carrier** | 3 |
| **Heavy carrier** | 4 |

You can craft a Light carrier after the **Plate Carriers** research.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fit your plates

Double-click a plate, pick **Fit in carrier**, or drag it onto a plate cell. Your armour goes up on the HUD.

| Plate | |
| --- | --- |
| **Steel plate** | Heavy and cheap. The least armour. |
| **Ceramic plate** | The everyday plate. |
| **Composite plate** | Light and strong. Four of them in a heavy carrier is full armour. |
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take hits

When you're hit, your plates soak it up, one at a time. A worn plate shows an amber number in the inventory. When a plate is used up it breaks and drops out of the carrier, and the next one takes over.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Your armour lives in the plates.** Take the carrier off and your armour drops to zero, but the plates stay inside with whatever they have left. Put it back on and it comes back. Logging out, dying or changing clothes doesn't wipe your plates either.
{% endhint %}

## Carry weight

You can only carry so much. Your pockets have a weight limit, and every item counts. Heavy gear like power tools, plates and guns fills it fast. Train **Strength** to carry more. See [Skills & Gym](skills.md).

## Exposed items

Anything you pick up in the subway comes out **exposed**. You'll see hazard stripes and a yellow edge on its tile.

Exposed items:

* can't go in any gear slot: no wearing, no equipping;
* can't go in your carrier or ammo pouch, and exposed ammo can't be loaded;
* won't stack with your clean items of the same kind.

They still move freely around your pockets, hotbar and backpack. To make them safe, put them in a **decontamination locker** before you leave the tunnels. See [The Subway](subway.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Don't walk out with exposed loot.** Anything still exposed on you when you step out of the subway is taken from you.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Fill your hotbar.** Food, water and a medkit on 3 to 7 can save your life mid-fight.
* **Pistol in Secondary, rifle in Primary.** Then 1 and 2 cover you at any range.
* **Get a bag early.** The Day pack is cheap and makes every run worth more.
* **Carry spare plates.** A broken plate is gone. Have a fresh one ready to fit.
* **Craft a GPS straight away.** Without one you're running blind.
