# The Subway (Extraction)

Something is seeping up from under the city. The subway tunnels are flooded with toxic gas, packed with the dead, and stashed with gear you won't find anywhere else. Go in, grab what you can, and find a way out before the gas or the other survivors get you.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The subway is Kill On Sight.** No rules, no roleplay protection. Anyone you meet down there may shoot you for your bag.
{% endhint %}

## What you need

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 🎫 **Transit Keycard** | Opens a station door. Used up when you swipe. Only found in the **red zones**, mostly on tougher zombies and bosses. |
| 😷 **Gas Mask** | Must be **worn in your Mask slot**. No mask, no entry. |
| 🧪 **Gas Filters** | One is used when you go in. Bring spares: each one only lasts so long. |
| 💊 **Antidote** | Not needed to get in, but you'll want it after. See **Infection** below. |

## Getting in

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find an open entrance

Only a few stations are open at a time, and they change every restart. Open entrances show on your map. The tunnels themselves show too, as a dark red band under the roads, in the **Danger** section of the legend.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Swipe your keycard

Stand at the door with your mask on and press **\[E] Swipe keycard**. Your keycard and one filter are used, and the door opens. Your run has started.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Bring your crew

For a few seconds after you swipe, crewmates standing at the door can press **\[E] Follow your crew in**. They don't need a keycard, but each one needs their own mask and uses one of their own filters.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Inside

Once you're through the door, a card appears on your HUD. Keep an eye on it.

| On the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Run time** | How long you have left before the gas gets you, filter or not. |
| **Filter** | How long your current filter has left. It turns red when it's nearly done. |
| **Spares** | How many filters you're carrying. |
| **Exposed** | How many marked items you're carrying. See **Found items are marked** below. |
| **Open exits** | The stations you can leave from this run. |

### No signal

You're underground. Chat doesn't work down here, only commands. Your **radio voice still works**, so bring one if you want to talk to your crew.

### Swapping filters

Your filter only runs down while your mask is on. When it's getting low, **use** a gas filter from your inventory. It takes a few seconds, so find a quiet spot first.

### Tougher dead

The zombies down here are a lot harder to put down than the ones on the surface. Bring more ammo than you think you need.

## Infection

If your mask is off, your filter runs out, or you stay past your run time, the gas gets in and your **infection** starts to climb.

| As it gets worse | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Early** | You start coughing and everything goes hazy. |
| **Worse** | Your stamina drops. |
| **Bad** | You start losing health, and the edges of your vision blur. |
| **Full** | You die. |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Infection doesn't wear off.** It stays with you after you leave the tunnels, until you take an **Antidote**. Antidotes are found in the red zones, or crafted once you've researched Field Medicine.
{% endhint %}

## Loot

Search the **crates** scattered through the tunnels, and the zombies you put down. This is the only place in the city for the heaviest gear:

* **Explosives:** grenades, pipe bombs, sticky bombs and mines.
* **Heavy weapons:** snipers, machine guns, launchers and more.
* **The best scopes.**

### Found items are marked

Anything you pick up inside, from a crate, a body, the floor or another player, is **marked as exposed**. You'll see hazard stripes on it in your inventory.

* **You can carry it** in your pockets and backpack.
* **You can't wear or equip it.** A gun you find can't go in your weapon slot, and marked ammo can't be loaded. You can't use found gear down here.
* **It won't stack** with your own clean items. Put it in an empty slot.

To keep marked items, you have to **decontaminate** them on the way out.

## Getting out

You can only leave through one of **this run's exits**. They're on your HUD card and posted in chat when you go in. The station you came in by is never one of them. Any other station you find is sealed.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Use the decontamination locker

Inside your exit station, press **\[E] Decontamination locker**. It's your own private locker. Put every marked item you want to keep in it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the exit

Press **\[E] Open exit**. If you're still carrying marked items, you'll get a warning listing them. Choose **Go back** to sort them out, or **Leave anyway**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hold the door

An alarm goes off while the door opens, and **every zombie nearby hears it**. Stay close to the door. If you wander off, it stops and you have to start again.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Step outside

The door opens. Walk out, and you've made it. You earn XP for getting out alive, and a bonus when your crewmates get out with you.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Collect your loot

At the **Collection locker** outside that same station, pick up everything you put in the decontamination locker. It's clean now, and yours to use.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Marked items you carry out are lost.** Anything still marked when you step outside stays behind. Always use the decontamination locker first.
{% endhint %}

### What you keep

* ✅ **Your own gear** that you went in with: weapons, backpack, armour, everything you had equipped.
* ✅ **Clean items** in your pockets and backpack.
* ✅ **Anything you put through decontamination**, from the collection locker.
* ❌ **Marked items still on you** when you step out.

### Other ways out

Leaving the tunnels any other way doesn't count as extracting. If you end up outside without using an exit, every marked item on you is destroyed.

Logging out inside is risky too. Come back in time and your run carries on. Take too long, and your marked items are gone.

## Dying down there

If you die in the tunnels, everything you carry drops on your body, and **anyone can search it**: your crew, or whoever killed you.

## Tips

* **Count your filters.** Running out halfway back to an exit is how most runs end.
* **Go with a crew.** One swipe gets everyone in, and you get bonus XP for getting out together.
* **Know your exits before you loot.** Don't wander so deep you can't get back out in time.
* **Don't get greedy.** A run where you get out with half a bag beats a run where you die with a full one.
* **Bring an antidote,** or have one waiting at home.
