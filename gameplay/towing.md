# Towing

Not every wreck is in a good spot to fix. With a flatbed tow truck, you can winch a vehicle onto the bed and haul it somewhere safer, like your base or a garage.

Anyone can tow. You don't need a job or any special items, just a working tow truck. You'll find them the same way as any other vehicle: as wrecks you restore.

## Controls

The prompts on the right of your screen only show what you can do right now.

| Key | What it does | Where you stand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **↓** | Roll the bed down (press again to stop) | Driver's seat |
| **↑** | Roll the bed up (press again to stop) | Driver's seat |
| **E** | Take, attach, drop or store the hook | Top of the bed, the vehicle's front, or the rear wheels |
| **←** (hold) | Wind the winch | Rear wheels or driver's seat |
| **→** (hold) | Unwind the winch | Rear wheels or driver's seat |
| **Alt** | Attach the vehicle to the bed now | Rear wheels |
| **Backspace** | Detach the vehicle | Rear wheels |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Alt also opens your third eye.** If that gets in the way, change "Tow truck: attach the vehicle" in Key Bindings.
{% endhint %}

## Loading a vehicle

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Line up

Park the truck in front of the vehicle you want, with your back end facing it. The straighter you line up, the easier this goes.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Roll the bed down

From the driver's seat, press **↓**. The bed rolls all the way down by itself. Trucks with a fixed bed skip this.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take the hook

Walk to the top of the bed and press **E**. The hook is in your hand, with a cable back to the winch. The truck won't move until the job is done.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hook the vehicle

Walk to the front of the vehicle and press **E** to attach the hook. You'll see **Vehicle hooked**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wind it in

Stand at the truck's rear wheels, or sit in the driver's seat, and hold **←**. The vehicle rolls up the bed, and its front wheels steer themselves to line it up.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### It locks on

When it reaches its place on the bed, it's attached by itself: **Vehicle attached**. Want it to stop sooner? Press **Alt** at the rear wheels.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Store the hook and go

Press **E** at the rear wheels to put the hook away. Get in, press **↑** to roll the bed up, and drive. The vehicle rides with you.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**The cable only reaches so far.** Walk too far with the hook, and you'll hear "The winch won't reach any further" as it winds back. Get the truck closer.
{% endhint %}

## Unloading

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Roll the bed down

Park where you want it, and press **↓** from the driver's seat.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hook it and let it go

Take the hook, attach it to the vehicle, then press **Backspace** at the rear wheels to detach it from the bed.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Let it roll

Hold **→**. The winch pays out and pushes the vehicle off the bed.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Tidy up

Press **E** at the vehicle to take the hook off, then store it at the top of the bed. The truck is free to move again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Tips

* **Don't need the hook yet?** Press **E** away from any vehicle to drop it on the ground. Press **E** next to it to pick it back up.
* **Rolling beds work from the driver's seat only.** You can't move them from outside.
* **Bring a lookout.** Loading takes time, and you can't drive off while the hook is out.
* **Towed vehicles stay where you leave them.** Drop a wreck at your base and fix it in peace.
