# Skills & Gym

The world ended, so nobody is going to hand you anything. But you can make yourself harder to kill. You have six **skills**. Each one gets better the more you train it, and each level gives you a small, real edge.

## The skills

| Skill | What it gives you | How to train it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💪 **Strength** | Carry more weight | Weight bench, pull-up bar, squat rack, dumbbell rack, push-ups |
| 🏃 **Condition** | More stamina, and it drains slower while you sprint | Treadmill, sprinting |
| 🫁 **Lung capacity** | Stay under water longer | Swimming under water |
| 🎯 **Shooting** | Less recoil | Shooting the target dummy |
| 🥊 **Toughness** | Hit harder in melee, and take less melee damage | Punching bag |
| 🤸 **Agility** | Take less fall damage | Yoga mat, sit-ups |

Each skill has many levels. The higher you go, the bigger the bonus.

## Training

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get the equipment

Research **Fitness** and craft the gym gear at your workbench. Then place it at your base. See [Base Building](base-building.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Third-eye it

Look at the equipment and third-eye it to start your workout. A training bar shows above your HUD with your skill, your reps and your energy.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Keep going

Every rep earns xp for that skill. When a skill goes up a level, you get a message.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Stop when you want

Press **E** to stop. Moving away, getting in a vehicle, getting knocked over or dying also ends your workout.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### No gym? No excuse

You can train some skills without any equipment:

* **`/pushups`** trains Strength.
* **`/situps`** trains Agility.
* **Sprinting** trains Condition.
* **Swimming under water** trains Lung capacity.

| Dumbbell Rack | Strength |
| Treadmill | Condition |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Old gyms are worth a visit.** Gym equipment you find out in the world can't be trained on, but you can third-eye it and strip it for materials.
{% endhint %}

## Energy, food and water

* **Energy.** Every rep uses some. Run out and your workout ends. You have to rest a while before you can start again.
* **Food and water.** Every finished set costs a little of both. If you're too hungry or thirsty, you can't train at all. See [Staying Alive](survival.md).

## Use it or lose it

Skills **fade** if you stop training. After a few days without training, a skill starts slowly dropping. It never falls below a certain level, so the basics stay with you.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Keep your best skills ticking over.** A short session every few days is enough to stop them fading.
{% endhint %}

## The Skills page

Press **Esc** to open the pause menu and choose **Skills**. You'll see a card for each skill showing:

* your **level**
* where to **train** it
* whether it's **fading**

## Tips

* **Strength first.** More carry weight helps with everything: gathering, looting and hauling building materials.
* **Eat before you train.** You can't train hungry, and every set eats into your food and water.
* **Sprint everywhere.** Condition trains just from running, so your loot runs count too.
