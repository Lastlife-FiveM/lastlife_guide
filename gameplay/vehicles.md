# Vehicles & Garages

Nobody hands you a car out here. Every working vehicle on the road was a wreck once, until a survivor brought it back to life. You'll start on two wheels and work your way up.

## Your first ride

You spawn with a **BMX**. **Use** it from your inventory to put it down, and third-eye it to **Get this Bicycle** when you're done. It goes back in your bag.

When you're ready for an engine, craft a **50cc Engine** at the safe-zone bench, then turn your BMX and that engine into a **Motorized Bike**. It needs no keys, and it works just like the BMX: put it down, ride, pick it back up.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Pick your bike up.** A bike left lying in the world isn't kept. Put it back in your bag before you log off.
{% endhint %}

## Finding a vehicle

Every restart, broken-down vehicles appear around the map: car parks, roadsides, airfields and work yards. Most are everyday junk. Now and then you'll find something faster.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find a wreck

Look for abandoned vehicles that won't start. Third-eye one to see what it needs.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Gather the parts

The **Restore Vehicle** menu lists every part it needs. Vehicle parts, engine parts, body panels, oil, a battery: whatever that vehicle takes. Search other cars, trash and workshops to find them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Restore it

With every part in your inventory, restore it. Your survivor gets to work, the parts are used up, and **the vehicle is yours**. You can drive it straight away.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Bigger means pricier.** Faster and heavier vehicles need more parts. Armoured ones need special parts on top, like vehicle armour, and helicopters need aviation parts.
{% endhint %}

### High-tier vehicles

Armed, armoured and military vehicles never turn up at ordinary spots. They only appear at a few of the most dangerous places on the map, guarded by armed men who shoot first. Go in with a crew, or don't go at all.

### Boats and helicopters

* **Boats** spawn out on the water each restart, off the coast and on the Alamo Sea. Swim out and restore them where they float.
* **Helicopters** turn up now and then at airfields. They need aviation parts to restore.

## Keys and access

There are no keys to lose. A vehicle knows who owns it.

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| 🔑 **You (the owner)** | Drive it, lock and unlock it. |
| 👥 **Your crew** | Drive and lock it, if their crew rank lets them drive crew vehicles. |
| 🤝 **Someone you trust** | Use `/givekeys` while you're in your vehicle to let a player drive it. You stay the owner. |
| 🚫 **Everyone else** | Can't drive it. |

Lock and unlock your vehicle with **L** (you can change it in Key Bindings).

### Stealing

Someone else's vehicle is fair game, if you've got the tools.

* **Locked doors:** third-eye the vehicle with a **lockpick** to pop them open.
* **Taking it:** sit inside with a lockpick and use it to open the **Steal Vehicle** menu. It lists what you need, usually cables. Armoured and military vehicles need a lot more, like a blowtorch.

Once you steal it, it's **yours**, and the old owner loses it.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The same goes for you.** Anyone with the right tools can take your vehicle. Park it in a garage when you're not using it.
{% endhint %}

## Keeping it running

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| ⛽ **Low fuel** | See **Fuel** below. |
| 🛞 **Burst tyre** | Carry a spare tyre for that kind of vehicle and third-eye the wheel. |
| 💥 **Dead or blown-up engine** | Third-eye it with a **repair kit**. Don't wait too long: a destroyed vehicle that's left too long is gone for good. |

### Fuel

Gas stations don't sell fuel any more. You have to scavenge it.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find a jerry can

Search cars and you'll sometimes find one, usually nearly empty.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find gasoline

Third-eye the old **fuel pumps** at gas stations to search them for **Gasoline**. Car searches turn some up too.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill the can

Hold the jerry can in your hands, then **use** the Gasoline from your inventory. It pours into the can.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill the tank

With the can in your hands, stand by the vehicle's fuel cap and press **E**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**A damaged tank leaks.** If the fuel tank takes a beating, you'll burn through fuel faster.
{% endhint %}

## Garages

There's a limit on how many vehicles you can have out in the world at once. Vehicles in a garage don't count, so park what you aren't using.

There are three kinds of garage, and each only takes its own kind of vehicle:

| Garage | Takes | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🚗 **Garage** (a shipping container) | Cars, bikes and trucks | Around the map, plus the **Base Garage** you can build |
| 🚁 **Helipad** | Helicopters | Around the map, plus the **Base Helipad** you can build |
| ⚓ **Dock** | Boats | On the coast. You can't build these. |

The public ones are on your map under **Garages**.

### Parking

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Pull into a spot

Drive into one of the garage's parking spots. As the driver, you'll see **\[E] Park vehicle**. Helicopters have to land first.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Press E

A quick fade, and the vehicle is parked. You're left standing on the spot.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

You can't park a wreck (repair it first), a vehicle that isn't yours, or in a spot that's already taken.

### Taking it out

Walk up to the garage. A screen floats above it showing its name and how many spots are free. Press **E**, or third-eye the garage, to open the menu.

Pick your vehicle and press **Pull out**. It comes out on the nearest free spot, with the same fuel and damage you left it with.

* **It comes out where you parked it.** Is your vehicle in another garage? It shows under **Parked elsewhere**. Press **Bring here** to move it over, for free.
* **Kinds don't mix.** A boat can't be brought to a container, and a car can't be brought to a dock.

### Docks

Boats work a little differently, because the spots are out on the water.

* **Parking:** get your boat near the spot. You've got plenty of room, since boats drift. Press **E**, and you'll be put back on the dock.
* **Taking out:** the boat comes out on the water and you're put straight in the driver's seat.

### Base Garage and Base Helipad

Research unlocks a **Base Garage** (Comforts) and later a **Base Helipad** (Industrial). Craft one and place it at your base. A marker shows where vehicles park and come out, and it has to be clear to use it.

* **Your crew can use it**, but everyone only sees their own vehicles.
* **If it gets destroyed, your vehicles aren't lost.** They show up under Parked elsewhere at any other garage of the same kind. Use Bring here to get them back.

## Tips

* **Start small.** Get a beater running before you chase anything fast. You'll learn what parts to hoard.
* **Hoard car parts.** Vehicle parts, cables and tyres are useful long after your first car.
* **Always carry fuel.** A full jerry can in the boot can save a long walk home.
* **Park it or lose it.** A vehicle left out in the open is a gift to any thief with a lockpick.
* **Need to move a wreck?** See [Towing](towing.md).
