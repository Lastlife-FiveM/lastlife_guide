# Base Building

Safe zones keep you alive, but they aren't home. A base is where you store your loot, run your benches, train and sleep. Here's how to build one that lasts.

## Getting started

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Craft a Totem

Make a **Totem** at the safe-zone bench. You can **only build inside a totem's zone**, so this always comes first.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Plant it

Pick your spot and **use** the Totem from your inventory to place it. It claims the ground around it as your build zone. Zombies won't spawn close to it either.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Place a Crafting Table

Craft a **Crafting Table** at the safe-zone bench and place it inside your zone. This is your workbench. Every base piece is made here.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Craft pieces, then place them

Every wall, floor and door is an item. Craft it at your workbench, then **use** it from your inventory to place it. The materials are paid when you craft, not when you place.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Changed your mind?** Pick up a piece while it's undamaged and you get the item back.
{% endhint %}

## Building tiers

Pieces come in three materials. The higher the tier, the tougher the wall, and the more work it takes.

| Tier | What it costs | How to unlock it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🪵 **Wood** | Logs for walls and foundations. Planks for floors, roofs, stairs and doors. Nails hold it together. | Known from the start |
| 🪨 **Stone** | Stone and **mortar**. Stone doors and gates use iron, planks and hinges. | **Stonework** research |
| 🔩 **Metal** | Iron ingots, steel and **metal fittings** | **Metalwork** research |

Each tier has the full set: foundations, walls, small and triangle walls, pillars, floors, roofs, stairs, ladders, window frames, door frames, gate frames, doors and gates. Wood also has big walls and big gates.

You can **upgrade** a piece in place instead of tearing it down. Third-eye it and pick the upgrade option. Going up to stone needs mortar, and going up to metal needs fittings.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Mortar and fittings need their blueprint.** You make mortar at a workbench with the Stonework blueprint in it, and fittings at one with the Metalwork blueprint. Steel comes out of the smelter. See [Crafting & Research](crafting-research.md).
{% endhint %}

## Doors, gates and locks

* **Hinges.** Wooden doors and gates hang on **Crude Hinges**. You can craft them at the workbench from the start.
* **Locked by default.** A door you place is locked to everyone except you and your crew. Open and close doors and gates with **E**.
* **Code locks.** Craft a **Code Lock** and fit it to a door or storage box, then set a code. Anyone who knows the code can get in. Guessers only get so many tries.

## Storage

| Box | How to unlock it |
| --- | --- |
| **Wood Storage** | Known from the start |
| **Metal Storage Lv2** | Stonework |
| **Metal Storage** | Metalwork |

Like doors, storage is locked to everyone except you and your crew when you place it.

## Upkeep: keep your base standing

Your base **wears down over time**. Leave it alone long enough and it will fall apart.

**Upkeep boxes** stop that. Place one near your pieces and stock it with building materials: logs, stone, iron, planks, nails, hinges, mortar, steel, fittings and glass. It uses them to repair everything within range, all on its own.

| Box | How to unlock it |
| --- | --- |
| **Upkeep Lv1** | Known from the start |
| **Upkeep Lv2** | Stonework |
| **Upkeep Lv3** | Industrial |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**An empty upkeep box does nothing.** Top it up every time you come home. Stone and metal bases need stone and metal materials to repair, not just wood.
{% endhint %}

You can also repair a damaged piece yourself. Third-eye it and pick the repair option. It costs the piece's basic material.

## Power

Lights, turrets and electric fences need power. You build your own grid.

| Part | What it does | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small and medium generators** | Make power. Burn fuel. | Comforts |
| **Big generator** | Makes a lot of power. Burns fuel. | Industrial |
| **Solar panel** | Free power. Strongest in daylight and clear weather. | Electricity |
| **Big solar panel**, **Wind turbine** | More free power | Industrial |
| **Battery pack** | Stores power | Electricity |
| **Power distributor, combiner, switch**, **Power line** | Split, join, switch and carry power around your base | Electricity |
| **Lights**: worklights, wall, ceiling and exterior lights | Light up the night | Comforts (Big light: Electricity) |

Connect everything with wire. Wire is made of **Cables**, and longer runs use more of them.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Cables are heavy.** A long wire run eats a lot of cables. Plan your layout so power doesn't have to travel far.
{% endhint %}

## Everything else for your base

* **Water Well.** Known from the start. Third-eye it to fill empty bottles with clean water. See [Staying Alive](survival.md).
* **Campfire and Hobo stove.** Cooking. Both burn wood.
* **Smelter.** Turns ore into ingots. Burns coal.
* **Weapons table and Recycler.** See [Crafting & Research](crafting-research.md).
* **Bed and sofa.** Unlocked with Comforts.
* **Base garage and helipad.** Store your vehicles and aircraft. Comforts gives the garage, Industrial gives the helipad.
* **Gym equipment.** Train your skills at home. Unlocked with Fitness. See [Skills & Gym](skills.md).
* **Defences.** Spike walls, an electric fence and turrets on stands. Turrets need power and ammo.

## Raids

Other survivors can break into your base, so build like they will. When your base takes damage, you get a warning in game.

Bases are protected while everyone who owns them is offline, but not straight away after the last person logs off. If a raid has already started, logging off won't save you.

## Tips

* **Totem first, always.** Nothing goes up without one.
* **Start with wood, upgrade later.** Wood is cheap and quick. Upgrade the walls that matter once you've unlocked stone.
* **Lock everything.** Doors, storage, and the room with your workbench, because your blueprints are in it.
* **Stock the upkeep box before a long trip.** Come back to a base, not a pile of rubble.
* **Build with your crew.** Crewmates can open your doors and help you build, depending on what you let them do.
