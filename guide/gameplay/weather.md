# Weather

Weather and time are the same for everyone, so if you're in a storm, so is the survivor hunting you.

## The clock

One in-game hour takes **4 minutes**, so a whole day is about **96 minutes**. The dead are at their worst from **20:00 to 06:00**. See [Zombies](zombies.md).

## What you'll see

| Weather | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Clear and sunny** | The most common. Long sight lines for you, and for anyone with a scope. |
| **Clouds, overcast, smog** | Greyer, a bit harder to see far. |
| **Fog** | Our own thick fog rolls in. See below. |
| **Rain** | Stays away for about the first hour after a restart, and you usually get a long dry spell between two bouts of it. |
| **Thunderstorms** | Rain, thunder and strong wind. |
| **Snow** | Only in December. |

It usually changes every few minutes up to every half hour, and one kind blends into the next.

## Fog

When the weather turns foggy, dense fog rolls in over a few seconds.

* **By day** it's a light haze.
* **From 19:00** it starts to thicken, and it's at its heaviest from **21:00 to 05:00**.
* It clears back to daytime levels by **07:00**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A foggy night is the worst time to be lost. You can't see the dead, but they can still hear you.
{% endhint %}

## Dark nights

There's no working power grid unless survivors keep it running. Parts of the map have **no lights at all** until the generators at Palmer-Taylor are feeding them: no street lamps, no lit windows. Out at sea it's always dark.

Vehicle headlights and flashlights always work. See [Power](power.md) for how to get the lights back.

## Tips

* **Carry a flashlight.** Every night, not just the bad ones.
* **Use the fog.** It hides you as well as it hides them.
* **Move loot by day** if you can.
