# Parkour

Press **J** (default) to switch parkour on or off. A score plate shows at the top of your screen while it's on.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Empty hands only.** Draw a weapon and your normal controls come back. Getting in a vehicle or going down switches it off.
{% endhint %}

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Left mouse** (hold) at a wall | Vault, quick climb or wall run |
| **Space**, let go at a low wall | Quick climb |
| **Q** at a wall in front | Wall flip |
| **Left mouse + Q** beside a wall | Tic-tac or side wall run |
| **Q** during a vault or trick | Extra distance |
| **Space + Q**, standing still | Press |
| **Space + W / A / D / S** | Front flip, side flip, gainer, cork |
| Keep holding the direction | A double, if there's room |
| **Space + left mouse + direction** | Pike or double-leg version |
| **Right mouse** at an edge | A big trick off the drop |
| **F** on flat ground | Tricking combo |
| **F / Space / R / X** in a combo | Arabian, back full, fly, back steps |
| **C** while running | Slide |
| **C** during a trick | Land in a press |
| **R** as you land | Roll |
| **G** | Speed boost, when the bar is full |

### Hanging on a ledge

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Shift** | Climb up |
| **Space** | Push up |
| **A / D** | Move along |
| **R** | Backflip off |
| **F** | Front flip off |
| **Q** | Leap away |
| **G** | Let go |

Near a vehicle, **F** still means get in.

## Stamina

Every move costs stamina, from 4 for a slide to 18 for a big trick. Under 10 you have to catch your breath.

## Landing

* Fall more than **7 metres** without a roll and you go down.
* A good **roll** or press landing takes away **half** the fall damage.
* It doesn't make you immortal. Above 15 metres you fall like anyone else.

## Score and boost

Moves earn score. **500** fills the boost bar: press **G** to run 25% faster for 3.5 seconds. Falling over costs score.

Your score also trains **Agility**: 10 XP for every 1,000 score, up to 20 XP a minute. See [Skills & Gym](skills.md).

{% hint style="success" %}
**Practise somewhere safe.** The middle of a horde is a bad place to learn the backflip.
{% endhint %}
