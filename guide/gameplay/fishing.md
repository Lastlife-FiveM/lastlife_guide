# Fishing

The dead don't swim, and the water is still full of life. A rod and a little patience will keep you fed, and the big ones are worth real chits on Elysian Island.

## How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Rig your rod

A bare rod catches nothing. **Right-click** it in your inventory and pick **Rig**. It needs a **line**, a **hook** and **bait or a lure**. A **weight or float** is optional and decides how deep you fish.

Click a piece of tackle to put it on. Click a slot on the rod to take it off again. The window tells you in plain words what the rig is good for. A rod holds up to **12** bait.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take it in your hands

**Use** the rod to take it out. Use it again to put it away.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cast

Face the water. When you're close enough a **Cast** prompt appears. **Hold E**: the longer you hold, the further it flies. Piers, bridges and cliffs are fine, and so is a seat in a boat.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait for the bite

Watch your float. When it bites, hit **E** fast: you have about a second and a half. Too slow and your bait is gone.

**X** (default) reels the line in early.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fight it

**Hold Space** (default) to reel. **Let go** to ease off. Keep the marker out of the **slack** on the left and out of the **red** on the right.

* Too long in the slack and it gets away.
* Too long in the red and your **line snaps**. The hook, weight and lure go with it.

When the fish **runs**, ease off. When it **tires**, reel.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Land it

Bring the line all the way in and it's yours. You won't know what you hooked until it's on the bank.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Nothing biting?** Something about your rig is wrong for this water. Change the bait, the hook size or the depth, or come back at another time of day.
{% endhint %}

## What swims where

There are **28** kinds to catch, from scrap to relic rarity. The records book lists them all.

| Water | Where | Common | For the patient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🏞️ **Freshwater** | Lakes and rivers inland | Bluegill, carp, catfish, largemouth bass, rainbow trout | Pike, salmon, sturgeon, golden trout |
| 🌊 **Coast** | Beaches, piers, harbours | Sardine, mackerel, flounder, surfperch, striped bass, redfish, squid | Halibut, stingray, leopard shark |
| 🚤 **Deep sea** | Far from shore, by boat | Cod, bonito, yellowtail, mahi-mahi | Tuna, swordfish, mako shark, marlin, oarfish |

Some fish bite better at a certain hour. A few **only** come at a certain hour, or in rain or a storm.

## Tackle

| Piece | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Line** | Twine holds **3 kg**, mono **10 kg**, braided **600 kg**. Better line also makes the fight more forgiving. |
| **Hook** | Small hooks for small mouths, big hooks for big ones. The wrong size never bites. |
| **Float or weight** | A float fishes the surface, split shot mid water, a heavy sinker the bottom. Nothing on it means mid water. |
| **Bait** | Worms, insects, cut bait, shrimp. Used up one at a time. Every fish has its favourites. |
| **Lure** | Spinner, spoon, shrimp lure, deep jig. Never used up, but lost if the line snaps. |

Keep it all in a **Tackle Box** (20 slots, up to 6 kg). It only holds fishing gear, and your rod pulls from it when you rig.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Re-rig the same** puts your last setup back on in one click, straight from your tackle box. Handy after a snapped line.
{% endhint %}

Worms and insects turn up when you [chop trees](gathering.md). Shrimp comes from traps and nets in salt water.

The weaker of your rod and your line decides what you can land. If you want the monsters, you need both. Salt on Elysian Island sometimes sells the two better rods.

## Other ways to catch

* **Fish Trap:** use it at the water's edge. It takes one small fish or bait every **20 minutes**, up to **4**. You can have **3** out. Hold **E** to check it. Anyone can empty your trap, but only you can pick it up. Forgotten for **48 hours**, it's gone.
* **Cast Net:** use it at the shore or from a boat for up to **4** small fish at once. Wait 20 seconds between throws. It falls apart after **25** throws. Never anything rare.

## What to do with your catch

* **Fillet it.** Right-click a fish. You get about **2 fillets a kilo** (40 at most), and scraps for the dog. Cook the fillets before you eat them. Oily fish give **fish oil** too.
* **Cut it into bait.** Any fish up to **2.5 kg** can become cut bait, 10 pieces at most.
* **Sell it.** Walk up to **Rook** on Elysian Island and **hold E** on **Sell your catch**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Sell your catch** sells every fish in your pockets. Cut up the ones you want to keep first.
{% endhint %}

| Rarity | Rook pays | Small one | Big one | Trophy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scrap | 2 chits | x0.7 | x1.5 | x2.5 |
| Salvaged | 6 | x0.7 | x1.5 | x2.5 |
| Scarce | 14 | x0.7 | x1.5 | x2.5 |
| Hoarded | 35 | x0.7 | x1.5 | x2.5 |
| Relic | 90 | x0.7 | x1.5 | x2.5 |

Size is judged against its own kind: a trophy sardine is still a trophy.

## Records and tournaments

Rook keeps **the book** (prompt: **Fishing records**): the biggest of every kind ever landed, your own personal bests, and the top anglers. Go and see whose name is on it.

Now and then there's a **fishing tournament**. You'll get a notice when one starts, and the standings are in the book while it runs.

## The Fishing skill

Landing fish raises your **Fishing** [skill](skills.md). At the top level: bites come **30%** faster, fights are **40%** more forgiving, your odds of something rare are **60%** better, and each piece of tackle has a **50%** chance to survive a snapped line. Lower levels give an even share.

Every fish you land also gives 10 [rank](ranks.md) XP, up to 40 a minute.

## Tips

* **Time and weather matter.** Dawn and dusk are good. Rain is good.
* **Match the bait.** If the small ones keep stealing your bait, you're fishing for the wrong thing.
* **Watch your back.** The water is quiet. The shore isn't always.
