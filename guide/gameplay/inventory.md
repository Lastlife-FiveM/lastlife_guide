# Inventory & Gear

What you carry decides how long you last. Your inventory is a grid of pockets with gear slots around it: weapons, a bag, armour and the kit that unlocks your radio and your map.

## Opening it

| Key (default) | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tab** | Open your inventory. In a vehicle's front seat it opens with the glovebox |
| **K** | Open the trunk of the vehicle you look at, or the glovebox |
| **1** / **2** | Draw or holster your Primary / Secondary weapon |
| **3** to **7** | Use what sits in that hotbar slot |
| **F1** | Show your hotbar |
| **R** | Reload |

You see up to three columns: **Gear** (what you're wearing), **Survivor** (your pockets) and whatever you're looking into.

## Pockets and hotbar

You have **50 pocket slots**. Scroll to see them all. Under them sit **five hotbar slots** on keys **3** to **7**. Put food, water and meds there.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**No weapons on the hotbar.** Guns, knives and grenades only come out of the two weapon slots.
{% endhint %}

Right-click an item for its options. Hover over it for its name, rarity, weight and condition. What the rarity colours mean is on [Loot & Rarity](loot-rarity.md).

## Weapon slots

| Slot | Key | What fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | **1** | Any weapon |
| **Secondary** | **2** | Pistols, shotguns, melee weapons and throwables |

Press the key to draw, press it again to holster.

## Gear slots

Each slot only takes its own kind of item.

| Slot | What goes in | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎒 **Back** | A backpack or a scuba tank | A backpack opens a third box. The tank is for [diving](diving.md) |
| 🦺 **Carrier** | A plate carrier | Holds armour plates |
| 🛡️ **Plates** | Armour plates | Your armour |
| 📻 **Radio** | A radio | Unlocks text chat. See [Chat & Radio](communication.md) |
| 😷 **Mask** | A gas mask or dive mask | For the [subway](subway.md) or under water |
| 🔫 **Ammo pouch** | An ammo pouch | Opens extra ammo cells |
| 🧭 **GPS** | A GPS | Turns on your [minimap](hud-and-map.md) |
| 🥾 **Shoes** | Flippers | Faster in the water, clumsy on land |

There are also slots for a helmet, eyes, top, bottom and gloves. Your normal clothes don't go in there. Those are part of [your look](character.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
**A radio or GPS in your pockets does nothing.** It has to sit in its own slot.
{% endhint %}

## Backpacks

Wear a backpack and a third box opens next to your pockets. Take it off and everything inside stays in the bag.

| Backpack | Slots | Holds | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Day pack** | 10 | 10 kg | **Packs** research, then 6 cloth, 1 hide, 2 duct tape |
| **Hiking pack** | 20 | 25 kg | **Expedition Packs** research, then 10 cloth, 3 hide, 2 duct tape, 2 aluminium |

* What's inside has its **own weight limit** and doesn't weigh down your pockets.
* You can't put a backpack inside a backpack.
* Whoever picks up your bag gets what's in it.

## Ammo pouches

Extra cells that only take ammo. When you reload, ammo in the pouch counts.

| Pouch | Cells | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small Ammo Pouch** | 1 | **Pouches** research, then 3 cloth, 1 hide |
| **Medium Ammo Pouch** | 2 | **Expedition Packs** research, then 4 cloth, 2 hide, 1 duct tape |

Empty the pouch before you take it off.

## Armour

Armour comes from **plates** in a **plate carrier**. A carrier alone gives nothing.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Wear a carrier

Craft a **Light carrier** after the **Plate Carriers** research. It holds **2 plates**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fit your plates

Double-click a plate, pick **Fit in carrier**, or drag it onto a plate cell. A fresh **Ceramic plate** gives **20 armour**. Some [traders](traders.md) deal in them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take hits

Your plates soak up damage one at a time. A used-up plate breaks, and the next one takes over.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Your armour lives in the plates.** Take the carrier off and your armour drops to zero, but the plates keep what they have left.
{% endhint %}

## Carry weight

Your pockets have a weight limit. Train **Strength** to carry up to **15 kg** more. See [Skills](skills.md).

## Weapons on your body

Guns, melee weapons and tools you carry show on your body: long guns on your back, pistols on your hip, blades on your belt. Attachments show too. They're hidden in cars and aircraft, but show on bikes, quads and boats.

Type `/weaponposition` to make it yours:

* Move each item a little and turn it however you like.
* Move it to another spot: back, hips, thighs, belt or chest.
* Switch **tools** off your body. Weapons always show, except the jerry can.

**W** moves, **E** rotates, **Shift** is for fine steps, **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels.

## Weapon charms

A charm hangs off your weapon, swings as you move and shows on your back too. The bell and the dog tags jingle when you sprint. It's only for looks.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find one

They turn up in loot, and some traders sell them: Bottle Cap Bundle, Pocket Teddy, Lucky Die, Rabbit's Foot, Spoon Lure, Brass Bell, Dog Tags and more.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Clip it on

Hold a weapon and **use** the charm. Place it, press **Space** to test the swing and **Enter** to save. The spot is kept for every weapon of that type.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Move it later

Type `/charmposition` with the weapon in your hands.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

One charm per weapon. They don't fit throwables.

## Exposed items

Anything you pick up in the subway comes out **exposed**, with a yellow edge. Exposed items can't be worn, loaded or stacked with clean ones. Put them in a **decontamination locker** before you leave. See [The Subway](subway.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Don't walk out with exposed loot.** Anything still exposed when you step out is gone.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Fill your hotbar.** A bandage on 3 can save your life mid-fight.
* **Pistol in Secondary, rifle in Primary.** Then 1 and 2 cover you at any range.
* **Get a bag early.** It makes every run worth more.
* **Carry a spare plate.** A broken one is gone.
