# Base Building

Safe zones keep you alive, but they aren't home. A base is where you store your loot, run your benches, train and sleep.

## Getting started

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Craft a Build Totem

**40 wood and 4 nails** at a safe-zone bench or a workbench. You can **only build inside a totem's area**, so this always comes first.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Plant it

**Use** the totem from your inventory and place it. Each survivor can own **one**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Place a Crafting Table

**20 wood and 2 nails**. Placed, it's your workbench, where every base piece is made.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Craft pieces, then place them

Every wall, floor and door is an item. Craft it, then **use** it to place it. You pay when you craft, not when you place.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Your build area

| What the totem does | How far |
| --- | --- |
| You can build | 150 m in every direction |
| Zombies don't spawn | 150 m |
| Bins, cars and cupboards can't be searched | 150 m (bodies still can) |

Walk near the edge and you'll see a **faint white outline on the ground**, from about 40 metres away. Only you and your crew see it. If you and a crewmate both have a totem, overlapping areas show as one shape, and you can build in both if your rank lets you build.

Claimed ground keeps strangers out: you can't build right next to someone else's base, and they can't build next to yours.

### Placing a piece

| Key (default) | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **← →** | Rotate |
| **Mouse wheel** | Closer or further away |
| **Shift** | Snap to other pieces |
| **Z** | Snap to the ground |
| **Left click** or **Enter** | Place |
| **Backspace** | Cancel |

After placing one, you're holding the next. Pieces go through bushes and plants while you place them.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Changed your mind?** Take a piece down while it's over 95% health and you get the item back. Below that, you get part of its materials.
{% endhint %}

### Using things in your base

Face a piece and a white dot appears. Step closer and press **E**, and scroll for more options. Walls, floors, foundations and pillars only show theirs while you hold a **Hammer**. Doors and gates open with **E**, big gates also from inside your vehicle. Their settings are on the third eye.

## Building tiers

| Tier | Wall health | Made from | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🪵 **Wood** | 10,000 | Wood and planks, with nails | Known from the start |
| 🪨 **Stone** | 15,000 | Stone and mortar | **Stonework** research |
| 🔩 **Metal** | 20,000 | Iron, steel and metal fittings | **Metalwork** research |

Stonework and Metalwork don't need each other. Every tier has foundations, walls, pillars, floors, roofs, stairs, ladders, windows, doors, gates and their frames. Wood also has **big walls and big gates** (30,000 health).

Upgrade a piece in place instead of tearing it down: you pay the cost of the new one. The **upkeep box** can upgrade a whole base at once.

## Doors, storage and locks

Doors and storage are **locked to everyone except you and your crew**. Fit a **Code Lock** and set a code to let someone else in. Each player gets **10 wrong guesses** per server restart.

| Storage | Room | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wood Storage** | 50 slots, 100 kg | Known from the start |
| **Metal Storage Lv2** | 100 slots, 500 kg | Stonework |
| **Metal Storage** | 75 slots, 200 kg | Metalwork |

## Upkeep

Your base **wears down all the time**. An untouched wood wall lasts about two weeks, stone about three, metal about four. Then it's gone.

Place an **upkeep box**, stock it with the materials your pieces are made of, and it repairs everything in range **when the server restarts**. It repairs at full building cost for the damage done.

| Box | Reach | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lv1** | 50 m | Known from the start |
| **Lv2** | 100 m | Stonework |
| **Lv3** | 150 m | Industrial |

The box shows your average cost for 24 hours, can highlight what it covers, and can repair the base on the spot. You can also repair a single piece by hand.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**An empty box does nothing.** Stone and metal need stone and metal, not just wood.
{% endhint %}

## Raids

Walls come down with **explosives**. Hold one, walk up to the piece and choose **Use c4** (hold **E**). Planting takes 7 seconds, and it goes off 5 seconds later.

| Explosive | Damage when planted |
| --- | --- |
| **Sticky bomb** | 7,500 |
| **Grenade** | 4,000 |

Rockets also damage pieces. You can't plant on your own or your crew's base.

### When a base can be raided

* **Protected while everyone is offline.** A base becomes safe **15 minutes** after the last owner logs off.
* **Logging off doesn't stop a raid.** Once one has started, the base stays raidable for **45 minutes**.
* **Check raiding status** on a piece tells you whether you can raid it right now.
* **You get a warning** in game when your base is hit, at most once every 25 minutes.
* **Crews are locked** during a raid: nobody in, nobody out.
* You can still repair and build while you're being raided.

## Tips

* **Lock the room with your workbench.** Your blueprints are in it.
* **Stock the upkeep box before a long trip.**
* **Build with your [crew](crews.md).** You decide what each rank may do.
