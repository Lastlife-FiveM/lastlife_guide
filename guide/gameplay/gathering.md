# Gathering

Before you can build, craft or trade, you need materials. You get them straight from the world: break rock, cut down trees, sweep the beaches, and later on, blow things up.

## How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get the right tool in your hands

Each job needs its own tool, and it has to be **in your hands**, not just in your bag.

* **Axes and pickaxes:** draw them like a weapon.
* **Jackhammer, chainsaw, metal detector:** **use** the item to take it out. Use it again to put it away.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Look at it

Go right up to rock, a tree trunk or sand and look straight at it. With the right tool out, a prompt shows up on the spot: **Mine rock**, **Chop tree**, **Sweep sand** and so on. No prompt means wrong tool, wrong surface, or a spot that's worked out.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Press E

Your survivor gets to work. When the bar fills up, the materials go into your inventory. You can cancel at any time, but then you get nothing.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Every spot runs out.** After 3 or 4 hauls it's worked out, and it comes back **20 minutes** after the last haul. It's shared, so other survivors use up the same spots.
{% endhint %}

## What you can gather

| Where | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| ⛏️ **Rock**: cliffs, boulders, quarries | 2 stone every haul, plus ore now and then. Iron, copper and coal are common. Aluminum, lead and zinc less so. Sulfur and saltpeter are uncommon, silver and gold rare, rough diamonds *very* rare. |
| 🪓 **Trees** | 2 wood logs every haul, plus sticks and tree bark. Sometimes worms or insects for bait. |
| 🔍 **Sand**: beaches and desert | Scrap, old cash, ammo, weapon parts, and now and then jewellery someone buried and never came back for. Plenty of sweeps turn up nothing. |

## Dynamite

One stick brings down more rock than a jackhammer does in a minute. It's the end of the gathering line, and it's loud.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Plant it

Look at **rock** from close by and **use** the dynamite. Planting takes 4 seconds and uses up the stick. You can have **3 sticks** waiting at once. A stick nobody blows up is gone after 30 minutes.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Get clear

Within **60 metres** of your sticks, use the **Remote Detonator**. Three seconds of beeping, then everything of yours in range goes off. You keep the detonator.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take the rubble

Look at the pile: press **E** on one find, or **hold E** on **Take all**. You and your crew get it to yourselves for **60 seconds**, then it's anyone's. Leftovers disappear after 15 minutes.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The blast is real.** It hurts you, your friends and anything else close by, and the noise draws the dead from about 150 metres. A blasted spot can't be mined or blasted again for **45 minutes**.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Bring a bag.** Ore and logs add up fast. Whatever doesn't fit drops at your feet.
* **Work in groups.** You can't fight while you're busy, so have someone keep watch.
* **Smelt your ore.** Raw ore isn't much use until it's been through a smelter.
* **It's worth XP.** Every haul gives 3 [rank](ranks.md) XP, up to 30 a minute.
