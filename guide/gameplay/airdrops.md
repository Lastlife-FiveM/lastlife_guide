# Airdrops

Somebody out there still has helicopters. Now and then one flies over and drops a crate. Everyone gets the message, and everyone can see roughly where.

## When they come

Every half hour there's a chance of a drop, day and night. In the **evening** (18:00 to midnight, US Central time) it's far more likely to be **weapons**.

## The crates

Each kind has its own colour, for its smoke and its circle on the map.

| Crate | Colour | What's inside | Opens after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Weapons** | 🔴 Red | Guns, attachments, ammo, weapon parts | 3 minutes |
| **Medical** | ⚪ White | Bandages, painkillers, first aid, splints, sometimes an antidote | 90 seconds |
| **Food** | 🟠 Orange | Water, food that keeps, seeds, kitchen basics | 90 seconds |
| **Materials** | 🟡 Yellow | Wood, stone, nails, metal and other building supplies | 90 seconds |
| **Supply** | 🔵 Blue | A bit of everything, no guns. Only from a beacon (below). | 30 seconds |

Weapons crates come down in places that draw a crowd. The others can land all over the map. They all land well away from safe zones.

## Getting to one

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Read the message

It tells you what kind of crate is coming. The helicopter shows on your map on its way in, until it reaches the area.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Search the circle

Your map shows a **search circle** 150 metres around, not the exact spot. The crate is always inside it, and **never in the middle**. Look for the parachute and then the coloured smoke.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait for it to open

The crate stays shut for a while so that others have time to show up. The smoke turns **green** once it's open.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open it

Use your third eye on the crate and pick **Open Crate**. Take what you need.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

A scheduled drop lasts **30 minutes**. After that it's over.

{% hint style="warning" %}
An airdrop isn't a red zone. Out in the open world the usual fighting rules still apply, even over a weapons crate. See [Zones & PvP](zones.md).
{% endhint %}

## Supply Beacon

Found now and then in **wrecked vehicles**. **Use** it from your inventory outside and a helicopter drops a supply crate where you stand.

* It's used up when you call it.
* It doesn't work inside a safe zone.
* **Everyone gets a message**, so expect visitors.
* The crate opens after 30 seconds and the drop ends after 15 minutes.

## Tips

* **Get there early, then hide.** The wait is when people show themselves.
* **Bring a vehicle with boot space** for materials.
* **Use a beacon somewhere you can defend,** or somewhere nobody will bother to drive to.
