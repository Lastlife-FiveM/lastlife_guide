# Cooking & Farming

Scavenged food runs out. A fire, a few garden beds and a couple of hens don't. This is how you feed a crew.

## Cooking

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Make a fire

| Fire | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| **Campfire** | 5 wood, 1 nail |
| **Hobo Stove** | 5 wood, 5 iron, 1 metal fittings |

Craft it, place it, fill it with **wood** and switch it on.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cook

Open the **lit** fire like a bench. It knows every cooking recipe from the start.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### The kitchen

Build these at a workbench.

| Piece | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hand Mill** | 12 stone, 2 mortar, 4 wood | 3 wheat into flour |
| **Butter Churn** | 4 planks, 2 metal scrap | A bucket of milk into 2 butter or 1 cheese, or 3 bottles of milk if you bring 3 empty bottles |

Glass jars (2 glass), empty pails (3 metal scrap) and cloth from 4 cotton are workbench recipes. Lard is made from pig fat over the fire.

## Growing crops

You build the pieces at your base. You and your crew can use them. No research needed.

| Piece | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| **Garden Bed** (4 plots) | 6 planks, 2 nails |
| **Greenhouse** (room for 4 beds) | 10 planks, 8 glass, 4 nails |
| **Compost Bin** | 6 planks, 2 nails |

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get seeds

You find them while looting farms and sheds, and **Brother Amos** at the Altruist Camp sometimes sells some. Potatoes are planted from a potato, wheat from wheat.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Compost

One **Compost Fertilizer** feeds the whole bed. It lasts 200 minutes while something grows. You can see the soil rise.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Plant and water

Fill a **Watering Can** (3 plastic) at a river, lake, the sea or a water well. One pour waters the whole bed. A full plot lasts almost 5 hours. Rain does it for you outdoors.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Weed

A weed shows up about every 40 minutes, up to three. Each one slows growth by **15%**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Harvest

When it's ready, take it. Most crops can give a seed or two back.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

| Crop | Time | Harvest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lettuce | 1 h | 2 to 3 |
| Peas | 1.5 h | 4 to 7 |
| Tomato | 2 h | 4 to 7 |
| Carrot | 2 h | 3 to 5 |
| Cucumber | 2 h | 3 to 6 |
| Onion | 2.5 h | 3 to 5 |
| Strawberry | 2.5 h | 5 to 9 |
| Potato | 3 h | 3 to 6 |
| Wheat | 3 h | 5 to 8, plus hay |
| Blueberry | 3 h | 6 to 10 |
| Cotton | 3 h | 3 to 5 |
| Sunflower | 3 h | 2 to 4 |
| Corn | 3.5 h | 4 to 7 |
| Pumpkin | 4 h | 1 to 2 |
| Melon | 4 h | 1 to 3 |
| Hops | 4 h | 3 to 6 |

* A plot without water **or** compost stops growing. Crops don't grow while the server is down.
* **Rotate.** The same crop twice in a row in one plot gives **30% less**.
* **Storms.** A ready crop left outdoors in a thunderstorm for 30 minutes is flattened: **half the harvest**.
* **Greenhouse:** crops grow **25% faster** and storms can't touch them. Rain can't either, so you water yourself. Add garden beds from the greenhouse's own screen.

### Compost

The bin turns rotten food, food scraps, tree bark and bones into Compost Fertilizer, **one per 15 minutes**. Manure counts double.

You can also craft compost at the Observatory bench or a workbench: 1 fish fillet or 1 raw game with 2 bark, or 3 worms with 1 bark.

## Keeping animals

Living hens, pigs and cows can be caught. Walk up, **hold E** on **Grab**, and carry it home. **Use** it next to the right pen.

| Pen | Holds | Cost | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chicken Coop** | 6 hens | 12 planks, 6 wood, 4 nails | From the start |
| **Pig Pen** | 6 pigs | 14 planks, 10 wood, 6 nails | Farming level 10 |
| **Cow Pen** | 4 cows | 20 planks, 16 wood, 8 nails | Farming level 20 |

| Animal | Eats | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🐔 **Hen** | Wheat, sunflowers, peas | An egg every 30 minutes. The nest holds 12 |
| 🐖 **Pig** | Food scraps, rotten food, most vegetables | Manure every hour, up to 10 |
| 🐄 **Cow** | Hay, wheat, carrots, lettuce | A bucket of milk every 2 hours. Bring an empty pail |

Keep the feed and water troughs filled. A water bottle adds 25, or use the watering can. Rain fills outdoor troughs. A hungry or thirsty animal gives nothing and loses **10% health an hour**.

### More animals

* **Pigs and cows** breed when two of them have at least 60% health and there's enough feed. Piglets (1 or 2) grow up in 6 hours, a calf in 10. Then they rest: 4 hours for pigs, 8 for cows.
* **Chicks** hatch in an **Incubator** (2 electronics, 2 glass, 4 plastic, 2 cables). It holds 6 eggs and needs base power. An egg hatches after 2 hours, and the chick is a hen 4 hours later. Keep a coop with room close by.

### Butchering

With a cutting weapon on you, butcher an animal from its pen.

| Animal | You get |
| --- | --- |
| Hen | 1 raw poultry, feathers |
| Pig | 2 raw pork, pig fat, hide |
| Cow | 3 raw beef, 2 bones, hide |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**If a pen is destroyed, every animal in it dies.** Damage alone doesn't hurt them. If you pick a pen up, the adults run loose as items on the ground and the young are lost.
{% endhint %}

## The Farming skill

Harvesting, collecting eggs, milking, filling troughs, breeding, hatching and butchering all train **Farming**. It never fades.

| Level | Perk |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Plant and harvest crops, keep hens |
| 5 | +10% crop yield |
| 10 | Keep pigs, compost works 25% faster |
| 15 | Weeds grow 25% slower |
| 20 | Keep cows, 25% chance of an extra egg |
| 25 | +20% crop yield |
| 30 | Breeding needs half the feed |
| 35 | +15% chance of seeds back |
| 40 | Animals lose health 30% slower |
| 45 | +30% crop yield, milk every 90 minutes |

The yield bonuses don't add up. The best one counts.

## Tips

* **Start with lettuce and peas.** Quick seeds, quick food.
* **Feed the bin.** Bark, bones and scraps you'd throw away become compost.
* **Grow wheat.** It's flour for you, feed for the hens and hay for the cows.
* **Corn, hops and fruit** have other uses too. See [Moonshine](moonshine.md).
