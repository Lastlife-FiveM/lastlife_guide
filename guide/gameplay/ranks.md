# Ranks

Your **rank** is your survivor's overall level. It starts at **1** and goes up to **100**. Nearly everything useful you do out there feeds it.

Press **Z** (default) to show your rank bar at any time. You can change the key in Settings > Key Bindings > FiveM. The bar also pops up by itself whenever you earn XP.

## How to earn XP

| What you do | XP | Most you can earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🧟 Kill a zombie | **10** each | 40 kills a minute (zombies and animals together) |
| 🦌 Kill an animal | **20** each | Counts toward the same 40 |
| 🔎 Search a fresh loot spot | **4** each | 40 XP a minute |
| 🛠️ Claim crafted items | **2** per item | 40 XP a minute |
| ⛏️ Gather (one finished bar of mining, chopping or sweeping) | **3** each | 30 XP a minute |
| 🎣 Land a fish | **10** each | 40 XP a minute |
| 🌾 Farm work (harvest, eggs, milk, feeding, breeding, hatching, butchering) | **3** each time | 30 XP a minute |
| 🐕 Your companion kills a zombie | **2** each | 20 XP a minute |
| 🥃 Moonshine work | **2 to 12** per job | Shares the crafting 40 XP a minute |
| 🚇 Get out of the subway alive | **150** | No limit |
| 🚇 Crew bonus for getting out | **75** for each crewmate who also got out within 3 minutes | Up to 3 crewmates |

Some details worth knowing:

* **You have to make the kill.** Running one over while you drive counts too. Each body pays once, and bosses pay the same as any other zombie.
* **Bodies count as loot spots.** So do cupboards, cars, bins and the spots inside houses. Once a spot has been searched it has to fill up again before it pays.
* **Crafting pays when you claim**, not when you queue. A big claim stops paying at the 40 XP cap.
* **Every activity has its own cap**, so mixing things earns more than doing one thing flat out.
* **The cast net counts as fishing**, as long as it catches something. Fish traps give no XP.

{% hint style="success" %}
**You never lose XP by dying.** Nothing you do in the world takes it away. Your rank only goes up.
{% endhint %}

## What your rank unlocks

Your rank moves your **class** up its tiers:

| Rank | Class tier |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Tier 0 (comes with your class) |
| 5 | Tier 1 |
| 10 | Tier 2 |
| 15 | Tier 3 |

The tier changes the moment you reach the rank. There is nothing to claim. See [Classes](classes.md) for what each tier does.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Past rank 15 your rank is bragging rights**, for now. It doesn't gate recipes, loot, weapons, zones or traders. Those open up through [Research](research.md), [Skills](skills.md) and what you dare to go and take. See [Progression](progression.md).
{% endhint %}
