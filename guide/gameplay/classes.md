# Classes

Your class is who your survivor was before it all went wrong. You pick it on the intake form when you [make your survivor](character.md).

It decides three things: your **starting kit**, the **safe zone you wake up at** and your **class tiers**.

## The classes

| Class | What they're about | Where you wake up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Soldier** | Frontline fighter | Sandy Shores Airfield |
| ⛑️ **Medic** | Keeps the crew alive | Galileo Observatory |
| 🔧 **Mechanic** | Keeps the wheels turning | Sandy Shores Airfield |
| 🔍 **Scavenger** | Finds what others miss | Elysian Island |
| 🔨 **Engineer** | Builds and holds the base | Altruist Camp |
| ❤️ **Survivor** | Built to last | Galileo Observatory |

## Starting kits

**Everyone** starts with a **Water**, a **Sandwich**, a **BMX**, a **Stone Hatchet** and their **Survivor ID**. On top of that:

| Class | Extra kit |
| --- | --- |
| 🎯 Soldier | Pistol, 12 rounds of 9mm, Knife |
| ⛑️ Medic | 4 Bandages, Painkillers, First Aid |
| 🔧 Mechanic | Wrench, Repair Kit, 5 Metal Scrap |
| 🔍 Scavenger | 2 Lockpicks, Flashlight |
| 🔨 Engineer | Hammer |
| ❤️ Survivor | Knife, a second Water and a second Sandwich |

## Tiers

Every class has four tiers. They follow your [rank](ranks.md) on their own.

| Tier | Rank needed | Total XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 1 (when you pick the class) | 0 |
| 1 | 5 | 4,220 |
| 2 | 10 | 11,170 |
| 3 | 15 | 18,990 |

| Class | Tier 0 | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 Soldier | Steady Hands | Adrenaline | Rally | Commander |
| ⛑️ Medic | Field Training | Second Wind | Medic Box | Trauma Surgeon |
| 🔧 Mechanic | Grease Monkey | Jury Rig | Spare Parts | Master Mechanic |
| 🔍 Scavenger | Keen Eye | Sweep | Spotter | Ghost |
| 🔨 Engineer | Builder | Barricade | Fortify | Demolitions |
| ❤️ Survivor | Tough | Grit | Steady Presence | Hardened |

You can see yours in game: press **Esc**, open **Skills** and pick the **Class** tab.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Only the Mechanic's perks work right now.** The other five classes show their tier names and short descriptions, and your tier does go up with your rank, but those perks don't do anything yet. Pick the one that fits how you want to play.
{% endhint %}

## 🔧 Mechanic perks

These all work today. They count in the [mechanic](mechanic.md) menus, on any vehicle. Each tier keeps what the tiers before it gave you.

| Tier | Rank | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grease Monkey** | 1 | Mechanic work is **30% faster**. You repair engines and bodies to **100%** (everyone else stops at **60%**). Fit and craft **level 3** parts. |
| **Jury Rig** | 5 | **No progress bars** on mechanic work: repairs and installs are instant. |
| **Spare Parts** | 10 | Repairs use **half the materials** (rounded up, at least 1). Your mechanic tools **never wear out**. Fit and craft **level 4** parts. |
| **Master Mechanic** | 15 | Fit and craft **level 5** parts, and fit all the top extras below. |

### Who can fit what

| Part | Everyone | Mechanic 0 and 1 | Mechanic 2 | Mechanic 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engine, suspension | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 |
| Transmission | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 4 (the highest there is) |
| Brakes, oil pump, drive shaft, cylinder head, battery cables, fuel tank | Level 2 | Level 3 (the highest there is) | Level 3 | Level 3 |
| Turbo, vehicle armour, bulletproof tires, nitrous and its colour injector, anti-lag | No | No | No | **Yes** |

Anyone can **take a part off**. The level 3 and higher recipes only show up at the bench for a Mechanic of the right tier, and they don't need research.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Not a Mechanic?** You can still do basic repairs, use duct tape and fit level 1 and 2 parts. A Mechanic in your crew covers the rest.
{% endhint %}

## Can I change my class?

There is no way to change it yourself in game. It's one class per survivor, and it stays with that survivor. If you want to try another class, make a new survivor in a free slot.
