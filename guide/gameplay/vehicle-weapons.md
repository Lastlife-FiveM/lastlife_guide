# Vehicle Weapons

Find and restore an armed vehicle and its guns are yours. They only fire if you bring the ammo.

## The Ammo Bay

Sit in the vehicle and open your **inventory**. Under the glovebox you'll see the **Ammo Bay**: 6 slots, 60 kg, vehicle ammo only. Any ammo you put there belongs to the vehicle until someone takes it out.

## Reloading

Sit in the seat that controls the gun and press **R** (default). The gun takes what it's short of from the bay. Leave the seat and the reload stops.

Your HUD shows the gun and how many rounds it has. An **empty gun won't fire**.

| Ammo | Feeds | One load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vehicle MG Rounds** | Machine guns and turrets | Usually 100, miniguns 200 |
| **Cannon Shell** | Mortars, cannons, grenade launchers | 1 to 3 |
| **Vehicle Rocket** | Missile pods | 2 |
| **Vehicle Bomb** | Mines | 4 |

**One item is one round.** Flame throwers run on MG Rounds. Water cannons need nothing. Reloads take 4 to 8 seconds, and rounds already **loaded are lost at a server restart**. Rounds still in the bay are safe.

## Making ammo

Research **Vehicle Armament** (after Submachine Guns: 350 chits, 8 steel, 4 electronics, 8 minutes).

| Ammo | Gives | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicle MG Rounds | 100 | 6 lead, 5 brass, 6 gunpowder, 1 steel |
| Cannon Shell | 1 | 2 steel, 2 brass, 4 gunpowder |
| Vehicle Rocket | 2 | 2 steel, 2 aluminum, 6 gunpowder, 1 electronics, 1 precision parts |
| Vehicle Bomb | 4 | 2 steel, 5 gunpowder, 2 electronics, 2 metal fittings |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Bring a gunner.** The driver usually can't work the turret.
{% endhint %}
