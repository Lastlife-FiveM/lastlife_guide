# Zombies

The dead are slow to notice you and quick to catch you. Learn what they react to and you can walk past most of them.

## Day and night

A full day takes about **96 minutes**. For the dead, night runs from **20:00 to 06:00** on the in-game clock, which is about **40 minutes**.

| | Day | Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How many** | Fewer | More |
| **Health** | Normal | 50% more |
| **Damage** | Normal | 50% more |

Some of the worst of them only come out after dark. Animals are the other way round: you'll see more of them by day.

## What they notice

Zombies **see** you up to about 26 metres away, and they **hear** you. Once one has heard you, it can keep following for about a minute.

| What you do | How far it carries, about |
| --- | --- |
| Sneaking | Half a metre |
| Walking | 5 metres |
| Running | 15 metres |
| Sprinting | 30 metres |
| An explosion | 200 metres |

Gunfire carries too. The **red circle** around you on the minimap shows how far you can be heard right now. You need a GPS to see the map: [HUD & Map](hud-and-map.md).

Other things ring the dinner bell: the **alarm** on a subway exit, a crashed **helicopter**, **dynamite** and a house door being **kicked in**.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Zombies don't climb ladders.** They also won't pull you out of a vehicle, but they will beat it until the engine gives up.
{% endhint %}

## Special infected

Most of the dead are ordinary. Some aren't, and those show on your map. They turn up anywhere now and then, and far more often in [red zones](zones.md).

| Type | What it does | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ☣️ **Toxic** | Gives off poison gas close to it. The gas kills engines. | Shoot it from a distance. Don't park near it. |
| 🔥 **Burning** | A burned fireman that sets you, or your vehicle, alight up close. | Keep a few metres away. |
| ⚡ **Juggernaut** | Shocks you off your feet and kills engines. Only in the **Prison** red zone. | Stay out of reach and bring plenty of ammo. |
| 📢 **Screamer** | Screams again and again, and every zombie within about 200 metres hears it. | Kill it first. |

The toxic, burning and electric ones are **bosses**. They have three to four times the health of a normal zombie and hit a lot harder, but headshots still work. In a red zone, all of that goes up again.

Bosses carry **better loot**. In a red zone that can mean guns, attachments, an antidote or a subway access card.

## Nests

A nest keeps sending zombies until someone burns it down with Molotovs. They grow in the red zones, at a few far-off spots and around crashed helicopters. The details are in [Zones & PvP](zones.md).

## Hordes

Noise is what makes a horde. Gunfire, screamers and explosions pull the dead in from all around, and they keep coming while the noise lasts. Inside a house you're robbing there's a [noise meter](house-robberies.md), and filling it brings them to the door.

## Are zombie hits infectious?

No. A zombie can hurt you, but it can't infect you. **Infection** only comes from the toxic gas in [the subway](subway.md), and only an **Antidote** clears it. See [Survival](survival.md) for wounds and healing.

## Places they don't spawn

* **Safe zones.**
* **Around your base.** A totem stops new zombies appearing within **150 metres**. They can still walk in. See [Base Building](base-building.md).

## Searching the dead

Walk up to a body and **look at it** until the prompt shows, then press **E** (default) to search it. Don't hang about: bodies are cleared away after a few minutes. Each kill also gives rank XP: [Ranks](ranks.md).

## Tips

* **Sneak past, don't sprint past.** Sprinting is heard six times further than walking.
* **Aim for the head.**
* **Go quiet at night.** Every mistake costs half as much again.
* **Never park next to a boss.** Toxic and electric ones kill your engine.
* The groaning too much? **K** (default) switches zombie sounds off and on.
