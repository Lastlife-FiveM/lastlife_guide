# Crews

A crew shares a base, vehicles, a chat channel and each other's backs. Everything is on the **Crew** page of the pause menu (**Esc > Crew**).

## Start or join one

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Start a crew

Pick **Start a crew** and choose a name (3 to 24 characters), a tag (2 to 4 letters or numbers) and a colour. Names and tags are unique, and very dark colours are refused because nobody could read them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Invite people

Stand within **15 metres** of the person, open **Members** and invite them. They find it under **Invites** on their own Crew page. An invite lasts **5 minutes**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### They join

New members start on the lowest rank, **Recruit**. A crew holds up to **10** people, and you can only be in one crew.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Ranks

Every crew starts with five ranks. The leader can rename them and change what each one may do on the **Ranks** page.

| Rank | What it starts with |
| --- | --- |
| **Leader** | Everything, always. The crew's owner. |
| **Officer** | Everything except changing the tag and colour. |
| **Builder** | Build, upgrade, doors, storage, upkeep stocks, use and manage base objects, crew vehicles. |
| **Member** | Doors, storage, base objects, crew vehicles. |
| **Recruit** | Crew doors. |

You can only promote, demote or remove people ranked **below** you, and never to a rank as high as your own (the leader excepted).

### Permissions

| Permission | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| Build | Placing pieces in the crew's build area |
| Remove, upgrade | Taking down or upgrading crew pieces |
| Doors, storages, stocks | Crew doors, storage boxes and upkeep boxes |
| Interact, advanced interact | Using crew objects and their settings |
| Change code | Codes on crew locks |
| Drive vehicles | Driving and locking crew vehicles |
| Invite, remove, manage members | Who is in the crew, and at what rank |
| See activity log | The **Activity** tab |
| Change tag and colour | How the crew looks |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Read this before you hand out ranks.** Some things are for every crew member whatever their rank, like the base garage, the helipad and the car lift.
{% endhint %}

## What you share

* **Base and research.** Crew build areas, doors, storage and benches work for members with the permission. See [Base Building](base-building.md) and [Crafting & Research](crafting-research.md).
* **Code locks.** Crewmates with the right permission don't need the code.
* **Vehicles.** Crewmates can drive and lock yours if their rank allows it. Only the owner can park a vehicle in a garage. See [Vehicles & Garages](vehicles.md).
* **Crew chat.** Your own channel, with a radio. See [Chat & Radio](communication.md).

## On the page

* **Members.** Who is in, who is online, and when the others were last seen.
* **Activity.** Joins, leaves, rank changes, raids and more.
* **Map.** Crewmates show in your crew colour on both maps. Hide one person or everyone, just for you.
* **Name tags.** `[TAG] Name` over crewmates' heads within 40 metres, when you can see them. Off until you turn it on.

## Leaving, handing over, deleting

* Leave or get removed and you wait **24 hours** before you can join another crew. Starting your own has no wait.
* The leader can't just leave. Hand the crew over first (you drop to Officer), or delete it.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**No changes during a raid.** While a raid is on, you can't create, delete, leave, invite, promote or remove. You can't kick someone to save the base.
{% endhint %}
