# Weapons

## Where guns come from

| Source | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Workbench** | Pistols, SMGs, two shotguns, melee, Molotovs |
| **Out in the world** | Now and then a simple pistol in a gun case. Mostly parts. |
| **Houses** | Small arms and tools, see [House Robberies](house-robberies.md) |
| **Red zones** | Rifles, the strong pistols, better shotguns, a rare marksman or sniper rifle |
| **Airdrops** | Red-zone guns. See [Airdrops](airdrops.md). |
| **The subway** | Machine guns, heavy snipers, launchers and explosives. See [The Subway](subway.md). |
| **Traders** | Little, and not always in stock. See [Traders](traders.md). |

**Rifles can't be crafted.** You take them from a red zone or a crate.

## Building a gun

Make the parts at a workbench, then put them together.

| Part | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Gun parts (2) | 3 metal scrap, 1 iron |
| Gun barrel | 3 iron |
| Trigger assembly | 1 iron, 2 gun parts |
| Pistol frame | 3 plastic, 1 iron |
| Shotgun receiver | 5 iron |
| Stock | 3 planks, or 4 plastic |
| SMG receiver (Machine Pistols) | 3 iron, 2 aluminum |
| Precision parts (Heavy SMGs) | 1 metal alloy, 2 steel |

| Guns | Extra parts | Research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pistol, SNS, Vintage, Ceramic, Perico, WM 29, Marksman Pistol, Double Action, Navy Revolver | Pistol frame, barrel, trigger, 4 gun parts | None |
| Combat Pistol, Pistol MK2, SNS MK2 | Pistol frame, barrel, trigger, 6 gun parts | **Sidearms** |
| Micro SMG, Mini SMG, Machine Pistol | SMG receiver, barrel, trigger, 6 gun parts | **Machine Pistols** |
| SMG, Tactical SMG | SMG receiver, barrel, trigger, stock, 8 gun parts | **Submachine Guns** |
| SMG Mk2, Assault SMG, Combat PDW, Gusenberg | SMG receiver, barrel, trigger, stock, 2 precision parts, 10 gun parts | **Heavy SMGs** |
| Double Barrel | Shotgun receiver, 2 barrels, trigger, stock | None |
| Pump Shotgun | Shotgun receiver, barrel, trigger, stock, 6 gun parts | **Long Guns** |

For ammo and add-ons, see [Ammo](ammo.md) and [Attachments](attachments.md).

## Melee

The knife and the bat come off the safe-zone bench. A workbench also makes the machete, dagger, switchblade, crowbar, hatchet, golf club, knuckle dusters, nightstick, pool cue, wrench and hammer. The **Battle Axe** needs **Felling** research. Scrap-built blades are a step up, see [Exotics](exotics.md).

## Carrying them

* **Primary** (key **1**) takes any weapon.
* **Secondary** (key **2**) takes pistols, shotguns, melee and throwables.

Guns you carry **show on your body**, on your back, hip, thigh or belt, with their attachments. Everyone can see what you have. `/weaponposition` moves them around, and lets you hide tools.

## Wear and repair

Every shot and swing wears a weapon down. At zero it's useless until you fix it.

A **Weapon Repair Kit** takes it back to **100%**: hold the weapon and use the kit (8 seconds). Research **Weapon Upkeep** (30 chits, 1 plastic) to make them at a workbench.

## Your crosshair

Build your own on the **HUD** page of the pause menu: pick a preset, build one from sliders, draw it, or take one from the library. You can use a different one for each kind of weapon, turn on hitmarkers, low-ammo colours and target colours, and share yours with other players by code.
