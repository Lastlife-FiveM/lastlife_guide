# Crafting

Everything worth having gets made at a bench. Start at a public bench in a safe zone, then build your own and unlock better recipes through [Research](research.md).

## The benches

| Bench | Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🛠️ **Safe-zone bench** | Galileo Observatory and Altruist Camp. Look for the crafting bench icon on your map. | The basics, see below. No research, no blueprints. |
| 🪚 **Workbench** | The Crafting Table, placed at your base | Almost everything. Has the **Research** and **Blueprints** tabs. |
| 🔫 **Weapons table** | Placed at your base (Weapon Upkeep research) | Repair Kits, Advanced Repair Kits, Cleaning Kits. |
| 🔥 **Campfire**, **Hobo stove** | Placed at your base | Cooking, water, burning wood down to coal. Must be lit. |
| 🏭 **Smelter** | Placed at your base | Ore into ingots, steel, alloy and bars. Must be lit. |
| ♻️ **Recycler** | Placed at your base (Recycling research) | Breaks junk down into materials. Needs no power. |
| 💧 **Water well** | Placed at your base | Fills empty bottles. |
| 🌾 **Hand mill**, **Butter churn** | Placed at your base | Flour, butter, cheese and bottled milk. See [Cooking & Farming](cooking-farming.md). |

To use a bench, walk up, look at it and press **E**.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Fires burn fuel.** Campfires and stoves burn wood, the smelter burns coal. Fill them up and switch them on, or they won't open. A job that's already running finishes even if the fire goes out.
{% endhint %}

### What the safe-zone bench makes

* **Survival:** Bandage, Painkillers, Splint, Water
* **Tools and weapons:** Knife, Bat, Stone Hatchet, Pickaxe, scrap 9mm, GPS
* **Materials:** Metal Scrap, Nails, Wood Planks
* **Your first base:** **Totem** and **Crafting Table**
* **Fishing and farming:** Makeshift Rod, Twine, Small Hook, Float, Cut Bait, Garden Shovel, Watering Can, Compost Fertilizer
* **Other:** 50cc Engine, Motorized Bike, Outfit Bag

## How crafting works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Pick a recipe

Pick a category on the left, then narrow it down with the chips on top. Base building also has a **Wood / Stone / Metal** switch.

* **Search** looks through every category at that bench.
* **Can craft** shows only what you have the materials for.
* **Hide locked** hides what still needs research.
* **Sort** by can craft first, name, fastest or best rarity.

A locked recipe shows as a dark shape with a padlock. Pick it and it tells you what unlocks it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hold to craft

Choose how many, up to **20** in one job, and hold the button until it fills. The materials leave your inventory **right away** and the job joins the queue along the bottom.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Walk away if you like

The queue keeps running while you're gone, even if you log out. Jobs at a table run **one after another**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Claim it

Come back and press **Claim**. Whatever fits goes into your inventory. The rest waits at the bench.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Claiming gives [rank XP](ranks.md): **2 per item**, up to 40 XP a minute.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Pin what you're working toward.** Pin up to **3** recipes and they show on your HUD with live counts.
{% endhint %}

### Queues

* A table holds up to **12 jobs**.
* **At a base, the queue is shared.** Everyone who uses that table sees every job and can claim what's ready, whoever queued it.
* **At a safe-zone bench, your jobs are yours.** Nobody else sees them or can take them.
* **If a table is destroyed**, its jobs go back to whoever queued them, and can be claimed at any bench.

### More than one recipe

Some items can be made more than one way. Use the **Change recipe** bar to switch. Iron Ingots can come from ore or metal scrap, and a Gun Stock from wood or plastic.

### Rarity

Crafted items carry a **rarity**: Scrap, Salvaged, Scarce, Hoarded or Relic. See [Loot & Rarity](loot-rarity.md).

### Class recipes

The level 3 and higher car parts, turbo, vehicle armour and the other top extras only show up for a **Mechanic** of the right tier. See [Classes](classes.md).

## Smelting

Put **coal** in the smelter, switch it on and open it like any bench.

| Makes | From | Seconds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron, Copper, Aluminium, Lead or Zinc Ingot | 2 of its ore, 1 Coal | 10 |
| Iron Ingot (other way) | 6 Metal Scrap, 1 Coal | 12 |
| Brass | 2 Copper Ingots, 1 Zinc Ingot, 1 Coal | 12 |
| **Steel** (Smelting research) | 2 Iron Ingots, 2 Coal | 15 |
| **Metal Alloy** (Metalwork research) | 2 Aluminium, 2 Copper, 1 Iron Ingot, 1 Coal | 25 |
| Silver Bar or Gold Bar | 3 of its ore, 1 Coal | 20 |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Research covers the smelter too.** Research Smelting or Metalwork at your workbench and your base's smelters know it. Only a blueprint **item** has to be put in the smelter itself.
{% endhint %}

## Making a gun

Guns are built from parts. Each part is its own craft.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Weapon Parts

3 Metal Scrap and 1 Iron Ingot make 2. You'll need a lot of these.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### The main pieces

A **Gun Barrel** and a **Trigger Assembly** for every gun. Then a **Pistol Frame**, **Shotgun Receiver** or **SMG Receiver**, and for long guns a **Gun Stock**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put it together

| Gun | What goes in |
| --- | --- |
| Pistol | Pistol Frame, Gun Barrel, Trigger Assembly, 4 Weapon Parts |
| Combat Pistol | Pistol Frame, Gun Barrel, Trigger Assembly, 6 Weapon Parts |
| Pump Shotgun | Shotgun Receiver, Gun Barrel, Trigger Assembly, Gun Stock, 6 Weapon Parts |
| SMG | SMG Receiver, Gun Barrel, Trigger Assembly, Gun Stock, 8 Weapon Parts |
| SMG Mk2 | SMG Receiver, Gun Barrel, Trigger Assembly, Gun Stock, 2 Precision Parts, 10 Weapon Parts |
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

**Ammo:** scrap 9mm needs only 5 Metal Scrap for 6 rounds. With **Reloading** you get 12 rounds for 1 Lead Ingot, 1 Brass and 1 Gunpowder. Gunpowder is 1 Coal, 1 Sulfur and 2 Saltpeter for 4. See [Ammo](ammo.md).

## Research and blueprints

Better recipes come from the research tree, and a few from blueprints. Costs, times and the whole tree are on [Research & Blueprints](research.md).

## Tips

* **Start at the safe-zone bench.** Nails, planks, a pickaxe, a Totem and a Crafting Table get you going.
* **Queue before a run**, claim when you're back.
* **Lock your base.** Anyone who gets to your table can claim what's ready.
* **Check the switcher.** Short on ore? There might be another way.
