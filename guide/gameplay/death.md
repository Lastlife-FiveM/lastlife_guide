# Dying & Your Body

You will die. Probably a lot. You don't lose your survivor, but you do lose everything you were carrying. The good news: it's all still on your body, if you can get back to it first.

## Going down

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### You're down

Take too much damage and you drop. You bleed out in **6 minutes**. A timer shows how long you've got. If you get hit hard enough again, it's over sooner.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### A friend can pick you up

Anyone with a **First Aid** kit can help you up by using it next to you. It takes **30 to 60 seconds**, uses up the kit, and they can't fight while they do it.

This works once your timer is at **5 minutes or less**, so there's a short wait at the start.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### You're dead

If nobody gets to you in time, you die. **Hold E** for about 5 seconds to respawn. If you don't, you respawn by yourself after **5 minutes**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wake up

You come back with **empty pockets**. Everything you had is where you fell.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**A revive means no corpse.** You keep everything, and your hunger and thirst are filled up.
{% endhint %}

Some dogs can save you too. See [Companions](companions.md).

## Where you wake up

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 🛏️ **You placed a bed** | You choose on the map: one of your own beds, or the closest safe zone |
| 🛡️ **No bed** | The closest safe zone to where you died |

You can wake up at the **Galileo Observatory**, the **Altruist Camp** or **Elysian Island**. Only a **Wood Bed** counts, and it has to be one **you** placed. A sofa won't do.

## Your body

When you respawn, your body stays where you died, wearing your clothes, with **everything you had on you**: weapons, ammo, gear, your plates, your backpack with what's inside, chits, food, all of it.

If you died with nothing on you, there's no body.

### Searching a body

Walk up to it and press **E**, or third-eye it and pick **Search**. It opens like any other container.

## Who can loot it

| Where you died | Who can search your body |
| --- | --- |
| 🌍 **Open world** | Only **you and your crew** for **10 minutes**. After that, **anyone** |
| 🔴 **Red zone** | **Anyone**, straight away |
| 🚇 **Subway** | **Anyone**, straight away |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Bodies don't last.** After **40 minutes** your body disappears with whatever is still on it. A server restart clears every body too.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Go back fast, but not alone.** Whatever killed you is probably still there.
* **Put a bed near where you play.** It can save you a long walk.
* **Carry a First Aid kit** and make sure your crew does too.
* **Think about what you bring into a red zone.** If you die there, anyone can take it the second you fall.
* **Lost your things to a bug?** Type `/report` before the body is gone.
