# Your Character

The world ended. That's no reason to look bad. Here's how to change your look, save outfits and carry your wardrobe with you.

## Where to change your look

Four shops are set up at the **Galileo Observatory** and marked on your map.

| Shop | What you change | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 👕 **Clothing store** | Clothes and accessories | $100 |
| 💈 **Barber** | Hair, beard, brows, makeup | $100 |
| 🖋️ **Tattoo parlour** | Tattoos | $100 per new tattoo |
| 🏥 **Plastic surgeon** | Your face | $100 |

You pay in **old-world cash** when you save. Leave without saving and it costs nothing.

### At your own base, for free

Build these at a workbench. No research needed.

| Piece | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wardrobe** | Clothes and outfits | 12 planks, 6 nails, 2 metal fittings |
| **Vanity** | Hair and face details | 8 planks, 4 glass, 4 nails |
| **Tattoo Chair** | Tattoos | 6 iron, 4 cloth, 2 rubber, 2 metal fittings |

## The appearance menu

Every piece is a picture card. Click one to try it on.

* **Turn** by dragging, **scroll** to zoom, and jump to head, body, legs or feet with the camera buttons. You can also raise your hands, turn around and switch the light on.
* **Search** clothes by number or pack, and tattoos by name or number.
* **Favourites:** hover a piece and tap its star.
* **Undo** takes back your last change. **Reset** takes back all of them.
* Prefer another look for the menu itself? Switch its style. It remembers.

**Tops and arms** go together: pick a top and the right arms come with it. **Gloves** have their own section and only show the styles that fit the arms you wear.

Makeup, blush, lipstick, beard, brows and chest hair have a **second colour**. Tattoos can be removed one by one or with **Remove all**. Rest your mouse on a tattoo for a moment to see it up close.

## Outfits

Save what you're wearing with a name and put it back on later. You can keep up to **50 outfits**. Changing into a saved outfit is free.

Each outfit can make a **share code**. Give it to a friend and they can add the outfit to their own list.

## The Outfit Bag

Your wardrobe in a duffel. Craft it at the Observatory bench or any workbench: **8 cloth, 2 duct tape**.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Put it down

**Use** the bag. It follows where you look. **Scroll** turns it, **E** puts it down, **Backspace** cancels.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Use it

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **E** | Open the bag |
| **G** | Change who can open it: **Off**, **Crew** or **Everyone** |
| **Hold H** | Pick it up |
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Get changed

Hover an outfit to try it on. Pick **Wear** and your survivor really gets changed, piece by piece. Moving or getting hurt stops it and puts your old clothes back on.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

You can save what you have on, with or without a **photo**, update, rename or delete outfits, and mark each one as **shared** or **private**. Visitors only see the shared ones. A copy goes into their own list.

{% hint style="warning" %}
One bag on the ground per character. Left alone for **45 minutes**, it's **gone for good**.
{% endhint %}

## Quick changes

Take single pieces off and on with a command. Type it again to put it back.

`/hat` `/glasses` `/mask` `/ear` `/neck` `/shirt` `/top` `/vest` `/gloves` `/watch` `/bracelet` `/bag` `/pants` `/shoes` `/hair` `/visor`

The same options are in the radial menu under **General > Clothing**.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Look broken?** `/reloadskin` reloads your look. `/clearstuckprops` removes something stuck to your body.
{% endhint %}

## Worn gear

Some gear shows on your body on top of your clothes, like a gas mask. Weapons and tools you carry show too. See [Inventory & Gear](inventory.md).

## Survivor ID

Your Survivor ID was made at the checkpoint. **Use** it to look at it: your photo, name, date of birth, sex, blood type, class and ID number. **Esc** closes it.

## Music on the road

The **Fruit Echo** is a small music player. Old Toller on Elysian Island sometimes sells one, and it turns up in houses now and then.

* **Use** it to open it. Add songs with a YouTube link or a direct link to an mp3, ogg, wav or m4a file.
* It holds **50 songs** and **10 playlists**. Only you hear the music, and it keeps playing when you close it.
* **The songs are on the Echo itself.** Lose it and they go with it. Find someone else's and you get their taste in music.
