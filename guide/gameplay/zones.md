# Zones & PvP

Los Santos isn't one big free-for-all. Some places are safe, some are lawless, and everywhere in between runs on roleplay. Know which one you're standing in.

## The rules at a glance

| Zone | Fighting | Loot | Zombies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🛡️ **Safe zones** | None. You can't attack anyone. | No looting | None |
| 🌍 **Open world** | Only if it's roleplayed out | Normal | Everywhere |
| 🔴 **Red zones** | **Kill On Sight** | Better (tier 2) | Tougher, plus bosses and nests |
| 🚇 **Extraction** (the subway) | **Kill On Sight** | The heaviest gear (tier 3) | Toughest |

{% hint style="info" %}
This page covers the basics. The full fighting rules are in the **Server Rules** book, under **PvP & Roleplay**. Read them before your first fight.
{% endhint %}

## 🛡️ Safe zones

Safe zones are where survivors catch their breath. They're marked in green on your map. There are four:

| Safe zone | Where | What you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Galileo Observatory** | The hills above Vinewood | Where Medics and Survivors wake up. **Margo** (requisitions) and **June** (medical), a crafting bench, a garage, and the laptop that shows the [power plant](power.md). A helipad is close by. |
| **Sandy Shores** | The airfield | **Dusty** (supplies), **Hollis** (dogs) and **Sgt. Dana Pryce** (quartermaster). |
| **Elysian Island** | The docks, south Los Santos | **Rook** and **Old Toller** to trade with, plus a boat dock. A helipad is close by. |
| **Altruist Camp** | Up in the hills, north west | **Brother Amos** (seeds), **Sister Wren** (building) and **Jessop** (the still), a crafting bench, a garage and a helipad. |

See [Traders](traders.md) for what they buy and sell.

A message pops up when you walk in or out. Inside a safe zone:

* **No fighting.** You can't shoot or swing at all, and nothing can hurt you.
* **No zombies.** They don't spawn here, and any that wander in are gone.
* **No looting.** Nothing inside a safe zone can be searched.

{% hint style="info" %}
**You stay protected for 20 seconds after you leave.** A countdown shows how long is left. You can't attack either until it ends. Use it to get clear of the edge, not to start a fight.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Safe zones have a height too. Flying high over one, or being down on the water below one, can count as outside. Watch for the message.
{% endhint %}

## 🌍 The open world

Everywhere that isn't a safe zone, a red zone or the subway is open world. This is where you'll spend most of your time: gathering, looting and building.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**PvP in the open world has to be roleplayed.** No random killing. If you want to rob, fight or kill another survivor out here, there has to be a story behind it: talk, threaten, make demands. Shooting someone on sight out here breaks the rules.
{% endhint %}

The game won't stop a bullet out here. Other survivors can hurt you anywhere outside a safe zone, so it's the rules that keep it fair.

The dead are everywhere. There are more of them at night, and they're tougher in the dark. See [Zombies](zombies.md).

## 🔴 Red zones

Red zones are where the good stuff is, and where people die for it. They're marked in red on your map. Right now that's the **Canal Houses** and the **Prison**.

* **Kill On Sight.** Anyone you see can shoot you, and you can shoot them. No roleplay needed.
* **Better loot.** On top of the normal stuff you can find rifles, strong pistols, shotguns, attachments, weapon parts and heavier ammo, and now and then a sniper. Bigger bags and parachutes turn up here too.
* **Tougher zombies.** They have more than twice the health and hit more than twice as hard.
* **Bosses** turn up here far more often. The electric **juggernaut** only walks the Prison.
* **Nests** grow here (see below).
* **Subway access cards.** The card that opens [the subway](subway.md) comes off the dead in red zones, most often off bosses.
* **Bodies are fair game at once.** If you die in a red zone, anyone can search your body straight away. See [Dying & Your Body](death.md).

There are no other lawless zones above ground at the moment.

## 🚇 Extraction: the subway

The subway under the city is the most dangerous place in Los Santos, and the most rewarding.

* **Kill On Sight**, just like a red zone.
* **The heaviest gear.** Explosives, heavy snipers, machine guns and the top scopes.
* **The toughest zombies.** They take a lot more hits down here than they do on the surface.
* **Bodies are fair game at once** here too.

Getting in takes a **Transit Access Card** for the team, and a worn **Gas Mask** and a **Gas Filter** for each person. The air down there will kill you without them. Only a few entrances are open at a time, and they change every restart. See [The Subway](subway.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Bring a crew, bring spare filters, and don't get greedy.** What you find down there is only yours once you make it out.
{% endhint %}

## Nests and outposts

A **nest** keeps sending zombies at you while it stands, and it shows on your map.

* **Zombie nests** grow in the **red zones**. They also hold a few far-off spots: **NOOSE HQ**, the **aircraft carrier** and **Cayo Perico**. A [helicopter crash](helicopter-crashes.md) can grow nests too.
* **Military Outposts** at **Fort Zancudo** are held by armed soldiers instead of the dead. They carry rifles.

How to clear one:

* **Burn it.** Molotovs are the only thing that takes a nest down.
* **Keep your distance.** In the red zones and at crash sites, standing right next to a nest hurts.
* Whoever destroys it gets its **loot** straight into their inventory: weapon parts, often rifle ammo and chits, and now and then an attachment or a rifle. If your bag is full, it drops at your feet.

A destroyed nest can grow back after about two minutes.

## Tips

* **Check your map before you go.** Green is safe. Red is not.
* **Leave your good gear at home** when you head into a red zone for the first time.
* **Fight at a distance near nests.** Throw your molotov and back off.
* **In the open world, talk first.** A good robbery is better roleplay than a bullet.
