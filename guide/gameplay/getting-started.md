# Getting Started

Everyone starts the same way: a refugee in a queue, a form on a clipboard, and then everything goes wrong. This page walks you through your first hour, from joining the server to putting down your first base.

## Joining the server

While the game loads, you'll see the LastLife loading screen. It's worth a look:

* **Your welcome card** shows your name, who's online and how long you've played.
* **Next restart** tells you when the city resets, in your own time. There are three restarts a day.
* **Tips** change above the loading bar.
* **Survivor Radio** plays music while you wait. Turn it up, down or off.

When it's done, the screen fades out and you arrive at the survivor camp.

## The survivor camp

This is your character select. Your survivors sit around a campfire while the dead rattle the fence.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **A** / **D** | Switch seats |
| **Enter** | Play this survivor, or start a new life on an empty seat |
| **Del** | Delete a survivor. You type their full name to confirm |

{% hint style="info" %}
Every player gets **two** character slots to start with.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Deleting is forever.** There's no way back.
{% endhint %}

## Making a new survivor

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### The broadcast and the queue

You hear an emergency broadcast, then you're in the refugee line at the Fort Zancudo checkpoint. Rain, floodlights, soldiers. Wait your turn.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill in the intake form

Fill in your name, date of birth, height, sex and nationality, and pick your **class**. Your blood type is assigned for you.

Names have to look real: 2 to 16 letters each, at most two words, and nobody else can have the same full name. Joke names and banned words get sent back.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### The medical tent

Now you make your character's look. Take your time: changing your face later means a visit to the surgeon. See [Your Character](character.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Your photo and Survivor ID

Look at the camera. You get a **Survivor ID** card with your photo and details. It goes in your inventory.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Run

The checkpoint is overrun. Alarms, gunfire, the dead pouring in. You run.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wake up

You wake up with your starting kit at the camp that fits your trade.

| Class | Where you wake up |
| --- | --- |
| Medic, Survivor | Galileo Observatory |
| Soldier, Mechanic | Sandy Shores |
| Scavenger | Elysian Island |
| Engineer | Altruist Camp |
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Your starting kit

Your class is what your survivor did before the fall. It decides where you wake up and what you carry. What else a class does is on [Classes](classes.md).

| Class | You start with |
| --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Soldier** | An empty pistol, 12 rounds of 9mm, a knife |
| ⛑️ **Medic** | 4 bandages, painkillers, a First Aid kit |
| 🔧 **Mechanic** | A wrench, a repair kit, 5 metal scrap |
| 🔍 **Scavenger** | 2 lockpicks, a flashlight |
| 🔨 **Engineer** | A hammer |
| ❤️ **Survivor** | A knife, an extra water and sandwich |

**Everyone** also gets a water, a sandwich, a **BMX**, a **Stone Hatchet** and a **Survivor ID**.

## Choosing where you wake up

When you come back with a survivor who has already been out in the world, you get the spawn screen.

| Option | Where it puts you |
| --- | --- |
| 📍 **Last location** | Right where you logged out |
| 🛡️ **Safe zones** | Galileo Observatory, Altruist Camp or Elysian Island |
| 🛏️ **Your bed** | Any bed **you** placed, also at a crewmate's base |

Switch with **A** and **D**, press **Enter** to spawn, or **Esc** to go back to the camp.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Last location means last location.** If you logged out in the middle of a horde, that's where you'll be.
{% endhint %}

## Your first steps

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get your bearings

Press **Tab** for your inventory and **Esc** for the map. In a safe zone zombies don't spawn. Read [Zones & PvP](zones.md) before you leave one.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Use the public bench

There's a public **crafting bench** at the **Galileo Observatory**. It makes the basics: bandages, painkillers, splints, water, a knife, a bat, a hatchet, a pickaxe, nails, planks, scrap ammo, simple fishing gear, an **Outfit Bag** and a **GPS**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Make a GPS

Your minimap only shows with a **GPS** in its gear slot. It costs 2 electronics, 2 plastic and 1 lithium. Take apart what you loot.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Go gather

Take your Stone Hatchet to the nearest trees. Make a pickaxe and break some rock. See [Gathering](gathering.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start a base

Back at the bench, craft a **Totem** and a **Crafting Table**. You can only build inside a totem's area, so the totem always goes down first. See [Base Building](base-building.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Get a bed

A **Wood Bed** (after the **Comforts** research) lets you spawn and [respawn](death.md) at home. Get one early.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Tips

* **Ride your BMX.** Use it from your inventory to put it down, and pick it up again when you're done.
* **Look for prompts.** Walk up to things. If you can use them, a small prompt tells you the key. Some things use the third eye (hold **Left Alt**).
* **Know your keys.** They're all on [Controls](controls.md), and you can change most of them.
* **Don't head into a red zone on day one.** You will lose everything you've got.
* **Need help?** Type `/report` and your message to reach staff.
* **Find a crew.** Everything is easier with people watching your back. See [Crews](crews.md).
