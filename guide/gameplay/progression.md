# Progression

There isn't one level bar in LastLife. You get stronger in several ways at once, and each opens different doors. This page shows how they connect. The details are one click away.

## The big picture

| What | How it grows | What it gives you | More |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ⭐ **Rank** (1 to 100) | XP from nearly everything you do | Class tiers at rank 5, 10 and 15 | [Ranks](ranks.md) |
| 🎖️ **Class tier** (0 to 3) | Rises with your rank | Mechanic perks. Other classes' perks aren't working yet | [Classes](classes.md) |
| 💪 **Skills** (0 to 50 each) | Training and doing | Carry weight, stamina, recoil, farming perks and more | [Skills](skills.md) |
| 🔬 **Research** (33 nodes) | Chits, materials and time | Recipes for your whole base | [Research](research.md) |
| 🛠️ **Crafting** | Better benches and recipes | Guns, tools, packs, base pieces | [Crafting](crafting-research.md) |
| 🎨 **Loot and gear** | Where you dare to search | Things you can't craft at all | [Loot & Rarity](loot-rarity.md) |

## How they connect

* **Rank never locks your gear.** A rank 1 survivor can use any weapon they can get hold of. Class recipes are the only exception, and only Mechanics have those.
* **Research belongs to the base**, not to you. Join a crew and you share everything they've learned.
* **Research gets you as far as SMGs.** Rifles and up can't be crafted. They come from red zones, airdrops, crashes and the subway.
* **Chits are the fuel.** Looting, nests, traders and fishing all end up in the research tree.
* **Skills are yours.** They follow you from base to base, but most fade if you stop training.
* **Death doesn't touch your rank, skills or research.** It does cost you what you were carrying. See [Dying](death.md).

## A path from day one to endgame

These are suggestions, not rules.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Day one: get on your feet (rank 1 to 3)

* Take your Stone Hatchet to some trees, and your starting kit with you.
* Make a **Pickaxe**, some Nails and Planks at a safe-zone bench.
* Get a **GPS** so you can see the map.
* Search cupboards, cars and bins. Every fresh spot is XP.
* Stay out of red zones.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### First base (rank 3 to 5)

* Craft a **Totem** and a **Crafting Table**, pick a quiet spot and build in wood. See [Base Building](base-building.md).
* Place a **Smelter** and a **Campfire**.
* Research **Smelting**, **Weapon Upkeep** and **Reloading**. Together that's 150 chits.
* Build your first **Pistol**. It needs no research.
* At **rank 5** your class reaches tier 1.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Settling in (rank 5 to 10)

* **Stonework** for walls that hold, **Mechanics** for lockpicks, repair kits and vehicle parts.
* **Packs** for a Day pack, **Plate Carriers** for your first armour, then plates from a trader.
* Get a [vehicle](vehicles.md) running. [Find a crew](communication.md) if you don't have one.
* **Comforts** for a bed and a garage, then **Fitness** for a gym. Start on Strength.
* At **rank 10** your class reaches tier 2.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Getting serious (rank 10 to 15)

* **Sidearms**, then **Machine Pistols** or **Long Guns**.
* **Metalwork** and **Electricity** for a proper base. See [Power](power.md).
* **Felling** then **Rock Drilling**: the jackhammer doubles every haul and works in half the time.
* Short red-zone runs with your crew for rifles and weapon parts. Watch for [airdrops](airdrops.md) and [crashes](helicopter-crashes.md).
* At **rank 15** your class reaches its last tier. Master Mechanics can now build level 5 parts.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Late game (rank 15 and up)

* **Submachine Guns**, **Heavy SMGs** and **Vehicle Armament**.
* **Industrial**, **Power Tools**, **Demolition**, **Deep Diving**. This is where the chits go.
* Armed [vehicles](vehicle-weapons.md), [boats and helicopters](boats-helicopters.md).
* Other ways to make a living: [moonshine](moonshine.md), [the underground](the-underground.md), [deep-sea fishing](fishing.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Endgame: the subway

* Find a keycard in a red zone, get a gas mask and filters, take a team down.
* Heavy snipers, machine guns, launchers and explosives come from [the subway](subway.md) and nowhere else.
* Getting out is **150 XP**, plus up to **225** more with your crew.
* Then keep climbing to **rank 100**, if only so everyone can see it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Gear at a glance

| Stage | Weapons | Wood and rock | Armour and bags | Base | Car parts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Start** | Melee, basic pistols, double barrel | Stone Hatchet, Pickaxe | Pockets | Wood | None |
| **Early** | Better pistols | Hatchet | Light carrier, Day pack, small pouch | Stone | Level 1 and 2 |
| **Middle** | Pump shotgun, machine pistols, SMG | Hatchet | Ceramic plates | Metal, power | Level 3 (Mechanic) |
| **Late** | Heavy SMGs, red-zone rifles | Jackhammer, Chainsaw | Hiking pack, medium pouch | Industrial | Level 4 (Mechanic tier 2) |
| **Endgame** | Snipers, machine guns, launchers | Dynamite | The same | The same | Level 5, turbo, nitrous (Mechanic tier 3) |

## Tips

* **Do a bit of everything.** Each activity has its own XP cap per minute, so variety pays.
* **Share the research bill.** 6,835 chits is a lot alone, and not much for a crew of five.
* **Save steel.** The whole tree takes 80, and everything good wants it.
* **One Mechanic per crew** saves everyone a lot of materials.
