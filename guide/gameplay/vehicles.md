# Vehicles & Garages

Nobody hands you a car out here. Every working vehicle on the road was a wreck once, until a survivor brought it back to life.

## Your first ride

You start with a **BMX**. **Use** it to put it down. To take it back, look at it and press **E** on **Get this Bicycle**.

For an engine, craft a **50cc Engine** at a safe-zone bench (8 metal scrap, 3 iron, 2 aluminum, 3 rubber, 2 plastic, 1 engine oil) and combine it with your BMX into a **Motorized Bike**. No keys, and it works just like the BMX.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Pick your bike up.** A bike left lying around isn't kept. Put it in your bag before you log off.
{% endhint %}

## Finding and restoring a vehicle

Every restart, broken-down vehicles appear on roadsides, in car parks, at old gas stations and in work yards.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Look at the engine

Walk up to a wreck, face its engine and press **E** on **Restore Vehicle**. You see every part it needs and how many you have.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Gather the parts

Search cars, trash and garages, or craft them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Restore it

The parts are used up and **the vehicle is yours**, ready to drive.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

| Kind | Always needs |
| --- | --- |
| Bike, quad | 6V battery |
| Car | 12V battery, 2 engine oil |
| Truck | 24V battery, 5 engine oil |
| Boat | 12V battery, marine engine |
| Jetski | 6V battery, marine engine |
| Helicopter | 24V battery, 2 aviation parts |

On top of that come tyres, vehicle parts, engine parts and body panels. **Faster and heavier means more.** Armoured vehicles need vehicle armor, armed ones gun mounts too.

### High-tier vehicles

Military vehicles and the worst of the armed ones only turn up at a few of the most dangerous places on the map. Armoured road cars are a rare find elsewhere. Go in with a crew. For their guns, see [Vehicle Weapons](vehicle-weapons.md). For boats and helicopters, see [Boats & Helicopters](boats-helicopters.md).

## Keys and stealing

A vehicle knows who owns it.

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| 🔑 **You** | Drive, lock and park it |
| 👥 **Your crew** | Drive and lock it, if their rank allows |
| 🤝 **Someone you trust** | `/givekeys` gives a key to everyone else in the car, or to the closest player if you're outside next to it |
| 🚫 **Everyone else** | Can't drive it |

Someone else's vehicle is fair game. **Third-eye** a locked one with a **lockpick** to open it, then sit inside and use the lockpick for the **Steal Vehicle** menu. It needs a lockpick and cables (a car takes 6, a truck 10). Armoured and armed vehicles also need a **blowtorch**.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Once stolen, it's theirs.** Park in a garage when you're not driving.
{% endhint %}

## Driving

The keys that matter show along the bottom of your screen while you drive, with **your own** bindings.

| Default key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **B** | Seatbelt |
| **G** | Engine on and off |
| **L** | Lock and unlock (within 5 m) |
| **F6** | Vehicle controls panel |
| **Y** | Cruise control |
| **R** | Reload a vehicle gun |

**F6** opens a panel for seats, engine, lights, hazards, indicators, doors, windows, hood and trunk. You can keep driving with it open. Passengers can open it too, but some controls are for the driver.

Wear your belt. Without it, a crash can throw you through the windscreen.

## Damage

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| 🛞 **Burst tyre** | Carry the right tyre, look at the wheel, press **E** |
| 🔧 **Damaged engine or body** | **Repair Kit** on the engine, or see [Mechanic](mechanic.md) |
| 💥 **Destroyed** | Repair Kit within **2 hours**. After that it's gone for good. |

Crashes hurt vehicles about half as much as you'd expect, but the engine stalls when it drops below 15% and the car won't go until you fix it. **Duct tape** gets it limping again.

## Fuel

Gas stations don't sell fuel any more. You have to scavenge it.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get a jerry can

Find one in a car, or craft it at a workbench.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find gasoline

Search the old **fuel pumps**: look at one and press **E**. Cars have some too.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill the can

Hold the can and **use** Gasoline from your inventory. Each one fills a **quarter** of the can.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill the tank

Can in hand, stand by the fuel cap and press **E**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

A badly damaged tank **leaks**.

## Garages

You can have **10 vehicles** out in the world. Parked ones don't count.

| Garage | Takes | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🚗 **Garage** | Cars, bikes, trucks | Safe zones, or your own **Base Garage** |
| 🚁 **Helipad** | Aircraft | Safe zones, or your own **Base Helipad** |
| ⚓ **Dock** | Boats | On the coast |

Public ones are on your map.

* **Park:** drive into a spot and press **E**. Only the owner can park. Land helicopters first, and repair wrecks first.
* **Take out:** walk up to the garage, press **E**, pick a vehicle and **Pull out**. It comes out on the marked spot with the fuel and damage it had.
* **Parked elsewhere?** Press **Bring here** to move it, free. Kinds don't mix.
* **Docks:** parking puts you back on the dock, taking out puts you straight in the boat.

### At your base

| Piece | Cost | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base Garage** | 80 iron, 8 metal fittings, 20 nails | Comforts |
| **Base Helipad** | 120 iron, 12 metal fittings, 30 nails | Industrial |

Your whole crew can use it, but everyone only sees their own vehicles. If it gets destroyed, your vehicles are safe: bring them out at any garage of the same kind.

## Tips

* **Start small.** A beater teaches you which parts to hoard.
* **Park it or lose it.**
* **Can't fix it here?** Drag it home. See [Towing](towing.md).
