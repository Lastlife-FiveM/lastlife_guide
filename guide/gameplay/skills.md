# Skills & Gym

The world ended, so nobody is going to hand you anything. But you can make yourself harder to kill. You have eight **skills**. Each gets better the more you train it.

Skills are separate from your [rank](ranks.md). Rank is what you've done. Skills are what your body has learned.

## Levels

* Every skill goes from **level 0 to level 50**.
* Every level takes the same: **1,000 skill XP**. Level 50 is 50,000.
* For all skills except Farming, every level gives an equal share of the bonus. Level 25 gives half.

## What each skill gives

| Skill | Every level | Level 25 | Level 50 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 💪 **Strength** | +0.3 kg carry weight | +7.5 kg | **+15 kg** (on top of the normal 120 kg) |
| 🏃 **Condition** | +1% stamina, 0.5% slower sprint drain | +25%, 12.5% | **+50% stamina, 25% slower drain** |
| 🫁 **Lung capacity** | +1.2 seconds under water | +30 seconds | **+60 seconds** (everyone starts with 15) |
| 🎯 **Shooting** | 0.8% less recoil | 20% | **40% less recoil** |
| 🥊 **Toughness** | +0.5% melee damage, 0.3% less melee damage taken | +12.5%, 7.5% | **+25% dealt, 15% less taken** |
| 🤸 **Agility** | 0.7% less fall damage | 17.5% | **35% less fall damage** |
| 🎣 **Fishing** | 0.6% quicker bites, 0.8% kinder fights, 1.2% better odds of rare fish, 1% chance to keep each piece of tackle when the line snaps | half of the next column | **30% / 40% / 60% / 50%** |
| 🌾 **Farming** | A new perk every 5 levels, see below | 6 perks | **All 10 perks** (the last comes at level 45) |

The numbers are the skill XP you actually get.

### In the gym

| Workout | Trains | XP per rep | Seconds per rep | Reps in a set |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pull-up Bar | Strength | 84 | 3.5 | 10 |
| Weight Bench | Strength | 72 | 3 | 12 |
| Squat Rack | Strength | 72 | 3 | 12 |
| Dumbbell Rack | Strength | 54 | 2.5 | 15 |
| Push-ups (no gear) | Strength | 36 | 2.5 | 15 |
| Treadmill | Condition | 60 | 3 | 20 |
| Punching Bag | Toughness | 60 | 2.5 | 15 |
| Yoga Mat | Agility | 72 | 4 | 10 |
| Sit-ups (no gear) | Agility | 36 | 2.5 | 15 |

### Out in the world

| What you do | Trains | XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint | Condition | 3 a second |
| Swim under water | Lung capacity | 6 a second |

| Land a fish | [Fishing](fishing.md) | About 24 to 960 for an average catch. Rarer fish pay more, and a big one of its kind pays more than a small one |

| Milk a cow | Farming | 30 |
| Fill a feed or water trough | Farming | 9 |
| Breed animals | Farming | 90 |
| Hatch a chick | Farming | 36 |
| Butcher an animal | Farming | 45 |

## Building a gym

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get the equipment

Research **Comforts**, then **Fitness**, and craft the gear at your workbench. See [Research](research.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Place it at your base

It goes down like any other [base](base-building.md) piece.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Use it

Walk up to it and use it to start your workout. A training bar shows your skill, your reps and your energy. The Target Dummy works differently: just shoot it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Stop when you want

Press **E** to stop. Walking off, getting in a vehicle, being knocked over or dying also ends it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Old gyms are worth a visit.** You can't train on equipment you find out in the world, but you can strip it for materials.
{% endhint %}

## Energy, food and water

* **Energy.** You have 100. Reps use 4 to 8 each. Run out and you have to rest until you're back to 40, which takes about 20 seconds. It comes back at 2 a second while you're not training.
* **Food and water.** Every finished set costs **2 food and 2 water**. Below **10** of either, you can't train. See [Staying Alive](survival.md).

## Farming perks

| Perk | Farming level | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | Plant and harvest crops, keep hens |
| 2 | 5 | +10% crop yield |
| 3 | 10 | Keep pigs, compost works 25% faster |
| 4 | 15 | Weeds grow 25% slower |
| 5 | 20 | Keep cows, and a chance of one extra egg when you collect |
| 6 | 25 | +20% crop yield (replaces the +10%) |
| 7 | 30 | Breeding needs half the feed |
| 8 | 35 | Better chance to get seeds back when you harvest |
| 9 | 40 | Animals lose health 30% slower |
| 10 | 45 | +30% crop yield (replaces the +20%), milk every 90 minutes |

For compost, weeds and animal health, the perks of the **last person who tended it** count. See [Cooking & Farming](cooking-farming.md).

## Use it or lose it

Skills **fade** if you stop training them.

* Fading starts after **4 days** without training that skill.
* After that you lose **half a level a day**.
* It never drops below **level 5**. A skill below 5 doesn't fade at all.
* **Farming never fades.**

Time you're offline counts too. Any training at all starts the 4 days over.

## The Skills page

Press **Esc** and choose **Skills**. Every skill has a card with its level, what it gives you now, where to train it and whether it's fading. The **Class** tab shows your [class](classes.md) tiers.

## Tips

* **Strength first.** More carry weight helps with everything.
* **Eat before you train.**
* **Sprint everywhere.** Around five and a half minutes of sprinting is a Condition level.
* **Touch every skill twice a week** and nothing fades.
