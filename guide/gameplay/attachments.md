# Attachments

Hold the weapon and **use** the attachment. Take it off again from the weapon's menu in your inventory. One of each kind: a muzzle, a sight, a magazine and so on.

## Known from the start

Made at a workbench, no research.

| Attachment | Fits | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tactical Flashlight** | Most guns | 2 plastic, 1 electronics, 1 lithium |
| **Suppressor** | Pistols | 2 iron, 1 gun parts |
| **Extended Pistol Clip** | Pistols | 2 iron, 1 gun parts |

## Weapon charms

Bottle caps, teddy bears, lucky dice, dog tags: small things you find in loot, or buy from [traders](traders.md). They do nothing but look good.

* Hold a weapon and **use** the charm, then place it where you like.
* They swing as you move, and bells and tags jingle when you sprint.
* They fit every weapon except throwables and cans.
* `/charmposition` moves the charm on the weapon in your hands.
