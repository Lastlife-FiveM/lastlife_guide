# Companions

Out here a good dog is worth more than a good gun. It walks at your side, bites what comes at you, growls at the dead you can't see and sniffs out the bins nobody has emptied yet. Look after it and it gets braver and stronger the longer you stay together.

You can have **one companion per character**.

## Getting a dog

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Buy a pup from Hollis

**Hollis**, the K9 handler at the **Sandy Shores** camp, sells pups for chits. Not every kind is on offer every restart, and prices swing. See [Traders](traders.md).

| Kind | Health | Good at | Usual price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pug | 140 | Small and weak, no pack | 90 |
| Poodle | 140 | Small, no pack | 110 |
| Westie | 140 | Small, quick nose, no pack | 120 |
| Retriever | 260 | Best nose: Seek reaches 50 m | 190 |
| Husky | 260 | Fastest | 210 |
| German Shepherd | 300 | Strong all-rounder | 240 |
| Mastiff | 320 | Big and tough | 260 |
| Rottweiler | 340 | Hardest bite, most health | 300 |
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the crate

You get a **pup crate**. **Use** it and give your pup a name (2 to 16 letters). It is now yours. Crates can be traded before they are opened.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Whistle

**Hold G** to open the whistle wheel and pick **Come back**. Your dog appears beside you, and a dot on your map shows you where it is.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The other way to get a pup is to **breed** one (see below).

## The whistle wheel

**Hold G** (default, change it in the pause menu under **Keybinds**). Move the mouse onto a command and **let go**: you whistle and the dog does it. **Right click** cancels. While the wheel is open your camera stops turning.

| Command | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Follow | 1 | Walks with you, runs when you run |
| Come back | 1 | Runs to you. Also calls your dog when it's not with you |
| Stay / Lie down | 1 | Sits or lies where it is |
| Dismiss | 1 | Trots off home |
| Alert | 1 | Zombie warnings on or off. Off to start with |
| Heel | 2 | Close at your side |
| Quiet | 2 | No barking for 5 minutes |
| Attack zombies | 3 | Goes for any zombie within 12 m of you, until you tell it otherwise |
| Seek | 3 | Finds a container nobody searched, or a wild plant |
| Guard here | 4 | Holds the spot. At your base it guards the base |

A **grey** slice tells you why: not loyal enough yet, too hungry, muzzled, or healing.

### Attack a player

At **level 5**, aim a gun at a player up to 40 m away. A hint shows by your crosshair. Press **G** and your dog goes for them.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Attack player** never works in a **safe zone** or in the **subway extraction zone** (for you or the target). Everywhere else the normal rules apply. See [Zones & PvP](zones.md). Setting your dog on people for no reason gets it taken away by staff. Every use is logged.
{% endhint %}

## Loyalty

Loyalty grows by playing together, up to **150 a day**:

* **Feeding** it when its hunger is under 60: +12 (4 times a day)
* **Time together** while it's out: +3 every 5 minutes (not while you're AFK)
* A **zombie killed together** (within 15 m): +5
* **Seek** finding something: +8
* **Petting** it: +3 (3 times a day)

| Level | Loyalty | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| 1 New | 0 | normal |
| 2 Trusting | 150 | +5% |
| 3 Loyal | 350 | +20%, runs 8% faster |
| 4 Devoted | 600 | +35%, runs 16% faster |
| 5 Bonded | 900 | +50%, runs 24% faster |

Strength means a harder bite and more health.

## Food and health

The **companion card** on your HUD shows health and hunger while your dog is out. You can move it in the HUD editor. Hunger drops **6 an hour** while it is with you. Feed it from the pause menu, by facing it (**Feed**), or by using the food in your inventory.

| Food | Hunger |
|---|---|
| Dog Food | +40 |
| Raw Meat | +20 |
| Cooked Game | +25 |
| Raw Game | +15 |
| Fish Scraps | +12 |

* **Dog Food**: cook it at a lit campfire or hobo stove: 1 raw meat or raw game + 2 food scraps makes 3, or 3 fish scraps + 1 food scraps makes 2. Hollis sells it too, and swaps it for fish scraps.
* **Fish Scraps**: you get them when you fillet a fish.
* Under 15 hunger your dog won't attack, guard or seek. A starving dog loses health and 10 loyalty an hour, but hunger alone never kills it.
* Health comes back by itself while hunger is over 50.

## When your dog goes down

At 0 health your dog **goes down**. You (or anyone) have **2 minutes** to save it: face the body and **hold E** for 8 seconds with a **Vet Kit**. It gets back up with 100 health.

If nobody does, it goes to the camp vet for **1 hour** (real time, it counts while you're offline) and loses **100 loyalty**. Its pack keeps everything.

A Vet Kit used on a hurt (not downed) dog heals 150 health. Make Vet Kits at a workbench (2 bandages, 1 painkillers, 1 cloth) or buy them from Hollis or June.

## When you go down

A **level 5** dog can save your life. Put an **Adrenaline Shot** in its pack (workbench: 2 painkillers, 1 plastic, 1 glass). When you go down within 80 m, your dog runs over, injects you and you get up with a **quarter of your health**. It uses up the shot and works once every **10 minutes**.

## Growling and barking

Your dog hears the dead before you do. Switch on **Alert** on the wheel first. When a zombie is **behind you or out of sight**, it growls, a **yellow arrow** around your crosshair points that way, and then it **barks**. It hears them up to 35 m away.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Barking is **noise**: zombies within 30 m hear it. Use **Quiet** (5 minutes) or put a **Dog Muzzle** on. A muzzled dog can't bite either.
{% endhint %}

## Seek

At level 3, whistle **Seek**. Your dog runs to the nearest container **nobody has searched yet** (or a wild plant), sits next to it and barks once. Most search 35 to 45 m around, a Retriever 50 m. One Seek a minute.

## Dog pack

Big dogs can wear a **Dog Pack** (workbench: 2 hide, 4 cloth, 1 chain): **10 slots, 12 kg**. Open it next to your dog or from the pause menu. It has to be empty before you take it off.

## Guarding your base

At level 4, whistle **Guard here**. Anywhere, it holds the spot and bites zombies within 15 m. Inside your base (or a crewmate's), it guards the base. Your dog **stays there** when you leave. It bites zombies that come close and barks at anyone who isn't in your crew. You get a message wherever you are. Hunger drops at half speed while it guards. Whistle **Come back** to end it.

## The kennel

Build a **Dog Kennel** at a workbench (10 planks, 4 nails, 2 cloth) and use **Interact** on it. You and your crew can all use the same kennel, but **each of you has your own 6 pens** and only sees your own dogs.

* Only **one dog is with you** at a time: your companion. Bring it to the kennel and press **Put in**. It keeps everything: name, loyalty, health, stats. Its pack has to be empty.
* **Take out** a kept dog (with no companion out) and it appears right beside you as your companion.
* Kept dogs get hungry at **a quarter of the speed**. They eat **Dog Food** and **Fish Scraps** from the kennel's **food bowl** on their own. The bowl is shared by your crew. With an empty bowl a starving dog slowly loses loyalty, but it never dies.
* **Feed** a kept dog from your pockets, **Rename** it, or **Release** it for good (click twice).
* A pup crate opened at your kennel while you have a companion out goes straight into a free pen.
* If the kennel is picked up or destroyed, your dogs go to your **Lost dogs** list. Collect them at any kennel you can open.

## Stats (genes)

Every dog has five stats in points, on top of what its breed is good at:

| Stat | Each point gives |
|---|---|
| Health | +3% max health |
| Bite | +2.5% bite damage |
| Speed | +0.6% run speed |
| Nose | +2% Seek range |
| Appetite | hunger drops 1.5% slower |

Pups from Hollis roll **0 to 10** in each stat. Stats are genes (you breed for them); loyalty is training. You see them on the kennel screen, in the pause menu (Companion page) and on a bred pup crate. Each breed also has a few **coats** (colours), passed on from a parent.

## Breeding

Pick **two of your own dogs of the same breed** in the same kennel, both **level 3+** and **well fed** (hunger 70+), on the kennel's **Breeding** tab. It shows the odds before you start:

* For each stat the pup gets the **higher** parent's value 55% of the time, the lower one 45%.
* **7%** chance of a **mutation**: +2 points on one random stat (it can go past 10), or now and then a new coat. A family line stops mutating after 20 mutations.
* **10%** chance of **twins**.

After **5 hours** you get a message: the pup crate is ready at the kennel. Take it from the Breeding tab. Each parent then rests for 48 hours. Taking a parent out while it breeds loses the pup.

## Good to know

* Your dog rides in cars and boats with a free seat. On a bike, or with no free seat, it waits and follows when you get off.
* Dogs **can't go into the subway** (the gas). Your dog waits at the entrance.
* The **Companion** page in the pause menu shows everything: loyalty, needs, food, the pack, the muzzle, the genes, breeding. You can **rename** your dog once a day, or give it up for good.
