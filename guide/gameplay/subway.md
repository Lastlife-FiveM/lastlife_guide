# The Subway (Extraction)

Something is seeping up from under the city. The subway tunnels are flooded with toxic gas, packed with the dead, and stashed with gear you won't find anywhere else. Go in, grab what you can, and find a way out before the gas or the other survivors get you.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The subway is Kill On Sight.** No rules, no roleplay protection. Anyone you meet down there may shoot you for your bag.
{% endhint %}

## What you need

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 🎫 **Transit Access Card** | One per team. Opens a station door and is used up when the team starts. It only comes from the **red zones**: off the dead, and most often off bosses. |
| 😷 **Gas Mask** | Every member must **wear one in the Mask slot**. No mask, no entry. |
| 🧪 **Gas Filters** | Every member needs at least one. It's used as you go in and lasts **10 minutes**, so bring spares. |
| 💊 **Antidote** | Not needed to get in, but you may want it after. See **Infection** below. |

**June** at the Galileo Observatory sometimes sells masks, filters and antidotes. See [Traders](traders.md).

## Getting in

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find an open entrance

Each restart opens **two** stations as entrances and **two** as exits. Open entrances show on your map. The tunnels show too, as a dark red band under the roads.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Form a team

Stand at the **terminal** by the door and press **E**. Create a team or join one, up to **6 people**. The leader can invite anyone nearby; an invite lasts **30 seconds**. Stay within about 10 metres of the terminal or you drop off the team. If the leader walks off, the team ends.

Going alone is fine: a team of one works.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put the card in and start

The leader puts a Transit Access Card in and presses **Start**. Everyone must be wearing a mask and carrying a filter. The card is only taken when you start.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Walk through

The door stays open for **15 seconds**. Walk through and your run begins. Miss it and you're off the team. Anyone who isn't on the team gets thrown back out.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Inside

You have **45 minutes**. A card appears on your HUD. Keep an eye on it.

| On the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Run time** | How long you have left before the gas gets you, filter or not. |
| **Filter** | How long your current filter has left. |
| **Spare filters** | How many filters you're carrying. |
| **Exposed items** | How many marked items you're carrying. See **Found items are marked** below. |
| **Open exits** | The stations you can leave from. |

### Swapping filters

Your filter only runs down while your mask is on. When it's getting low, **use** a gas filter from your inventory. It takes **4 seconds** and throws away whatever was left in the old one.

### Tougher dead

The zombies down here have about **two and a half times** their usual health. Bring more ammo than you think you need.

## Infection

If your mask is off, your filter runs out, or you stay past your run time, the gas gets in and your **infection** starts to climb.

| Infection | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **25%** | You start coughing and everything goes hazy. |
| **50%** | Your stamina never goes above 40%. |
| **75%** | You lose health every second while you're inside, and your vision blurs. |
| **100%** | You die. |

Without working air it climbs **12% a minute**: a little over 8 minutes from clean to dead.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Infection doesn't wear off.** It stays with you after you leave the tunnels, until you take an **Antidote**. Only dying of it also clears it. Antidotes can be found in the red zones and in medical airdrops, bought from June when she has one, or crafted once you've learned Field Medicine.
{% endhint %}

## Loot

Search what the army left behind, and the zombies you put down. This is where the heaviest gear turns up:

* **Explosives:** grenades, pipe bombs, sticky bombs and mines.
* **Heavy weapons:** heavy snipers, marksman rifles, machine guns and automatic shotguns.
* **The best scopes,** night vision and thermal included.
* **Weapon parts, heavy ammo** and the odd spare filter.

A crate fills up again after about **30 minutes**.

### Found items are marked

Anything you pick up inside, from a crate, a body, the floor or another player, is **marked as exposed**. You'll see hazard stripes on it in your inventory.

* **You can carry it** in your pockets and backpack.
* **You can't wear or equip it.** A gun you find can't go in your weapon slot, and marked ammo can't be loaded. You can't use found gear down here.
* **It won't stack** with your own clean items. Put it in an empty slot.

To keep marked items, you have to **decontaminate** them on the way out.

## Getting out

You can only leave through one of the **open exits**. They're on your HUD card and posted in chat when you go in. Normally that's never the station you came in by. Any other station you find is sealed.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Use the decontamination locker

Inside your exit station, look at the **Decontamination locker** and press **E**. It's your own private locker, with 30 slots and room for 60 kg. Put every marked item you want to keep in it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the exit

Press **\[E] Open exit**. If you're still carrying marked items, you'll get a warning listing them. Choose **Go back** to sort them out, or **Leave anyway**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hold the door

An alarm goes off for **30 seconds** while the door opens, and **every zombie within about 120 metres hears it**. Someone on a run has to stay within about 6 metres of the door. If nobody does, it stops and you have to start again.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Step outside

The door opens for only a few seconds. Walk out, and you've made it. You earn **150 XP** for getting out alive, plus **75 XP** for each crewmate (up to 3) who got out within 3 minutes of you.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Collect your loot

At the **Collection locker** outside that same station, use **Collect your loot** to pick up everything you put in the decontamination locker. It's clean now, and yours to use. You can only take things out of it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Marked items you carry out are lost.** Anything still marked when you step outside stays behind. Always use the decontamination locker first.
{% endhint %}

### What you keep

* ✅ **Your own gear** that you went in with: weapons, backpack, armour, everything you had equipped.
* ✅ **Clean items** in your pockets and backpack.
* ✅ **Anything you put through decontamination**, from the collection locker.
* ❌ **Marked items still on you** when you step out.

### Logging out inside

Logging out down there is risky. If you come back inside the tunnels, your run carries on. If you come back anywhere else, every marked item on you is destroyed.

## Dying down there

If you die in the tunnels, everything you carry drops on your body, and **anyone can search it** straight away. Get revived where you fell and your run carries on. Respawn anywhere else and it's over. See [Dying & Your Body](death.md).

## Tips

* **Count your filters.** Running out halfway back to an exit is how most runs end.
* **Go with a crew.** One card gets the whole team in, and you get bonus XP for getting out together.
* **Know your exits before you loot.** Don't wander so deep you can't get back out in time.
* **Don't get greedy.** A run where you get out with half a bag beats a run where you die with a full one.
* **Bring an antidote,** or have one waiting at home.
