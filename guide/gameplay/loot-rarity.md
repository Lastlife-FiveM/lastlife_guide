# Loot & Rarity

Not everything you find is worth carrying home. This page tells you what the colours mean, which gear beats which, and where each tier comes from.

## Rarity

From most common to rarest:

| Scrap | Salvaged | Scarce | Hoarded | Relic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Everyday stuff, or no label | Made with some know-how | Hard to come by | Late-game gear | The very best |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Rarity is a label, not a stat.** It shows how hard something is to get. A Scarce pistol you crafted shoots exactly like the same pistol found in a wreck with no label on it. What matters is **which item** it is.
{% endhint %}

A few things to know:

* Items you **craft** get their recipe's rarity. Plain Scrap adds no label, so crafted Scrap stacks with looted items.
* The same item with and without a label **won't stack** together.
* **Fish** use the same five names, and there it does matter: rarer fish bite less often and sell for more. See [Fishing](fishing.md).
* **Weapon charms** have a rarity too. It's only for looks.

### 🦺 Armour

A plate carrier does nothing by itself. It's the **plates** you slide into it that give armour, and they wear down as you get hit.

| Carrier | Plates it holds | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light carrier | 2 | Plate Carriers research |

| Plate | Armour each | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ceramic plate | 20 | Traders |

So a Light carrier with two fresh Ceramic plates gives you **40 armour**.

## Zone tiers

Where you search decides what you can find.

| Tier | Zone | What's there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | 🛡️ Safe zones | Nothing. Looting is off. |
| **1** | 🌍 Open world | Materials, food, parts, tires, fuel, basic ammo and, rarely, a basic pistol. |
| **2** | 🔴 Red zones | Everything from tier 1, **plus** rifles, strong pistols, heavier shotguns, rifle attachments, weapon parts, gas grenades, level 2 car parts, parachutes, bigger packs, antidotes and **subway keycards**. Bosses carry the best of it, and nests drop weapon parts and chits. |
| **3** | 🚇 The subway | The top of everything: heavy snipers, machine guns, launchers, explosives, night and thermal scopes, vehicle ammo. |

The risk goes up with it. See [Zones & PvP](zones.md).

## How loot comes back

* Most searched spots refill after **15 minutes**. Zombie bodies roll their own loot.
* Petrol pumps take **45 minutes**, and electrical equipment takes **60**.
* Subway crates take **30 minutes**.
* World loot within **150 metres of a base totem** can't be searched. Bodies still can.
* Searching a fresh spot gives [rank XP](ranks.md).

## Tips

* **Know the prop.** Workshops have tools, cars have car parts, kitchens have kitchen stuff. Nobody keeps a rifle in a bin.
* **Strip what you don't want.** Some pistols and rifles can go in the [recycler](crafting-research.md) for weapon parts.
* **Getting it home is half the job.** In a red zone, anyone can search your body at once.
