# Moonshine & Brewing

Out in the hills a man called **Cass Dalton** runs shine. He sells the kit and buys what you make. The hard part is the still: the first drops out of it are poison, and only you can tell when they stop.

| Drink | The road |
| --- | --- |
| 🥃 **Moonshine** | Mash, ferment, distil, proof it if you like |
| 🍺 **Beer** | Malt the wheat, boil the wort, ferment, bottle |
| 🍓 **Fruit moonshine** | Press fruit, steep it in moonshine, proof it |

## Finding Cass

Every restart he sets up somewhere else, and you only see him when you're close. Some **rumour notes** from loot are about him, with one to three hints for where he is *now*. A note from before a restart has gone cold. Walk up to him and press **E** on **Trade**.

## Building the distillery

Put a blueprint in your base **workbench's blueprint slot** to craft its stations, then place them at your base. You and your crew can use them, one person per station.

* **Moonshine Kit:** Mash Kettle, Fermenter, Pot Still
* **Brewer's Kit:** Fruit Press, Bottling Table, Malt Screen (fits in the kettle), Fermenter Heater

Work is done in hands-on scenes with the mouse. **Backspace** cancels, usually with nothing used.

## Step 1: the mash

| Recipe | Goes in (plus 4 water, 2 logs) | Comes out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Corn mash** | 8 corn, 2 sugar | 4 pails, a little better quality |
| **Wheat mash** | 8 wheat, 2 sugar | 4 pails |
| **Junk mash** | 6 rotten food, 2 sugar | 4 pails, fuel only |
| **Beer wort** | 4 malted wheat, 2 hops | 4 pails |

One coal counts as 2 logs. You need 4 empty pails to drain it.

1. Open the lid, drag the water jug over the pot and click to stop in the green. Light the fire, tip in the grain.
2. Hold it between **63 and 68 °C** for **25 seconds** with the damper: LOW, MID, HIGH. The heat lags, so think ahead. Past 75 it scorches.
3. Add the sugar, close the lid, let it cool for a minute, drain it into pails.

Don't leave it in the kettle for more than 6 hours or it goes sour.

## Step 2: ferment

The fermenter has **4 carboys**. Each takes **2 mash pails and 1 yeast** and gives 2 wash pails after **15 minutes**. Beer takes **40 minutes**. Sometimes you get the yeast back. Left for **3 hours** after it's ready, it turns sour and loses 25 quality.

A **Fermenter Heater**, switched on with [base power](power.md), makes every carboy half as fast again.

## Step 3: the still

**2 to 4 wash pails**, 3 logs and glass jars. A full run is about 1.5 jars per pail, and roughly half of that is good.

Pour the wash in, lock the cap, light the fire. Hold **Space** to work the bellows and keep the heat in the green. **E** turns up the cold water if the tub gets hot. The spirit comes in four parts:

| Part | Vapour | Smell | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☠️ **Foreshots** | 64 to 78 °C | Sharp, like nail polish | Catch them in the cup, then **tip it into the slop bucket** |
| 🟠 **Heads** | 78 to 82 °C | Hot, solventy | Press **R**: its own jar. Ethanol |
| 🟢 **Hearts** | 82 to 91 °C | Clean, sweet grain | Press **R**: your moonshine. Click a new jar when one is full |
| 🔵 **Tails** | 91 to 98 °C | Wet cardboard, oily | Press **R**: ethanol. **Enter** ends the run |

The parts never end at the same moment twice. Read the temperature and the smell, not the clock.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Foreshots are methanol.** Keep them, or let them run into a hearts jar, and that moonshine **poisons** whoever drinks it. The label won't tell you. Cass can tell, and pays half.
{% endhint %}

Too much heat makes the still **puke**: worse moonshine, and loud. It's noisy anyway: zombies within about **25 metres** come to look, **45** when it pukes.

### Ethanol

Put **2 ethanol** back into your next run for a bit more moonshine at slightly lower quality, or sell it to Cass. A **fuel run** turns any wash, even junk, into ethanol only.

## Beer

1. **Steep** 6 wheat and 2 water in a carboy for **30 minutes**: green malt.
2. **Kiln** 6 green malt in the kettle with the Malt Screen and 1 log. Hold **40 to 55 °C** for 20 seconds and click the grain to turn it when it asks. A clean kiln gives **6 malted wheat**. Scorching it or missing a turn costs you some.
3. **Wort:** mash as above, then bring it to a boil. Drag in one hop bundle at the **start** (bittering) and one at the **end** (aroma). Bad timing costs up to 20 quality.
4. **Ferment** for 40 minutes: beer pails.
5. **Bottle** at the Bottling Table: **6 bottles per pail**. Each needs an empty bottle and a cap.

Grow your own hops and wheat: see [Cooking & Farming](cooking-farming.md).

## Fruit moonshine

1. **Press** fruit at the Fruit Press: 6 blueberries, 4 strawberries or 1 melon fill one jar. Turn the handle up to **3 times**. A fourth turn makes it bitter.
2. **Steep** 2 moonshine and 1 jar of fruit mash in a carboy for **20 minutes**: 2 jars.

## Proofing

At the Bottling Table you can water spirits down: **50%** for moonshine, **40%** for fruit moonshine. Hold the tap and stop on the mark. You get more jars out of it and **+8 quality** for hitting it within 2%. Too much water and it's weak.

## Drinking it

Each drink adds to how drunk you are, stronger drinks more. It wears off by itself.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Tipsy** | 5% less damage taken |
| **Drunk** | 10% less damage, a little slower, the world sways |
| **Hammered** | 15% less damage, slower still, shaky aim, you may trip when you sprint |
| **Too much** | You **black out** for 15 seconds |

* Drink also keeps you warm: less stamina lost in the rain and at night.
* **Beer** fills you up a little and heals a little. **Fruit moonshine** helps your stamina: blueberry the most, strawberry heals, melon quenches thirst.
* After a heavy session comes the **hangover**, up to **20 minutes** of slow stamina and extra thirst. Badly cut moonshine makes it half as long again.
* You get the empty jar or bottle back.

{% hint style="danger" %}
A poisoned jar does nothing for **two minutes**. Then your health drains, and a bad one turns everything dark. An **antidote** saves you.
{% endhint %}
