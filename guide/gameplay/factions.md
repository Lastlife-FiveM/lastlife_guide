# Factions

Nobody is in charge any more, but four places still hold. Each one has its own people and its own trade.

| Safe zone | Who you'll meet | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Galileo Observatory** | Margo, June | Selling scrap, medical supplies |
| **Sandy Shores** | Dusty, Hollis, Sgt. Pryce | Vehicle supplies, dogs, ammo |
| **Elysian Island** | Rook, Salt, Old Toller | Fishing and diving gear, selling your catch, odd swaps |
| **Altruist Camp** | Brother Amos, Sister Wren, Jessop | Seeds, building supplies, moonshine |

{% hint style="info" %}
**You don't join them.** There are no sides to pick and no standing to earn with the safe zones. Everyone is welcome at all four, and every survivor gets the same offers.
{% endhint %}

## Who does remember you

Two people outside the safe zones keep track of you. Trade with them and they trust you with more:

* **Moss**, in [The Underground](the-underground.md)
* **Cass Dalton**, in [Moonshine & Brewing](moonshine.md)

## Your own side

The only side out here is the one you make. Start a [crew](crews.md) and hold your own ground.
