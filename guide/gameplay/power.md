# Power

The lights are off because nobody is keeping them on. Four generators at the **Palmer-Taylor power station** feed the whole map. They need parts, fuel and people who care. Anyone can repair them.

## How it works

The plant stores **energy**, up to **10,000**. Each district's lights come on once there's enough of it, and go off again when it drops.

| District | Lights on at |
| --- | --- |
| **Middle:** Sandy Shores, Vinewood Hills, Palmer-Taylor, Chumash | 2,500 |
| **Los Santos:** downtown, Vespucci, the airport, Elysian Island | 5,000 |
| **East Los Santos:** La Mesa, El Burro Heights, Murrieta, the east port | 7,000 |
| **North:** Paleto Bay, Grapeseed, Mount Chiliad | 9,000 |

* The map **uses 1 energy every second**, all the time.
* Each **working generator makes 3 every second**.

| Working generators | Net per second | Empty to full |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +2 | About 1 hour 23 minutes |
| 2 | +5 | About 33 minutes |
| 3 | +8 | About 21 minutes |
| 4 | +11 | About 15 minutes |

With nothing running, a full store lasts a little under **2 hours 50 minutes**. The energy, the parts and the fuel are all kept through restarts.

## Checking on it

There's a laptop at the **Galileo Observatory**. Look at it and press **E** (default) on **Check Generators Status** to see every generator's parts and fuel. The four generators are also marked on your map.

When the last one goes down, everyone gets a warning.

## Repairing a generator

A generator runs when **all seven parts** work and there's **fuel** in it. Each repair uses up one item.

| Part | Needs | How many per generator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fuse 1 and 2** | Industrial Fuse | 2 |
| **Motor** | Generator Motor | 1 |
| **Pipes** | Pipe Set | 1 |
| **Pump 1, 2 and 3** | Fuel Pump | 3 |
| **Fuel** | Jerry can | 1 |

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to a generator

They stand at Palmer-Taylor. Only the parts that are broken show a prompt.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pick what to fix

Press **E** (default) on it with the item on you. Parts start with a quick two-step key check on **W, A, S, D**. If you miss, you keep the item.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Hold your ground

A part takes **20 seconds** and refuelling takes **12**. You can't move, fight or cancel, so clear the area first.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

One jerry can fills the tank, whatever is left in the can. A full tank runs a generator for about **5 and a half hours**.

## Making the parts

All four are made at a **workbench**, with no research needed.

| Part | Materials | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Industrial Fuse** | 2 copper, 1 glass, 1 plastic, 1 cables | 10 s |
| **Pipe Set** | 3 steel, 4 metal scrap, 2 rubber | 30 s |
| **Fuel Pump** | 3 iron, 2 aluminum, 2 rubber, 2 copper, 1 electronics | 40 s |
| **Generator Motor** | 6 copper, 4 iron, 2 steel, 2 cables, 1 electronics | 60 s |

See [Crafting & Research](crafting-research.md).

## Getting faster

Each repair teaches you that part. Fuses and the motor give **10 XP**, pipes and pumps **15**, fuel **5**. Every part counts on its own (Fuse 1 and Fuse 2 are separate).

| XP in that part | Repair time |
| --- | --- |
| 100 | 2% faster |
| 500 | 10% faster |
| 1,200 | 25% faster |
| 2,000 | 50% faster |

## Breakdowns

Nothing lasts. Every so often there's a chance that one part of a running generator fails. It stops until someone replaces that part. Going round with spare fuses keeps the lights on.

{% hint style="info" %}
Two working generators are plenty to fill the store. The other two are your safety net.
{% endhint %}
