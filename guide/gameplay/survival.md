# Staying Alive

Zombies aren't the only thing out to get you. Hunger, thirst, blood loss and broken bones will put you down just as fast. Keep an eye on your HUD and always carry the basics.

## Hunger and thirst

Every **5 minutes** you lose **4.2%** food and **3.8%** water. From full, that's about two hours. A finished gym set takes 2% of each on top.

{% hint style="danger" %}
When either one is empty, you start **losing health**.
{% endhint %}

## Water

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find empty bottles

Search bins and vending machines. They turn up all the time.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill them

Take them to a lake or river, or use a **Water Well** at a base.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Or make it

Turn an empty bottle into Water at the Observatory bench, or at a lit campfire or stove.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Water and Sprunk each give **20%** thirst.

### Hunting

Kill an animal, then search it with a **cutting weapon** in hand: a knife, dagger, switchblade, machete, hatchet, stone hatchet or battle axe. You get raw meat and hide.

| Animal | Raw meat |
| --- | --- |
| Cow | Raw Beef |
| Deer | Raw Venison |
| Pig, boar | Raw Pork |
| Chicken, birds | Raw Poultry |
| Most others | Raw Game Meat |

Cook it first. Recipes, farming and the kitchen are on [Cooking & Farming](cooking-farming.md).

## Getting hurt

Hits can do more than take health.

* **Bleeding** drains you and gets worse with time. Moving makes it worse.
* **Leg injuries** make you limp and can make you fall, mostly when you run.
* **Arm injuries** get in the way of aiming and shooting.
* **Head injuries** mess with your sight.

## Medical

| Item | What it does | Takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🩹 **Bandage** | Stops bleeding and gives back a little health | 4 s |
| 💊 **Painkillers** | Hides the effects of your injuries for 1 minute. Stacks up to 3 | 3 s |
| 🦴 **Splint** | Heals injured arms and legs. Stand still for it | 6 s |
| 🧰 **Individual First Aid Kit** | Half your health bar, works like a painkiller, and may ease bleeding | 3 s |
| ⛑️ **First Aid** | Gets a **downed survivor** back up. Use it next to them | 30 to 60 s |
| 💉 **Antidote** | Clears infection | 3 s |

## Infection

The air in the [subway](subway.md) is poison. Without a working gas mask filter, infection climbs **12% a minute**.

| Infection | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 25% | Coughing and haze |
| 50% | Heavier haze, and your stamina is capped |
| 75% | Blurred sight. Inside the tunnels you lose health |

The cough, the haze and the short breath follow you back out. Only an **Antidote** clears it.

## Tips

* **Never leave without water and bandages.** They're cheap, and they'll save your life.
* **Carry spare empty bottles.** Fill them at the next lake you pass.
* **Stop, then bandage.** Running while you bleed costs you more blood.
* **Splint, then run.** A bad leg drops you in front of a horde.
* **One First Aid per group.** Make sure someone in your crew has one.
