# Sea Life

The dead don't own the sea. Swim, dive or take a boat out and you'll find it full of life, and some of it is hungry.

## What lives where

| Animal | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Fish schools** | All over, close to shore and out in the open ocean |
| **Stingrays** | The sea floor and the shallows |
| **Dolphins** | Along the coast |
| **Sharks:** hammerhead, tiger and great white | Deep open ocean |
| **Humpback and killer whales** | The deepest open ocean, near the surface |

## Sharks

Sharks only live out in the deep, but out there they're common.

* A shark goes for **anyone swimming within about 30 metres**. Boats are safe to sit in.
* A bite costs you health, and it **grabs you**. For about **3 seconds** you're in its mouth and can't do anything but look around.
* Once it lets go, you have about **6 seconds** before one can grab you again. Use them.

{% hint style="danger" %}
If you've been bitten once, get out of the water. A second grab usually comes before you can heal.
{% endhint %}

## Cutting them up

Swim up to a dead animal with a **cutting weapon in your hand** (knife, dagger, hatchet, stone hatchet, machete, switchblade or battle axe) and press **E** (default) on the prompt. It works under water too. A body you don't touch is gone in about 10 minutes, sooner if everyone leaves.

| Animal | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Fish** | Fillet, sometimes scraps |
| **Stingray** | Fillets, sometimes scraps |
| **Dolphin** | Fillets, fish oil, sometimes scraps |
| **Shark** | Raw shark meat, shark skin, shark fin, sometimes bones or fish oil |
| **Whale** | Raw whale meat, whale hide, whale fin, a lot of fish oil, sometimes bones |

## What it's good for

| From | Make | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 shark meat | 2 cooked fish | Lit campfire or hobo stove |
| 1 whale meat | 3 cooked game | Lit campfire or hobo stove |
| 1 whale fin, 1 water, 1 salt | Whale Fin Soup | Lit campfire or hobo stove |
| 1 shark skin | 3 cloth | Workbench |
| 1 whale hide | 5 cloth | Workbench |

Two of them give you a boost as well as food:

| Food | Boost |
| --- | --- |
| **Shark Fin** (eat it as it is) | For **5 minutes** you barely run out of breath sprinting or swimming. |
| **Whale Fin Soup** | For **10 minutes** your melee hits are **25% stronger**. |

Eating another one starts the timer again. Logging out ends it.

## Tips

* **Stay on the boat over deep water** unless you have to get wet.
* **Diving for crates?** Watch the edges of what you can see. See [Diving](diving.md).
* **A shark fin before a long swim** is the cheapest life jacket there is.
* Want fish without teeth? Try a rod: [Fishing](fishing.md).
