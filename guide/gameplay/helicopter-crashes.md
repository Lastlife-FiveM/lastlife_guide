# Helicopter Crashes

Every now and then a military helicopter loses its engine and comes down somewhere out in the open. If you're close you'll see it smoke, spin and hit the ground. Everyone else gets a message: *A military helicopter just went down. Look for the smoke.*

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The crash is real.** Its explosions can hurt you. If you see it falling towards you, run.
{% endhint %}

## Finding it

A red circle shows on your map, **180 metres** around. It's only roughly where it is, so don't trust the middle. Look for the **column of smoke** and follow it. The fire burns for about **10 minutes**, and after that you'll have to search.

They come down on open ground in the hills, the desert, the forests and the coast, well away from safe zones.

## The crate

There's one **Military Crate** near the wreck. You can find it by its beeping when you're close.

| Smoke on the crate | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 🔴 **Red** | Too hot to touch. It stays that way for **90 seconds** after the crash. |
| 🟢 **Green** | Look at it and press **E** (default) to **Search crate**. |

Inside: the same kind of thing as the red zones. Rifles, heavy pistols, shotguns, attachments, ammo and weapon parts, with a small chance of a sniper or its gear. Everybody shares the one crate, and it's gone once someone empties it.

## What's waiting for you

* **The noise.** Every zombie within about **300 metres** hears the crash, and they keep coming for **5 minutes**.
* **Nests.** From about a minute in, up to three **zombie nests** can take root around the wreck. They keep sending zombies until you **burn them** with Molotovs. Nests drop loot of their own. See [Zones & PvP](zones.md).
* **Other survivors.** The whole server saw the message.

The site is cleared **30 minutes** after the crash: wreck, crate, nests and all.

## Tips

* **Bring Molotovs.** The nests won't stop on their own.
* **Arrive quietly.** Park short and walk the last stretch.
* **Be first or be patient.** Early means the fire and the dead. Late means somebody else has been.
* It's still open world, so the [fighting rules](zones.md) apply.
