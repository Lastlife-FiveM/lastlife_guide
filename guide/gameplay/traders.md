# Traders

Not everyone out here wants to kill you. A few survivors have set up shop in the safe zones. They'll take what you've scavenged, pay you in **chits**, and sell or swap you something useful.

## How to trade

Open your map and look under **Traders** to find them. Walk up to one, look at them and press **E** on **Trade**. The screen has three tabs:

* **Buy:** pay chits for what they have today.
* **Sell:** hand items over, get chits back. What they want is highlighted in your pockets and bag.
* **Barter:** swap items for items.

There's no reputation with these traders. Everyone gets the same offers at the same price. Better goods simply cost more: uncommon goods cost 1.25 times their base price, rare 1.5 times and epic twice.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Only your pockets and backpack count,** and that goes for chits too. Traders never touch your hands or gear slots, so your weapon and armour are safe.
{% endhint %}

## New offers every restart

Traders don't want the same things forever. Every server restart, each one picks a fresh **6 to 10** offers (fewer if they deal in less), and every price moves.

| | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 📈 📉 **Prices** | Anywhere from **half** to **double** the usual. The screen shows how far above or below it is. |
| 🔄 **Offers** | Something they dealt in yesterday might be gone today. |
| 📦 **Stock** | Limited and **shared with every survivor**. The screen shows what's left. Once it's gone, it's gone until the next restart. |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Make room first.** If you can't carry what you're getting, the trade won't go through. You won't lose anything: a failed trade hands everything back.
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Shop around.** The same item can fetch a very different price from one day to the next.
* **Come early.** Stock is shared, so the first survivors in get the best of it.
* **Clean your loot first.** Items still marked as exposed from the [subway](subway.md) can't be traded.
* **Walking away closes the screen.** Stay close until you're done.
