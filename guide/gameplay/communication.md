# Chat, Radio & Voice

The grid went down with everything else. There are no phones any more. If you want to talk to anyone past shouting distance, you need a radio.

## Voice

| Default key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Your push-to-talk key | Talk to people near you. Set it in your game's voice settings. |
| **`** (the key under Esc) | Switch between **whisper**, **normal** and **shout**. Your HUD shows which. |
| **Left Alt** | Talk on the radio, once you're on a channel. |

## The radio

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Get one

Craft it after the **Radio Repair** research (150 chits, 2 copper): 2 aluminum, 4 copper, 4 plastic. Or swap electronics and cables for one with **Margo** at the Observatory, when she has the offer.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put it in the Radio slot

Open your inventory and drag it into the **Radio** slot in your gear. Voice works from your pockets too, but **text chat needs it in the slot**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pick a channel

**Use** the radio, or press **=** (default), and join a channel from **11** to **500**. Everyone on the same channel hears you while you hold **Left Alt**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

* **.** and **,** (default) step one channel up or down.
* Your HUD shows your channel and lights up while you transmit.
* Channels 1 to 10 and 420 to 422 are locked.
* Dying takes you off the channel, and so does losing your last radio.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Pick your channel with your crew before you split up.** Channels aren't private: anyone who finds yours can listen.
{% endhint %}

## Text chat

Text chat runs over the radio, like a pager. **No radio in the slot, no chat.** You can still type commands, but your messages won't go out and you won't see anyone else's.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **T** | Open chat |
| **Tab** | Next channel (or finish a command you're typing) |
| **↑** / **↓** | Go through what you sent before |
| **Esc** | Close chat |
| **L** (default) | Switch how chat shows: **When active**, **Show** or **Hide**. Your choice is saved. |

| Channel | Who hears it |
| --- | --- |
| **Global** | Everyone with a radio. In character. |
| **Crew** | Only your [crew](crews.md). Your tag shows in your crew colour. |
| **OOC** | Out of character talk. Shows your player ID. |
| **Announcements** | Server news. Read only. |

New messages on your channel pop up for a moment. Other channels show a count on their button. A message can be up to 200 characters.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Announcements reach everyone**, radio or not, even with chat hidden.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**No signal underground.** In the [subway](subway.md), text chat cuts out. Commands and radio voice still work.
{% endhint %}

## The Fruit Echo

A music player from before. Only you hear it.

* **Get one** from **Old Toller** on Elysian Island (usually around 120 chits), or find one in a [house](house-robberies.md).
* **Use** it to open it. Tap **Add** and paste a **YouTube** link, or a link that ends in mp3, ogg, wav or m4a.
* It holds **50 songs** and **10 playlists**, with shuffle and repeat.
* Music keeps playing when you put it away. A small plate on screen shows what's on.
* **The songs live on that Echo.** Drop it, trade it or get looted, and they go with it. Loot someone else's and you get their taste in music.

## Tips

* **Your radio is your lifeline.** Keep a spare.
* **Use Crew chat for plans.** Global is read by everyone, including the people you're about to rob.
* **Headphones in means you don't hear what's behind you.** Pause the music outside the walls.
