# Exotics

Some weapons never came out of a factory. Survivors made these.

## Scrap blades

Three one-handed melee weapons, made at any workbench with **no research**. They always come out **Hoarded** (see [Loot & Rarity](loot-rarity.md)).

| Blade | What it is | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mower Machete** | A lawnmower blade wrapped in tape | 4 steel, 3 metal scrap, 2 cloth, 1 cables |
| **Rebar Tomahawk** | A rebar shaft with a saw-plate head | 4 iron, 3 steel, 3 cloth, 2 crude hinges |
| **Hubcap Hatchet** | Half a hubcap on a broken pipe. **Chops trees** like a hatchet. | 1 body panel, 5 metal scrap, 1 duct tape, 2 crude hinges |

They wear like any other weapon. See [Weapons](weapons.md) for repairs.

## Coil Tether

A grappling hook launcher with **120 metres** of line. It can't be crafted or bought. It's a very rare find in **red zones**.

Aim at a wall, a roof edge or a ceiling and fire. The winch pulls you in, and you climb onto ledges by yourself.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **W / S** | Up and down the wall or rope |
| **E** | Climb the ledge |
| **Space** | Let go, or jump off the wall |
| **X** | Drop |

The reticle shows the distance and what you're aiming at. The nearest it hooks is 3 metres. Let go and you get a few seconds without fall damage. It doesn't grab vehicles.
