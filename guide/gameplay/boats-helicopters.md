# Boats & Helicopters

## Boats

Wrecked boats float at a few spots off the coast and on the **Alamo Sea** after each restart. Get out to one, look at it and press **E** to restore it.

| Kind | Always needs |
| --- | --- |
| Boat | 12V battery, marine engine |
| Jetski | 6V battery, marine engine |

Bigger boats take more vehicle parts and body panels. Marine engines need the **Marine Engines** research (6 iron, 6 aluminum, 3 copper, 3 rubber, 3 plastic, 1 electronics, 1 gasoline).

Store boats at a **dock**. See [Vehicles & Garages](vehicles.md).

### The anchor

Boats drift. An anchor stops that, and you want one for [fishing](fishing.md) and [diving](diving.md).

* **Craft it:** 8 iron, 2 chain, 2 metal fittings at a workbench, no research. The boat trader on Elysian Island can have one too.
* **Drop it:** driver's seat, nearly still (under 2 km/h). Press **E** on **Drop Anchor**, or use it from your inventory. 5 seconds.
* **Raise it:** press **E** on **Raise Anchor** as the driver. 5 seconds, and it goes back in your pockets, so leave room.

While it's down, the boat won't drive off. It stays with the boat, so anyone in the driver's seat can raise it.

## Helicopters

Helicopters that fly are military machines, and they only turn up at the most dangerous places. Restoring one always takes a **24V battery** and **aviation parts**, and armed ones also need gun mounts and vehicle armor. Bring a crew to carry it all.

* **Aviation Parts:** 4 steel, 2 metal alloy, 2 plastic composite, 8 aluminum, 4 copper, 2 glass, 3 plastic, 2 cables, 3 electronics. No research of their own.
* Land on a **helipad** to park it.

### Rappelling

Some helicopters, like the **Annihilator**, have a **Rappel Bay**.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Make a Rappel Rope

10 cloth and 1 metal fittings at a workbench, no research.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Stow it

Sit in the helicopter, open your inventory and put the rope in the **Rappel Bay** under the glovebox (4 slots).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Go down

In a **back seat**, at least 5 metres up, press **E** on **Rappel**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**The rope isn't used up.** One is enough for everyone in the back.
{% endhint %}
