# Mechanic

Anyone can fix and tune a car out here. The **Mechanic** [class](classes.md) just does it better. There are no shops: you make the parts at a workbench and fit them wherever the car stands.

## The tools

| Item | What it's for | Cost (workbench, no research) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mechanic Tools** | Check and repair a vehicle | 4 iron, 6 metal scrap, 1 rubber, 2 plastic |
| **Toolbox** | See and remove performance parts | 6 iron, 8 metal scrap, 1 metal fittings, 2 plastic |
| **Mechanic Sheet** | Keeps a list of the work you planned | 2 plastic, 1 plank |
| **Duct Tape** | Limp-home fix, up to 45% engine and body | Found |

Use them from your inventory next to the vehicle. Tools wear out with use.

## Repairs

A car is more than an engine and a body. Worn-out oil, axles, spark plugs, battery and fuel tank cause trouble of their own.

| Part | What a repair uses |
| --- | --- |
| Engine | Vehicle parts |
| Body | Body panel |
| Oil | Engine oil |
| Axle | Axle parts |
| Spark plugs | Spark plugs |
| Battery | 12V battery |
| Fuel tank | Steel |
| Tyres | One tyre for each broken wheel |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Not a Mechanic?** You can bring engine and body up to **60%**. Everything else goes to 100%. A **Repair Kit** on the engine works for anyone.
{% endhint %}

## The Mechanic class

Tiers come with your [rank](ranks.md).

| Tier | Rank | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grease Monkey** | From the start | Repairs to 100%, work 30% faster, make and fit level 3 parts |
| **Jury Rig** | 5 | No waiting: repairs and fitting are instant |
| **Spare Parts** | 10 | Half the repair materials (tyres excepted), tools never wear out, level 4 parts |
| **Master Mechanic** | 15 | Level 5 parts and the top extras |

## Performance parts

| Part | Levels |
| --- | --- |
| Engine, suspension | 1 to 5 |
| Transmission | 1 to 4 |
| Brakes | 1 to 3 |
| Oil pump, drive shaft, cylinder head, battery cables, fuel tank | 1 to 3 |

| Level | Who can make and fit it |
| --- | --- |
| **1 and 2** | Everyone, with **Mechanics** research. Level 2 also turns up in red zones. |
| **3** | Any Mechanic |
| **4** | Spare Parts |
| **5** | Master Mechanic |

Anyone may **remove** a part, whatever its level.

### Extras

* **Everyone** (Mechanics research): manual gearbox, drift tyres, stancer kit.
* **Master Mechanic only:** turbo, car armour, bulletproof tyres, anti-lag and nitrous. If a crewmate is one, let them fit it for you.

### Nitrous keys

| Default key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Left Shift** (hold) | Use the nitrous |
| **Left Ctrl** | Switch nitrous mode |
| **Page Up / Page Down** | Level up or down |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Don't lean on it.** Boosting wears the engine, there's a 7 second cooldown, and holding level 3 too long blows the tank.
{% endhint %}

## Styling

Paint, liveries, rims, bumpers, hoods, spoilers, exhausts, seats, horns, tints, headlights, underglow and more. Cheap to make, no research, free for everyone to fit.

## The car lift

A base piece that raises a vehicle so you can work under it.

* **Cost:** 40 stone, 6 mortar, 40 iron, 6 steel, 6 metal fittings, 2 engine parts.
* **Unlock:** Stonework.
* Park on the platform, get everyone out, look at the posts and press **E** to **Raise lift** or **Lower lift**. It takes about 4 seconds.
* You and your crew can use it, nobody else.
