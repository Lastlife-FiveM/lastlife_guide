# Diving

No dead walk the seabed, and plenty went down with the old world: crates that fell off ships, off piers, off trucks that never made the bridge. Bring air. You won't be completely alone, though: see [Sea Life](sea-life.md).

## The gear

Three pieces, three slots. Each does one job, and you can dive with any mix of them.

| Gear | Slot | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scuba Tank** | Back | **30 minutes** of air |
| **Dive Mask** | Mask | Clear sight under water. Without one it's dark and murky |
| **Flippers** | Shoes | Swim **40%** faster. On land you run **20%** slower |

{% hint style="warning" %}
The tank takes your **Back** slot, so it's the tank **or** a backpack. The dive mask shares its slot with the **gas mask**. Pack for the trip you're making.
{% endhint %}

## Air

A tank only loses air while your **head is under water**. Floating at the surface costs nothing.

* Your **O2 bar** shows the tank while you're in the water.
* Hover the tank in your inventory to see how much air is left.
* You get a warning at **2 minutes** and another at **30 seconds**. Take the first one seriously.
* When the tank is **empty** you're holding your breath like anyone else: 15 seconds, and up to 60 more with the **Lung capacity** [skill](skills.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
An empty tank at the bottom is how divers die. Turn back while you still have air for the swim up.
{% endhint %}

## Refilling

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="At your base" %}
Build an **Air Compressor** and place it like any other base piece. Walk up with a tank that needs air and **hold E** on **Refill tank**. An empty tank takes 25 seconds, a half-full one half that. It costs nothing.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="On Elysian Island" %}
**Rook** fills a tank for **10 chits**. Same prompt, on him. An empty tank takes 8 seconds.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

The tank on your back is filled first. If that one is full, the emptiest tank you carry is filled instead.

## Sunken crates

There are crates on the seabed all around the coast. They are **not** on your map, and they don't stay put.

When you find one, swim down and **hold E** for 3 seconds on **Open crate**. What's inside is the kind of thing you'd otherwise risk a red zone for.

* A crate can be opened **once**. After that it stays **2 minutes** so you can take the rest, then it's gone.
* Something new can wash into the same stretch of water about **90 minutes** later. Never in the exact same place.

## Sonar

Searching the whole coast by eye will take you a lifetime. A sonar narrows it down.

| | Reach | Search circle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sonar** | 1,500 m | 150 m |
| **Advanced Sonar** | 3,500 m | 60 m |

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Use it from the surface

A boat, the shore, or floating with your head above water. It does **not** work while you're under. A ping takes 4 seconds.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Read the map

If something is in range, a **search circle** appears on your map. The crate is somewhere inside it, never dead centre, so don't just dive on the middle.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Dive the circle

The circle lasts **5 minutes**, and it disappears the moment **anyone** opens that crate. If it vanishes while you're still swimming down, someone beat you to it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**"Nothing in range"** means exactly that. Move along the coast and try again: the sonar is ready after 10 seconds.
{% endhint %}

Your **crew** sees your search circle too. One of you pings from the boat, the other is already in the water.

## Getting the gear

| Research | Cost | Needs first | Gives you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Diving** | 300 chits, 8 rubber, 4 steel | Angling | Tank, mask, flippers, sonar |
| **Deep Diving** | 600 chits, 8 electronics, 2 precision parts | Diving | Advanced sonar, air compressor |

* **Buy it.** Rook sometimes has a tank, a mask, flippers or a sonar. His stock changes every restart.
* **Find it.** A mask or a pair of flippers turns up now and then where people kept their weekend gear.

## Tips

* A boat is the best dive platform: ping from the deck, stop over the circle, come back up to a dry seat.
* Go down with a full tank. You can't top it up at the bottom.
* The mask matters more than you'd think. A crate is hard to spot in murky water even when you're almost on top of it.
* Take the flippers off before a fight on land.
