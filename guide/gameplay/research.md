# Research & Blueprints

You know how to make the basics. Everything better has to be worked out first. That's research: you pay **chits** and materials, wait, and your whole base learns new recipes.

## How research works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Build a Crafting Table

Make it at a safe-zone bench and place it in your base. It becomes your **workbench**, and only the workbench has the **Research** tab. See [Crafting](crafting-research.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pick a node

Click a node to see what it costs and unlocks. Some nodes need another one first. Lines join nodes in the same row. If the one it needs is in another row, it says so under the name.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pay and start

Hold to start. The cost is taken straight away from **whoever starts it**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait

The clock keeps running while you're away, and even while you're offline.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Craft it

When it's done, the recipes unlock on their own. There is nothing to pick up.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Research belongs to the base

* **Everyone who uses a workbench at that base shares its research.** If one person pays, the whole crew gets it.
* **Your crew's tables share one tree.** In a crew, all tables placed by crew members count as the same base. Alone, it's all the tables you placed.
* **The smelter counts too.** Research Smelting and Steel unlocks at your base's smelters.
* **One node at a time per base.** You can't queue up several.
* **It's done once and kept for good.** Losing or moving a table doesn't lose the research.
* **Crews and solo tables are separate.** If you leave a crew, its research stays with the crew.
* **Public safe-zone benches have no research.** They always make the same basics.

{% hint style="success" %}
**A crew saves a fortune.** The whole tree costs **6,835 chits**, and it only has to be paid once per base. The waiting adds up to just under 3 hours.
{% endhint %}

## Blueprints

A **blueprint** is a real item. Put one in a workbench and it unlocks its recipes at **that one table**, for everyone who uses it.

* Open the **Blueprints** tab at the bench and drag it in. Drag it out to take it back.
* It only counts while it's in there. Anyone who can open the bench can take it.
* If a blueprint covers the same thing as a research node, the node counts as done at that table, and you can start the nodes after it.
* If the table is destroyed, its blueprints fall on the ground for whoever gets there first.

**Research doesn't give you a blueprint item.** Blueprints come from people who sell them: shady contacts who need to trust you first. They unlock things the tree doesn't have, like the stations for [The Underground](the-underground.md) and [Moonshine](moonshine.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Guard your bench.** Research can't be stolen. A blueprint can.
{% endhint %}

## What needs no research

* Most melee weapons, basic pistols and the Double Barrel Shotgun
* Weapon parts, the basic Suppressor, Tactical Flashlight and Extended Pistol Clip
* Scrap 9mm, the weakest way to make ammo
* Nearly all medical items and all cooking
* Pickaxe, Hatchet, Metal Detector and other hand tools
* Wood building pieces
* Everything at the weapons table (repair kits included) and the recycler, once you have one
* Car styling parts
* Level 3 and higher car parts, but only for the [Mechanic class](classes.md)

## Getting chits

* Search zombies. Now and then one has a few. Bosses carry more.
* Clear nests.
* Sell to [traders](traders.md).
* Sweep sand with a metal detector.
* Fish, and sell the catch. See [Fishing](fishing.md).

## A good order to start with

1. **Smelting** (40 chits, 1 min). Steel is needed for a lot later.
2. **Weapon Upkeep** (30 chits, 1 min) so your gear doesn't die on you.
3. **Reloading** (80 chits, 2 min). It also opens Sidearms and Plate Carriers.
4. **Stonework** (120 chits, 4 min) before someone tests your wooden walls.
5. **Mechanics** (90 chits, 3 min) for lockpicks, repair kits and car parts.
6. **Plate Carriers** and **Packs**, then **Metalwork** and up the gun line.

You don't have to do it tier by tier. Packs and Comforts are drawn far to the right but need nothing first and are cheap, so take them early.
