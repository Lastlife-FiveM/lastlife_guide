# Ammo

To load, hold the gun and press **R**, or use the ammo from your inventory.

## Making it

At a workbench with **Reloading** research (80 chits, 2 copper, 2 minutes).

| Ammo | Gives | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **9mm, scrap** | 6 | 5 metal scrap (no research, also at the safe-zone bench) |
| **9mm** | 12 | 1 lead, 1 brass, 1 gunpowder |
| **.45** | 10 | 1 lead, 1 brass, 1 gunpowder |
| **.22** | 15 | 1 lead, 1 brass, 1 gunpowder |
| **.38** | 10 | 1 lead, 1 brass, 1 gunpowder |
| **.44** | 8 | 2 lead, 1 brass, 2 gunpowder |
| **12 Gauge** | 6 | 1 lead, 1 brass, 1 plastic, 1 gunpowder |

Scrap rounds and reloaded rounds are the same 9mm once they're in your bag.

## Finding the rest

Rifle, sniper and .50 rounds can't be crafted. Look in **ammo cases**, red zones, [airdrops](airdrops.md), [helicopter crashes](helicopter-crashes.md) and [the subway](subway.md). Launcher ammo is subway only.

## Armor piercing rounds

Now and then a weapons airdrop or a helicopter crash holds **armor piercing 5.56**. It fits the Mk II rifles that fire 5.56: **Carbine MK2, Special Carbine MK2 and Bullpup MK2**.

* Hold the gun and **use** the special ammo to switch. What was loaded goes back to your inventory.
* **R** reloads the type that's already in the gun. Use normal ammo to switch back.
* Special rounds need the **normal magazine**. Take off any extended or drum mag first.
* Your HUD shows which type is loaded.

## Ammo pouches

Worn in your Pouch slot, they give you extra room that only takes ammo.

| Pouch | Ammo slots | Cost | Research |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Small** | 1 | 3 cloth, 1 hide | Pouches |
| **Medium** | 2 | 4 cloth, 2 hide, 1 duct tape | Expedition Packs |
