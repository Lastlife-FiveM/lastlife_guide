# HUD, Map & GPS

Your HUD tells you if you're about to starve, bleed out or run dry. The map tells you where the safe ground is. Learn to read both at a glance.

## What the HUD shows

### Your bars

| Bar | What it means | Turns red at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HP** | Your health | 30% |
| **ARM** | Armour, from the plates in your carrier. See [Inventory & Gear](inventory.md) | never |
| **FOOD** | Hunger | 25% |
| **H2O** | Thirst | 25% |
| **STA** | Stamina. Drops while you sprint | 15% |
| **INF** | Infection, from bad air in the subway. High is bad | 50% and up |
| **O2** | Air. Only under water | 30% |

A bar in trouble turns red and pulses.

### Everything else

* **Compass:** which way you're facing, plus the street and area.
* **Status box:** your ID photo, name, ID, class, voice range, cash and radio channel.
* **Weapon:** name, picture and ammo. The spare count only counts the kind of ammo you have loaded, and a tag shows special ammo. The clip goes red at 5 rounds.
* **Vehicle:** speed, gear, revs, fuel, engine, body, seatbelt, lights, lock, and mileage on vehicles you own. Aircraft show altitude and heading. Armed vehicles show their rounds.
* **Driving key hints:** your own keys along the bottom of the screen.
* **Pinned recipes:** up to 3 recipes you pinned, with what you still need.
* **Companion card:** your dog's health and hunger while it's out.
* **Subway card:** your filter and your run, inside the tunnels.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Buckle up.** **B** is your seatbelt by default. Drive without it and the HUD chimes until you do. **Y** is cruise control.
{% endhint %}

## Vehicle controls

Press **F6** in a vehicle for the controls panel: seats, engine, lights, hazards, indicators, doors, windows, hood and trunk. Some of it is for the driver only.

## The minimap needs a GPS

**The minimap only shows when a GPS sits in the GPS slot** of your inventory. In your pockets it does nothing.

Craft one at the Observatory bench or a workbench: 2 electronics, 2 plastic, 1 lithium.

What the minimap shows:

* **You:** the arrow.
* **Places** near you, with the same icons as the big map.
* **Zones:** safe zones, red zones and nests, as glowing areas.
* **Your route** to your waypoint.
* **Your crew**, as dots in your crew colour.
* **Noise:** a faint red circle shows how far zombies can hear you.

It zooms out the faster you go, and much further when you fly. Hold **Z** to look further out.

## The pause menu

Press **Esc** or **P**. The game keeps running behind it, so don't read the map in the middle of a horde.

| Page | What's on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | The full map |
| **Keybinds** | Every key you can change. See [Controls](controls.md) |
| **Settings** | Graphics, audio and controls, plus the menu sound volume |
| **HUD** | The HUD editor and the crosshair creator |
| **Crew** | Your [crew](crews.md) |
| **Survivor** | Your [skills](skills.md) and your [class](classes.md) |
| **Companion** | Your [dog](companions.md) |

## The big map

The pause menu map works without a GPS.

* **Drag** to move, **scroll** to zoom. The target button brings you back to yourself.
* **Double-click** to set a waypoint. **Right-click** to clear it.
* **Hover** over an icon or zone to see its name.

### The legend

Click a row to hide or show it, on the big map and the minimap both. The main groups:

| Group | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| 🏰 **Safe places** | Safe zones and traders |
| ☠️ **Danger** | Red zones, nests, boss zombies, the subway |
| 🛒 **Shops and supplies** | Clothing, barber, tattoos, workbenches |
| 🚗 **Vehicles** | Fuel, garages and places to find vehicles |
| 🧱 **Base building** | Areas where you can't build |
| 🚨 **Activity** | Things that come and go |

A group only shows when there's something in it.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Green is safe. Red is not.** Know which one you're walking into.
{% endhint %}

### Map settings

Open **Map settings** on the map to change zone edges, icon size, the route line, brightness, the size of your arrow and more. Your choices are saved.

## Make the HUD yours

Go to **Esc > HUD > Open HUD editor**.

* **Pick a layout:**

| Layout | Style |
| --- | --- |
| **Field tape** | The default. Flat strips, compass on top |
| **Rings** | A round map wrapped in health and armour |
| **Survival watch** | A rugged device with a heartbeat trace |
| **Ghost** | Almost nothing. A reading shows when it's low or changes |
| **Skewed** | Panels tilted like a helmet visor |
| **Street** | Small pills in the corners, a round gauge when driving |

* **Move and resize** anything by dragging. Each reading can show always, only when low, or never.
* **General:** mph or km/h, how dark the backing is, how the map turns and tilts, the seatbelt chime, update speed and more.
* **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Lost something?** Type `/resethud` and everything goes back to the default.
{% endhint %}

### Crosshair

Under **Esc > HUD > Crosshair** you can build your own. Pick a preset, change every part of it, set one per weapon type or draw your own. You can save up to **50**, send one to a player standing close, or share a code. A code lasts **30 days**. Your crosshair only shows while you aim.

## Tips

* **Get a GPS early.** Running without a map is how people end up in a nest.
* **Watch the noise circle.** If it's big, everything nearby knows where you are.
* **Plan before you leave.** Set your waypoint in the safe zone.
* **Hide what you don't need.** A clean map is easier to read.
