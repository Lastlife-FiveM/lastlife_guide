# House Robberies

People left in a hurry, and they left things behind. Houses all over the map can be broken into: the city, the hills, the desert and the coast. Whoever lived there may still be home.

## Getting in

Walk up to a front door. If it's one you can rob, prompts appear for whatever you can do right now. The options depend on what you're carrying.

| Way in | Needs | How it works | Noise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pick the lock** | Lockpick | Press **Space** or **E** when the needle is in the green, 4 times. A miss costs you a pin and might snap the pick. Too many misses and the lock holds. | Quiet |
| **Pry the door** | Crowbar in your inventory | Hold **Space** or **E** to push, and keep the force in the green. Push too hard and it slips with a bang. The crowbar never breaks. | Loud |
| **Kick the door in** | Nothing | Just wait 3.5 seconds. | Very loud |

**Backspace** gives up. Only one person can work a door at a time.

Once it's open, the door stays open for **2 minutes**. Anyone can **Walk in** during that time: your crew, or a stranger.

## Inside

It's **dark**, so bring a flashlight. Everyone who goes in is in the same house together.

Walk up to cupboards, fridges, drawers, wardrobes, desks and the like:

| Option | Time | Noise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Search** | 6 seconds | Low |
| **Ransack** | Under 3 seconds | Three times as loud |

**X** or **Backspace** stops a search. What you find goes straight into your pockets. If it doesn't all fit, the rest stays there: come back and **Take the rest**. Some things, like a small TV or a toaster, you just pick up.

Each spot can only be searched once per robbery.

## Noise

The **noise meter** fills up as you work and drains slowly when you're quiet.

| What you do | Adds |
| --- | --- |
| Picked the lock | 10 |
| Pried the door | 30 |
| Kicked the door in | 60 |
| A search | 6 |
| A ransack | 18 |
| A gunshot | 25 (a quarter of that with a suppressor) |

It drains by **2 every second**. At **100**, *something heard you* and a horde comes in through the door. That can happen up to **3 times** in one robbery, at least a minute apart. A loud way in also draws the zombies outside.

## What kind of house

| House | Zombies inside | Loot | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shack** | 1 to 2 | A little | 2 hours |
| **Suburban house** | 2 to 3 | Better | 3 hours |
| **Rich house** | 3 to 5, often something nastier | The best, jewellery included | 4 hours |

Some houses are empty, but don't count on it.

Find food in the kitchen, medicine in the bathroom, clothes and bags in the wardrobe, electronics on the desk and tools in the toolbox.

## Leaving

Use the prompt at the door you came in by: **Leave the house**. When nobody has been inside for **30 seconds**, the robbery is over and the house goes on its cooldown. The door then says **Cleaned out** and how long is left, for everyone.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A house is still open world. Robbing another survivor in there needs roleplay. See [Zones & PvP](zones.md).
{% endhint %}

## Tips

* **Lockpick in, search slowly, let the meter drain.** It's the safest way to strip a house.
* **Use melee inside.** Four shots in a row can fill the meter by themselves.
* **Post someone at the door.** Those 2 minutes work for strangers too.
