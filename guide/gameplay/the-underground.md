# The Underground

The world ended and people still want to get high. Weed and coca grow wild. Somewhere on the map a man called **Moss** buys what you make and sells what you can't find.

| Product | Starts with | Stations | Moss buys it from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🌿 **Weed** | Wild seeds | Grow frame, drying rack, packing table | **Nobody** |
| ❄️ **Coke** | Wild coca seeds | Grow frame, cauldron, drying cabinet, packing table | **Known** |
| 💎 **Meth** | Scavenged ingredients and Moss's kit | Cook table, chiller, packing table | **Trusted** |
| 🧱 **Bricks** | Raw product and brick wrap | Brick press | **Connected** |

## Finding Moss

**Every restart he sets up somewhere new**, out of the way.

* **Rumour notes** tell you where. You find them on zombies and in rubbish. Use one to read it: it carries one to three hints about **this restart's** spot. Some notes are about someone else entirely: see [Moonshine & Brewing](moonshine.md).
* A note from an earlier restart has **gone cold**.
* Moss sells notes with all three hints. Handy for your friends.

Walk up to him and press **E** on **Trade**.

## Reputation

Selling to Moss earns reputation, and better stuff earns more. Buying earns **1 point per 20 chits**. It stays with your character.

| Rank | Points | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Known** | 200 | Selling coke, the Coca Works |
| **Trusted** | 600 | Selling meth, reagent kits, the Cook's Lab |
| **Connected** | 1,500 | Brick wrap, selling bricks |
| **Made** | 4,000 | **+15%** on everything you sell him |

| **Grower's Kit**: grow frame, planter, small grow light, drying rack, packing table | 150 chits | Nobody |

The first three are **blueprints**. Put one in your **base workbench's blueprint slot** and that bench can build those stations, for everyone who uses it. Take it out and the bench forgets, but placed stations stay.

## Weed

### Wild plants

Weed grows in patches around the map, in **12 strains**, each with its own part of the world. **Ditchweed** grows by roadsides. **Red Zone Kush** grows only in red zones and pays best. The rest you'll have to find. Seeds are never sold.

Carry **trimming shears** and press **E** on a plant for **1 to 3 seeds**. A patch grows back about **30 minutes** after the first plant is picked, and everyone shares it.

### The gear

| Item | What for | Where from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Garden Shovel** | Use it on dirt or grass to dig a bag of **potting soil** (5 seconds, move 3 metres between digs) | Craft, garage shelves |
| **Watering Can** | Fill it at any water or a water well | Craft, garage shelves |
| **Trimming Shears** | Picking and harvesting | Craft, garage shelves |
| **Compost Fertilizer** | Feeding plants | Craft, sometimes Brother Amos |
| **Empty Baggies** | Packing | Craft, rubbish, Moss |
| **Rolling Papers** | Joints | Zombies, Moss |

### Growing

Every job is a short **hands-on scene** done with the mouse. Place a **grow frame** at your base, install a **planter**, pour in soil, plant a seed, water it, spray fertilizer.

* A plant takes about **2 hours**. The frame needs no power, but a **small light** grows it **twice** as fast and a **large** one **four times**, as long as the frame is on and has [base power](power.md).
* Keep the water in the **green**, the top of the gauge. One spray fills half the fertilizer gauge.
* With either one empty it stops growing. After about **2 hours** like that it withers.
* Good care raises quality. Nothing grows or dies while the server is off.

### Harvest, dry, pack

* **Harvest:** click each bud with your shears. **6 to 12** wet buds, often a seed or two back.
* **Dry:** hang them on the rack, 3 to a hook, 8 hooks. **20 minutes** gives **+5 quality**. Taking them down early costs **10**.
* **Pack:** a **baggie** takes 2 dried buds and an empty baggie. A **joint** takes 1 bud and 1 paper. Up to 10 in one go.

## Coke

Wild coca grows in a few small patches, in wet, hidden places, and takes about an hour to come back. Pick it like weed for **1 or 2 seeds**.

| Step | Station | In | Out | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grow | Grow frame and planter | 1 seed | 10 to 16 leaves | About 15 minutes |
| Cook | Cauldron | 12 leaves, 1 gasoline | 4 wet paste | 10 minutes |
| Dry | Drying cabinet (base power) | 4 wet paste | 4 raw cocaine | 8 minutes |
| Pack | Packing table | 1 raw, 1 baggie | 1 coke baggie | |

* **Cauldron:** stop the pour with the gauge in the green, then close the lid when the mix is in the green. Worth up to **10 quality** either way.
* **Drying cabinet:** 6 trays. Spread the paste and let go in the green, worth up to **10 quality** either way. A dry tray forgotten for **30 minutes** loses **20%** of its quality.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Fumes draw zombies.** A cooking cauldron calls everything within about **40 metres**, a meth cook about **60**. Don't cook where you sleep, or guard it.
{% endhint %}

## Meth

| Ingredient | Per cook | Where from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cold Pills** | 2 | Zombies, household rubbish |
| **Acetone** | 1 | Garage shelves |
| **Chem Reagent Kit** | 1 | **Moss**, once you're Trusted |

At the **cook table**, add the containers in the order the scene asks, then play the heat round: **hold Space** for heat, let go to cool, keep the needle in the green for **20 seconds**. Time in the green is your quality. Too little and the batch is lost. One cook makes **one tray**.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**It can blow up.** Off the green, the danger meter fills, much faster when it's too hot. Past halfway it can explode at any second, and over **300** degrees it goes at once. **The blast kills the cook**, and the bang draws zombies from far away.
{% endhint %}

The **chiller** (base power, switched on) sets a tray in **5 minutes** and holds 12. Click a ready one and break it with a **hammer** from your pockets: 6 hits, **6 shards**. Pack 1 shard and 1 baggie into a **meth baggie**.

## The mixer: your own strains

The **mixer** (base power) turns plain product into a strain of your own. Put in one base (dried buds, raw cocaine or shards) and **one to three** add-ins:

Baking Soda, Sprunk, Painkillers, Antidote, Lithium, Salt, Sulfur, Gunpowder, Fish Oil, Blueberries, Cold Pills, Adrenaline Shot, Coffee, Whiskey, Vodka, Beer.

* **2 base + 1 of each add-in makes 3** (4 with baking soda). More add-ins, lower quality.
* Each one changes how it feels, and some make you **see things that aren't there**. Find out for yourself.
* The **first person** ever to mix a recipe **names the strain**. What's in it is never written anywhere.
* The **Strains** button shows what's been found and how many are still out there. A mixed strain can't be mixed again.
* Moss pays more for good strains and sometimes asks around for one in particular.

## The brick press

| Brick | Takes (plus 1 brick wrap) |
| --- | --- |
| Weed | 20 dried buds of one strain |
| Coke | 10 raw cocaine |
| Meth | 10 meth shards |

Line the mould with wrap, fill it, pull the lever, take the brick. Moss pays a brick like **10 baggies plus 15%**.

## Selling

Moss pays chits. The price is what it is, times quality, times **demand**. Every sale drops demand for that one thing, and it slowly recovers. Each strain has its own, so selling a mix keeps prices up.

| At top quality and normal demand | Chits |
| --- | --- |
| Weed baggie | 19 (Ditchweed) to 48 (Red Zone Kush) |
| Joint | About a third of its baggie |
| Coke baggie | 60 |
| Meth baggie | 90 |

## Using it

Takes 3 seconds, moving cancels it. These are for quality 50: Pristine is stronger and longer, Poor weaker and shorter.

| Drug | Lasts | What it does | Afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🌿 **Joint** | 5 min | 25% less recoil, slowly heals a quarter of your health, twice as hungry, 5% slower | Nothing |
| ❄️ **Coke** | 2 min | 15% faster, endless stamina | 3 min crash: sprinting drains you fast, slight blur |
| 💎 **Meth** | 4 min | Half damage taken, 50% harder melee, three times as thirsty | 5 min comedown: shaky aim |

Going down or dying ends every effect.

* **Overdose:** another dose of coke or meth while one is working risks collapsing. **10%** for the second, **15%** more for each after that, up to 90%.
* **Addiction:** **4** doses of coke or meth within a day and you're hooked. About **20 minutes** after your last one, withdrawal starts: shaky aim and slow stamina. It fades **24 hours** after the last dose, or take **detox meds** to end it at once. Craft them, or ask June at the Observatory.

Weed does neither.

## Tips

* **Start with weed.** It's all Moss buys from strangers.
* **Cook away from home**, or with someone on the door.
* **Read your notes fresh.** They're only good until the next restart.
