# Welcome to LastLife

![](.gitbook/assets/banner.png)

The city fell. The dead walk. What's left of Los Santos belongs to whoever is willing to fight for it.

This guide teaches you how to stay alive: where it's safe, how to gather and craft, how to build a base, and who to trade with.
It won't tell you everything. Some things are better found out the hard way.

{% hint style="info" %}
**We're in Closed Beta.** Things change often. If something in this guide doesn't match the game, let us know in Discord.
{% endhint %}

## Where to start

* **New here?** Read [Getting Started](gameplay/getting-started.md), then [Zones & PvP](gameplay/zones.md). The second one has the server's rules on fighting.
* **Need materials?** [Gathering](gameplay/gathering.md) and [Crafting & Research](gameplay/crafting-research.md).
* **Ready to settle down?** [Base Building](gameplay/base-building.md).
* **Feeling brave?** [The Subway](gameplay/subway.md).
* Got a question that isn't answered here? Ask in our Discord.
